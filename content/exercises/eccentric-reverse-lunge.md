# Eccentric Reverse Lunge

A beginner bodyweight lunge-pattern exercise targeting the quadriceps. Eccentric Reverse Lunge develops single-leg strength and stability that transfers to walking, stairs, and sport.

## Details

- **Category:** Lunge
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** lunge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings

## Instructions

1. Stand tall, then step one foot straight back into a reverse lunge.
2. Lower over a slow 3-4 count until both knees approach 90 degrees.
3. Drive through the front heel to return to standing — the way up is normal speed.
4. Alternate legs. The slow lowering builds single-leg strength and control.

## Cues

- Torso tall over hips
- Front knee over ankle
- Push through the front heel

## Longevity notes

Single-leg strength is balance insurance — the ability to catch yourself on one leg prevents falls. Train lunges for control, not maximal load.
