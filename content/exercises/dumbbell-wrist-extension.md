# Dumbbell Wrist Extension

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Forearms

## Overview

A beginner dumbbell pull-pattern exercise for the forearms. Dumbbell Wrist Extension strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Rest your forearm on a bench or thigh, wrist hanging off the edge, palm down, holding a light dumbbell.
2. Extend the wrist upward, lifting the dumbbell.
3. Pause briefly at the top, then lower slowly.
4. Keep the forearm still — only the wrist moves. Use light weight and higher reps.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
