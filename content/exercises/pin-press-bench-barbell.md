# Pin Press Bench (Barbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Overview

A bench press from pins that starts each rep from a dead stop, removing the stretch reflex.

## Instructions

1. Set the pins in a rack at your chosen height and lie on the bench under the bar.
2. Grip the bar and retract your shoulder blades.
3. Press the bar up from the pins to full extension.
4. Lower the bar back to the pins and let it settle.
5. Reset your tension before the next rep.

## Cues

- Dead stop each rep
- Shoulder blades pinned
- Drive through the feet

## Common mistakes

- Bouncing off the pins
- Losing tightness
- Lifting the hips

## Safety

Use a spotter and appropriate safeties; start light.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
