# EZ Bar Curl

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Forearms

## Overview

A beginner barbell pull-pattern exercise for the biceps. EZ Bar Curl strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Stand holding an EZ bar with an underhand grip, arms extended.
2. Curl the bar to shoulder height, keeping the elbows tucked.
3. Squeeze the biceps at the top.
4. Lower with control. The angled bar is easier on the wrists than a straight bar.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
