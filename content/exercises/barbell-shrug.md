# Barbell Shrug

Barbell Shrug is a beginner pulling exercise for the upper back, performed with a barbell. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Forearms

## Instructions

1. Stand tall holding a barbell at arm's length, grip just outside the hips.
2. Shrug your shoulders straight up toward your ears — don't roll them.
3. Squeeze hard at the top for a full second.
4. Lower slowly to a dead hang. A power-shrug variant may use slight leg drive; keep the back flat.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
