# Elevated Pigeon Stretch

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Abductors

## Overview

Elevated Pigeon Stretch is a beginner mobility exercise for the glutes, performed with just your bodyweight. Regular mobility work keeps joints moving freely and makes strength training safer.

## Instructions

1. Place your shin on a bench or box, knee bent about 90 degrees.
2. Square your hips toward the surface as best you can.
3. Hinge forward gently over the front leg until you feel a deep glute stretch.
4. Keep the spine long and hold 30-60 seconds per side, breathing into the stretch.

## Cues

- Move slowly, no forcing
- Breathe into the stretch
- Stay relaxed outside the target area

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
