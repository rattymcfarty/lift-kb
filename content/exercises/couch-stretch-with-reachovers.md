# Couch Stretch with Reachovers

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

A deep hip-flexor and quad stretch with an overhead reach that adds a side stretch through the torso.

## Instructions

1. Kneel facing away from a couch or bench and place one foot up against it behind you.
2. Step the other foot forward into a lunge position, torso upright.
3. Squeeze your back glute and tuck your pelvis slightly.
4. Reach the arm on the back-leg side overhead and lean gently away.
5. Hold 20 to 30 seconds, then switch sides.

## Cues

- Squeeze the back glute
- Ribs down
- Breathe slowly

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back
- Forcing the stretch
- Letting the front knee cave in

## Safety

Pad your back knee; ease off if you feel pinching in the knee or low back.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
