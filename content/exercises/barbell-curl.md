# Bicep Curl (Barbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Forearms

## Overview

Bicep Curl (Barbell) is a beginner pulling exercise for the biceps, performed with a barbell. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Instructions

1. Stand holding a barbell with an underhand grip, arms extended.
2. Curl the bar to shoulder height, keeping the elbows pinned to your sides.
3. Squeeze the biceps hard at the top.
4. Lower slowly. Don't swing the torso — strict form builds bigger arms.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
