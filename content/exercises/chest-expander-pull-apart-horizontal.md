# Chest Expander Pull-Apart (Horizontal)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back, Rear Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Lats

## Overview

A chest-expander (spring or band) pull-apart in front of the body that trains the rear delts and upper back.

## Instructions

1. Hold the chest expander at chest height with arms straight in front of you.
2. Pull the handles apart by moving your hands out to the sides.
3. Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the end of the movement.
4. Pause for one second.
5. Return slowly to the start.

## Cues

- Arms long
- Squeeze shoulder blades
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging
- Arching the lower back
- Letting the handles snap back

## Safety

Check the expander for wear; use a resistance you can control.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
