# Band Clamshell

Band Clamshell is a beginner lower-body exercise that builds the glutes, usually performed with minimal equipment. It trains the deep knee-and-hip bend you use every time you sit, stand, or climb stairs.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Abductors

## Instructions

1. Lie on your side with a mini band above your knees, hips stacked, knees bent about 45 degrees.
2. Keep your feet together and rotate the top knee open against the band.
3. Pause at the top without rolling the hips backward.
4. Lower slowly with control. Complete all reps, then switch sides.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
