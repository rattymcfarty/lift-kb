# Pendlay Row

A strict barbell row where each rep starts from a dead stop on the floor.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back, Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet hip-width apart and the bar over your mid-foot, then hinge until your torso is nearly parallel to the floor.
2. Grip the bar with an overhand grip just outside your legs.
3. Pull the bar explosively to your lower chest.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Lower the bar back to the floor and reset between reps.

## Cues

- Torso parallel to the floor
- Pull to the lower chest
- Reset each rep

## Common mistakes

- Standing up during the pull
- Rounding the back
- Using the hips to heave the bar

## Safety

Use a flat back and a manageable load; stop if your back rounds.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
