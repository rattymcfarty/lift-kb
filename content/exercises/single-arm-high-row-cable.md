# Single Arm High Row (Cable)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back, Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

A single-arm cable row from a high pulley.

## Instructions

1. Set a cable pulley at about head height and hold the handle with one hand.
2. Step back to create tension.
3. Pull the handle toward your shoulder, leading with your elbow.
4. Squeeze your upper back.
5. Return slowly.

## Cues

- Lead with the elbow
- Squeeze the upper back
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the torso
- Shrugging
- Using momentum

## Safety

Use a moderate load.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
