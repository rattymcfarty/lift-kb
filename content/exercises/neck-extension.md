# Neck Extension

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Neck

## Overview

A gentle neck-extension exercise for the muscles at the back of the neck.

## Instructions

1. Sit tall and place your hands behind your head for light resistance, or lie face down with your head off a bench.
2. Keep your shoulders relaxed.
3. Slowly lift your head back against the resistance.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Return to neutral under control.

## Cues

- Very light resistance
- Slow and smooth
- Shoulders relaxed

## Common mistakes

- Using too much load
- Jerking
- Craning the head too far back

## Safety

Use very light resistance; stop with dizziness, tingling or pain.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
