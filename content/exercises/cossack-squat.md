# Cossack Squat

An intermediate bodyweight squat-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Cossack Squat develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Adductors, Glutes

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet wide, toes pointing slightly out.
2. Shift your weight to one side, squatting deep on that leg while the other leg extends straight.
3. Keep the straight leg's heel on the floor and torso upright.
4. Push through the bent leg to return to center, then alternate sides.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
