# Scapular Push-Up

Scapular Push-Up is a beginner pushing exercise for the chest, performed with just your bodyweight. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Chest

## Instructions

1. Assume a push-up position with straight arms.
2. Without bending the elbows, let your chest sink between your shoulder blades.
3. Then push the floor away, spreading the shoulder blades apart and rounding the upper back.
4. Move slowly through the protraction/retraction. Trains the serratus anterior.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
