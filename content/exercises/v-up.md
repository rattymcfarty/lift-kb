# V-Up

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A V-shaped core exercise where hands and feet meet at the top.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back with arms and legs extended.
2. Lift your legs and torso at the same time.
3. Reach your hands toward your feet.
4. Pause at the top.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Reach for your toes
- Slow lowering
- Legs straight

## Common mistakes

- Swinging
- Bending the knees
- Dropping fast

## Safety

Bend your knees to make it easier.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
