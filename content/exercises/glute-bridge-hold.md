# Glute Bridge Hold

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings

## Overview

A beginner bodyweight hinge-pattern exercise for the glutes. Glute Bridge Hold builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat and hip-width apart.
2. Lift your hips into a full glute bridge — knees, hips, shoulders in line.
3. Hold the top position, squeezing the glutes, for the prescribed time.
4. Breathe steadily; don't let the hips sag. An isometric glute strengthener.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
