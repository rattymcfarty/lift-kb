# Prisoner Good Morning

Prisoner Good Morning is a beginner hip-hinge exercise targeting the hamstrings, performed with just your bodyweight. The hinge is the foundation of safe lifting — it teaches you to load the hips, not the spine.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Lower Back

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet hip-width apart, hands laced behind your head (prisoner position).
2. Hinge at the hips, pushing them back with a soft knee bend and flat back.
3. Lower until you feel a strong hamstring stretch, torso near parallel.
4. Drive the hips forward to stand, squeezing the glutes.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
