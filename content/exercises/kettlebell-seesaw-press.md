# Kettlebell Seesaw Press

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

Kettlebell Seesaw Press is an intermediate pushing exercise for the front delts, performed with a kettlebell. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Instructions

1. Clean two kettlebells two your shoulders.
2. Press one kettlebell.
3. Lower the kettlebell and immediately press the other kettlebell. Make sure to do the same amount of reps on both sides.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
