# Incline Chest Fly (Dumbbell)

A dumbbell fly on an incline bench that stretches the upper chest.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Lie on an incline bench set to 30 degrees holding dumbbells over your upper chest, palms facing in, slight bend in the elbows.
2. Open your arms out to the sides in a wide arc until you feel a stretch.
3. Keep your elbows slightly bent throughout.
4. Squeeze your chest to bring the dumbbells back together.
5. Keep your shoulder blades pinned back.

## Cues

- Slight elbow bend
- Open wide
- Squeeze together

## Common mistakes

- Pressing instead of flying
- Dropping too low
- Using too heavy a weight

## Safety

Use light-to-moderate weights; stop at the stretch.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
