# Deficit Handstand Push Up

A handstand push-up from a raised surface that increases the range of motion for the shoulders and triceps.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Middle Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Rear Delts, Triceps

## Instructions

1. Stand with your hands on two sturdy blocks or plates and kick up into a handstand against a wall.
2. Brace your core and keep your body in a straight line.
3. Lower your head slowly down between your hands until it is below hand level.
4. Press back up powerfully to full lockout.
5. Come down from the handstand carefully.

## Cues

- Straight line body
- Controlled descent
- Press through the whole hand

## Common mistakes

- Flaring the elbows
- Arching the lower back
- Dropping too fast

## Safety

Advanced; master regular handstand push-ups first, use a safe landing area, and avoid if you have neck or wrist issues.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
