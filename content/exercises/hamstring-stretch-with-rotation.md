# Hamstring Stretch with Rotation

A standing or seated hamstring stretch with a thoracic rotation that adds mobility through the trunk.

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Instructions

1. Stand with one heel forward on the floor, toes lifted, and hinge forward at the hips with a flat back.
2. Reach the opposite hand toward your front foot.
3. Rotate your torso and reach the other arm up toward the ceiling.
4. Hold for a few seconds, then return and repeat for 6 to 8 reps.
5. Switch sides.

## Cues

- Flat back
- Slow rotation
- Breathe out as you rotate

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the back
- Bouncing
- Locking the knee hard

## Safety

Move gently into a mild stretch; avoid if you have acute hamstring or back strain.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
