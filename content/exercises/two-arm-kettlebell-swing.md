# Two-Arm Kettlebell Swing

Two-Arm Kettlebell Swing is an intermediate hip-hinge exercise targeting the glutes and hamstrings, performed with a kettlebell. The hinge is the foundation of safe lifting — it teaches you to load the hips, not the spine.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Lower Back

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet wider than shoulder-width, kettlebell on the floor in front of you.
2. Hinge back and grip the bell with both hands, then hike it between your legs.
3. Drive your hips forward explosively, letting the bell swing to chest height — arms are just hooks.
4. Let the bell fall back down, hinging to absorb it, and repeat rhythmically.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
