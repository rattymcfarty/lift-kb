# Seated Wide-Grip Row (Cable)

A seated cable row with a wide grip that emphasises the upper back.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back, Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Instructions

1. Sit at the cable row with a wide-grip bar and your feet on the platform.
2. Sit tall with arms extended.
3. Pull the bar to your upper abdomen, flaring your elbows.
4. Squeeze your shoulder blades.
5. Return slowly.

## Cues

- Chest tall
- Elbows wide
- Squeeze shoulder blades

## Common mistakes

- Leaning back
- Shrugging
- Rounding the back

## Safety

Keep a neutral spine.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
