# Narrow Push-Up

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Overview

Narrow Push-Up is a beginner pushing exercise for the triceps, performed with just your bodyweight. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Instructions

1. Assume a push-up position with hands directly under your shoulders, close together.
2. Lower your chest toward your hands, keeping elbows tucked to your sides.
3. Descend until your chest nearly touches the floor.
4. Press back up to straight arms. The narrow grip shifts emphasis to the triceps.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
