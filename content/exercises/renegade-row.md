# Renegade Row

A plank-position row with dumbbells that trains the upper back and core.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back, Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Get into a high plank with hands on dumbbells and feet wide.
2. Brace your core and keep your hips level.
3. Row one dumbbell up to your ribs.
4. Lower it and row the other side.
5. Avoid rotating your hips.

## Cues

- Hips level
- Brace the core
- Row to the ribs

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the hips
- Using momentum
- Using too heavy dumbbells

## Safety

Use hex dumbbells for stability; widen your feet for balance.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
