# Pinwheel Curl

A beginner dumbbell pull-pattern exercise for the biceps. Pinwheel Curl strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Forearms

## Instructions

1. Hold dumbbells with a neutral (hammer) grip at your sides.
2. Curl one dumbbell across your body toward the opposite shoulder.
3. Alternate arms in a continuous pinwheel motion.
4. Keep the elbows pinned to your sides. Hits the brachialis and forearms.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
