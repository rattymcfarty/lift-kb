# Sissy Squat (Weighted)

Sissy Squat (Weighted) is an advanced lower-body exercise that builds the quadriceps, usually performed with just your bodyweight. It trains the deep knee-and-hip bend you use every time you sit, stand, or climb stairs.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps

## Instructions

1. Hold a plate at your chest, feet hip-width apart, heels elevated on a wedge.
2. Lean back, bending only at the knees, lowering your body.
3. Descend until your knees are well forward of your toes.
4. Drive back up through the quads. Despite the name, it's brutally hard on the quads.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
