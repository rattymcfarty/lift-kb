# Runner's Lunge with Thoracic Extension

A beginner just your bodyweight mobility drill targeting the glutes. Runner's Lunge with Thoracic Extension restores range of motion that desk life and hard training both tend to steal.

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps

## Instructions

1. Step into a deep runner's lunge, back knee resting on the floor.
2. Place both hands on the front thigh.
3. Lift the chest and gently arch the mid-back, looking slightly upward.
4. Feel the hip-flexor stretch and hold 20-30 seconds per side, breathing deeply.

## Cues

- Move slowly, no forcing
- Breathe into the stretch
- Stay relaxed outside the target area

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
