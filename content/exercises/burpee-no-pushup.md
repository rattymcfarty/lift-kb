# Burpee (No Push-Up)

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Full-Body
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest, Quadriceps

## Overview

Burpee (No Push-Up) is an intermediate core exercise targeting the full-body, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. From standing, drop into a squat and place your hands on the floor.
2. Jump your feet back into a plank position.
3. Jump your feet back to your hands and stand up explosively.
4. Add a small jump at the top for the full cardio effect. No push-up in this variant.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
