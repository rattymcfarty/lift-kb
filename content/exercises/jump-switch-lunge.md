# Jump Switch Lunge

A plyometric lunge where you jump and switch legs in the air to train power and conditioning.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Calves

## Instructions

1. Start in a lunge position with one foot forward.
2. Jump up explosively.
3. Switch legs in the air.
4. Land softly in a lunge with the opposite foot forward.
5. Immediately repeat.

## Cues

- Land softly
- Chest tall
- Drive up through the front foot

## Common mistakes

- Landing with a stiff knee
- Letting the knee cave in
- Leaning forward

## Safety

Skip if you have knee problems; use an alternating step lunge as a regression.
