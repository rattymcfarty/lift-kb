# KAS Glute Bridge

A barbell glute bridge in the KAS (Kinetic Alignment Standard) style that keeps constant tension on the glutes with a partial range.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Lower Back

## Instructions

1. Sit on the floor with your back against a bench and a barbell over your hips with padding.
2. Place your feet flat, knees bent.
3. Drive through your heels and lift your hips until your torso is parallel to the floor.
4. Squeeze your glutes without overarching.
5. Lower only partway so tension stays on the glutes, then repeat.

## Cues

- Squeeze the glutes
- Ribs down
- Hold constant tension

## Common mistakes

- Overarching the lower back
- Pushing through the toes
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Pad the bar well; start light and avoid if you have back pain.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
