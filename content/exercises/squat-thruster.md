# Squat Thruster

A front squat that flows into an overhead press for full-body conditioning.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts, Triceps, Abs

## Instructions

1. Hold dumbbells at your shoulders and stand with feet shoulder-width.
2. Squat down with your chest tall.
3. Drive up explosively.
4. Press the dumbbells overhead as you stand.
5. Lower them back to your shoulders.

## Cues

- Chest tall
- Drive up
- Press at the top

## Common mistakes

- Pressing before standing
- Rounding the back
- Using too heavy dumbbells

## Safety

Use light-to-moderate weights.

## Longevity notes

Squatting is the foundation of getting up, sitting down, and climbing stairs. Keeping leg strength and knee range preserves independence decades longer than any single other movement.
