# Single Arm Farmer Carry

## Details

- **Category:** Carry
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** carry
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Abs, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Forearms, Hamstrings

## Overview

A single-arm loaded carry that trains grip and the core's anti-lean strength.

## Instructions

1. Pick up a dumbbell in one hand with a hinge.
2. Stand tall with your shoulders level.
3. Walk with short steps without leaning toward the weight.
4. Keep your core braced.
5. Switch sides.

## Cues

- Stand tall
- Shoulders level
- Short steps

## Common mistakes

- Leaning
- Hiking the shoulder
- Dropping the weight

## Safety

Use a weight you can carry upright.

## Longevity notes

Carrying is the most functional strength there is — groceries, luggage, a grandchild. Loaded carries build grip, posture, and core stability in one movement.
