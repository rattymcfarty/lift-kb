# Bodyweight Skull Crusher

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

Bodyweight Skull Crusher is an intermediate pushing exercise for the triceps, performed with just your bodyweight. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Instructions

1. Set up like the top of a push-up, hands on the floor or an elevated surface.
2. Keeping the elbows tucked and upper arms still, lower your forehead toward the surface by bending only the elbows.
3. Extend the elbows to press back up.
4. The lower the surface and the closer the hands, the harder the rep.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
