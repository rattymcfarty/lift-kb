# Preacher Curl (EZ Bar)

Preacher Curl (EZ Bar) is a beginner pulling exercise for the biceps, performed with a barbell. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps

## Instructions

1. Sit at a preacher bench with your upper arms resting on the pad.
2. Grip the EZ bar with an underhand grip, arms extended.
3. Curl the bar up until your biceps are fully contracted.
4. Lower slowly to full extension. The pad isolates the biceps completely.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
