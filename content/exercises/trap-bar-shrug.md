# Trap Bar Shrug

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Forearms

## Overview

A beginner minimal-equipment pull-pattern exercise for the upper back. Trap Bar Shrug strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Stand inside a trap bar and grip the handles, arms straight.
2. Shrug your shoulders straight up toward your ears — don't roll them.
3. Squeeze hard at the top for a second.
4. Lower slowly to a full stretch. The neutral grip is easy on the shoulders.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
