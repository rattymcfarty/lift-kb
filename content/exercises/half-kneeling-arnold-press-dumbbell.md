# Half Kneeling Arnold Press (Dumbbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Middle Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Rear Delts, Triceps, Abs

## Overview

An Arnold press performed half-kneeling to challenge shoulder strength while the core and hips stabilise.

## Instructions

1. Half-kneel with one knee down and the other foot forward, holding a dumbbell in each hand at chin level with palms facing you.
2. Brace your glutes and abs so your torso stays tall.
3. Press the dumbbells overhead while rotating your palms to face forward.
4. Lock out at the top without arching your back.
5. Lower, rotating your palms back toward you.

## Cues

- Tall posture
- Glute squeezed on the down-knee side
- Smooth rotation

## Common mistakes

- Leaning back to press
- Flaring the ribs
- Rotating too fast

## Safety

Use a lighter weight than seated; stop if the shoulders feel pinched.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
