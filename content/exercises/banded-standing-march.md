# Banded Standing March

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes

## Overview

Banded Standing March is a beginner core exercise targeting the abs, performed with minimal equipment. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. Loop a band around your feet or hold it underfoot, standing tall.
2. Brace your core and march in place, driving one knee to hip height against the band.
3. Keep the torso tall — don't lean back as the knee rises.
4. Alternate legs with control for reps or time.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
