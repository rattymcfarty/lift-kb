# Single Leg Romanian Deadlift

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps, Lower Back, Abs

## Overview

A single-leg hip hinge that trains the hamstrings, glutes and balance.

## Instructions

1. Stand on one leg holding a dumbbell in the opposite hand, knee slightly bent.
2. Hinge at the hip, sending your free leg back behind you.
3. Lower until your torso is near parallel with the floor.
4. Keep your back flat.
5. Squeeze your glute to return to standing.

## Cues

- Hips back
- Flat back
- Squeeze the glute

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the back
- Twisting the hips
- Bending the knee too much

## Safety

Use a light weight or touch a wall for balance.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
