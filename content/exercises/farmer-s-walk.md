# Farmer's Walk

An intermediate loaded carry with minimal equipment for the forearms. Farmer's Walk builds the grip, core, and postural strength used every time you haul groceries or luggage.

## Details

- **Category:** Carry
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** carry
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Forearms
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs, Glutes, Hamstrings, Lower Back

## Instructions

1. There are various implements that can be used for the farmers walk. These can also be performed with heavy dumbbells or short bars if these implements aren't available. Begin by standing between the implements.
2. After gripping the handles, lift them up by driving through your heels, keeping your back straight and your head up.
3. Walk taking short, quick steps, and don't forget to breathe. Move for a given distance, typically 50-100 feet, as fast as possible.

## Cues

- Tall posture, ribs down
- Grip hard, breathe behind the brace
- Short, quick steps

## Longevity notes

Grip strength and loaded walking correlate strongly with longevity. Carries are simple, safe, and brutally effective — do them often.
