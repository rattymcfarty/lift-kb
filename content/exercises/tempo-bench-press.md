# Tempo Bench Press

An intermediate barbell press-pattern exercise targeting the chest. Tempo Bench Press builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Instructions

1. Unrack a barbell for a standard bench press.
2. Lower over a slow 3-4 count, keeping the bar path consistent.
3. Touch the chest lightly, then press up at normal speed.
4. The slow eccentric increases time under tension and control.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
