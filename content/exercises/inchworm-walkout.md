# Inchworm Walkout

A mobility drill that stretches the hamstrings and challenges the core and shoulders by walking the hands out to a plank.

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Stand tall, hinge forward and place your hands on the floor.
2. Walk your hands forward until you are in a high plank position.
3. Pause and brace your core.
4. Walk your feet toward your hands in small steps.
5. Stand up and repeat.

## Cues

- Small steps
- Core braced
- Smooth rhythm

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag
- Rushing
- Locking the knees too hard

## Safety

Bend your knees as needed; skip with wrist or shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
