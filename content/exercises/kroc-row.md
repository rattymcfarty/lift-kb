# Kroc Row

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Upper Back, Biceps

## Overview

Kroc Row is an advanced pulling exercise for the lats, performed with dumbbells. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Instructions

1. Take a very heavy dumbbell and a staggered stance, one hand braced on a bench.
2. Row the dumbbell hard to your hip with an aggressive, powerful pull.
3. Allow some body English — this is a high-rep strength builder. Aim for 20+ reps per arm.
4. Rest, then match the reps on the other side.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
