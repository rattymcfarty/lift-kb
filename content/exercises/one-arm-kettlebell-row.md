# One-Arm Kettlebell Row

An intermediate kettlebell pull-pattern exercise for the upper back. One-Arm Kettlebell Row strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps, Lats

## Instructions

1. Place a kettlebell in front of your feet.
2. Bend your knees slightly and then push your butt out as much as possible as you bend over to get in the starting position.
3. Grab the kettlebell and pull it to your stomach, retracting your shoulder blade and flexing the elbow.
4. Keep your back straight.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
