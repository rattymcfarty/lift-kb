# Sling Shot Bench Press (Barbell)

A bench press using a sling-shot device that assists the bottom of the lift.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Put the sling shot on your upper arms and lie on a bench.
2. Grip the bar and retract your shoulder blades.
3. Lower the bar to your chest.
4. Press up with the band's assistance.
5. Re-rack.

## Cues

- Shoulder blades pinned
- Control the lowering
- Press through the lockout

## Common mistakes

- Letting the band bounce you
- Flaring the elbows
- Using too much load

## Safety

Use a spotter and appropriate safeties.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
