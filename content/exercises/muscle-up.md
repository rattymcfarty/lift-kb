# Muscle Up

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs, Biceps, Forearms, Upper Back

## Overview

Muscle Up is an intermediate pulling exercise for the lats, performed with minimal equipment. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Instructions

1. Grip the rings using a false grip, with the base of your palms on top of the rings. Initiate a pull up by pulling the elbows down to your side, flexing the elbows.
2. As you reach the top position of the pull-up, pull the rings to your armpits as you roll your shoulders forward, allowing your elbows to move straight back behind you. This puts you into the proper position to continue into the dip portion of the movement.
3. Maintaining control and stability, extend through the elbow to complete the motion.
4. Use care when lowering yourself to the ground.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
