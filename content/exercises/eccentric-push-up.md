# Eccentric Push-Up

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A beginner bodyweight press-pattern exercise targeting the chest. Eccentric Push-Up builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Instructions

1. Start in a high plank, hands under shoulders.
2. Lower yourself as slowly as possible — aim for 3-5 seconds down.
3. Keep the body rigid and elbows at about 45 degrees.
4. Once your chest nears the floor, reset to the top. The slow lowering builds full push-up strength.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
