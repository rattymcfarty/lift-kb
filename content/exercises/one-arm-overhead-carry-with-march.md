# One Arm Overhead Carry with March

## Details

- **Category:** Carry
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** carry
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Middle Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Quadriceps, Adductors

## Overview

A single-arm overhead carry with a marching step that trains shoulder stability and core control.

## Instructions

1. Press a dumbbell overhead in one hand until your arm is locked out.
2. Stand tall with your ribs down and your glutes squeezed.
3. March in place or walk, lifting your knees without leaning.
4. Keep the dumbbell directly over your shoulder.
5. Switch arms after the set.

## Cues

- Ribs down
- Biceps by your ear
- Stand tall

## Common mistakes

- Leaning to the side
- Letting the arm drift forward
- Arching the lower back

## Safety

Use a light weight; stop with shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Carrying is the most functional strength there is — groceries, luggage, a grandchild. Loaded carries build grip, posture, and core stability in one movement.
