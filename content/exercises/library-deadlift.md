# Library Deadlift

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back, Abs

## Overview

A deadlift variant that uses a partial range with the bar lifted from an elevated start to focus on the upper portion of the pull.

## Instructions

1. Set the bar on blocks or in a rack at about knee height.
2. Stand with feet hip-width apart and grip the bar just outside your legs.
3. Hinge to position your shoulders over the bar with a flat back.
4. Drive through your feet and extend your hips and knees to stand.
5. Lower the bar back to the supports under control.

## Cues

- Flat back
- Push the floor away
- Squeeze the glutes

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the back
- Jerking the bar
- Overextending at the top

## Safety

Use a belt if needed and a load you can control; avoid with acute back pain.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
