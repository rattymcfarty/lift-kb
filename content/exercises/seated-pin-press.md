# Seated Pin Press (Barbell)

Seated Pin Press (Barbell) is an advanced pushing exercise for the front delts, performed with a barbell. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Instructions

1. Set a barbell on safety pins at forehead height inside a rack, seated on a bench.
2. Press the bar from the dead stop on the pins to full overhead lockout.
3. Lower back to the pins and pause — every rep starts from zero.
4. Builds explosive pressing power from the sticking point.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
