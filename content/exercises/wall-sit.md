# Wall Sit

A beginner bodyweight squat-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Wall Sit develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Instructions

1. Lean your back flat against a wall, feet shoulder-width apart and about two feet out.
2. Slide down until your thighs are parallel to the floor, knees over ankles.
3. Press your lower back into the wall and hold, breathing steadily.
4. Keep the weight through your heels for the prescribed time.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
