# Back Extension (Weighted)

A loaded back extension that strengthens the spinal erectors, glutes and hamstrings through hip extension.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Lower Back, Glutes

## Instructions

1. Set the pad of a back-extension bench just below your hip crease and hook your heels under the rollers.
2. Hold a plate against your chest with crossed arms and start with your torso hanging down, spine neutral.
3. Raise your torso by squeezing your glutes until your body forms a straight line.
4. Pause briefly without leaning back past neutral.
5. Lower under control to the start.

## Cues

- Hinge at the hips
- Squeeze glutes at the top
- Stop at a straight line

## Common mistakes

- Hyperextending the lower back at the top
- Swinging up with momentum
- Rounding the upper back at the bottom

## Safety

Keep the weight light enough that you can stop at a neutral spine; skip with acute low-back pain.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
