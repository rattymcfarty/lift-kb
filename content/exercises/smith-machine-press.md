# Press (Smith Machine)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A beginner Smith machine press-pattern exercise targeting the front delts. Press (Smith Machine) builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Instructions

1. Set a bench under the Smith bar and position it at shoulder height.
2. Unrack and press the bar overhead to full extension.
3. The fixed path lets you focus on pressing without stabilizing.
4. Lower with control to shoulder height. A stable overhead press option.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
