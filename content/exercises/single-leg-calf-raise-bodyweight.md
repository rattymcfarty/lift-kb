# Single Leg Calf Raise (Bodyweight)

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Calves

## Overview

A single-leg calf raise using your bodyweight for the calf and ankle stabilisers.

## Instructions

1. Stand on one foot holding a wall for balance.
2. Rise onto the ball of your foot.
3. Pause at the top.
4. Lower slowly until your heel is below your toes if on a step.
5. Switch legs.

## Cues

- Rise tall
- Pause at the top
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Bouncing
- Rolling the ankle
- Rushing

## Safety

Hold a support; use a step only if balance is good.

## Longevity notes

Squatting is the foundation of getting up, sitting down, and climbing stairs. Keeping leg strength and knee range preserves independence decades longer than any single other movement.
