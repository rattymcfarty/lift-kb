# Powell Raise

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Middle Delts

## Overview

Powell Raise is an intermediate pulling exercise for the rear delts, performed with dumbbells. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your side on a bench, holding a dumbbell with the top arm.
2. Raise the dumbbell in an arc from hip to overhead.
3. The path combines a lateral raise with external rotation.
4. Lower slowly. A rear-delt and rotator-cuff hybrid.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
