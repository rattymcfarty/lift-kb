# Inch Worm + Push Up

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs, Front Delts

## Overview

A dynamic movement that combines an inchworm walkout with a push-up for mobility and upper-body strength.

## Instructions

1. Stand tall, then hinge forward and place your hands on the floor.
2. Walk your hands forward until you are in a high plank.
3. Perform one push-up with a straight body.
4. Walk your hands back toward your feet.
5. Stand up and repeat.

## Cues

- Straight body in the plank
- Smooth walkout
- Brace your core

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag
- Rushing
- Bending the knees too much

## Safety

Bend your knees as much as needed on the walkout; skip with wrist pain.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
