# Roman Chair Sit-Up

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

An intermediate a machine core exercise for the abs. Roman Chair Sit-Up builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Instructions

1. Hook your feet under the pads of a roman chair and cross your arms at your chest.
2. Lower your torso back until roughly parallel to the floor — only as far as you control.
3. Sit up by contracting the abs; avoid yanking with the hip flexors.
4. Keep it controlled; add load only when bodyweight is easy.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
