# Lying Pullover (Cable)

A lying cable pullover that keeps constant tension on the lats and chest.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Lats

## Instructions

1. Lie on a bench with your head toward a low pulley, holding a bar or rope behind your head.
2. Keep a slight bend in your elbows.
3. Pull the bar in an arc over your face to above your chest.
4. Squeeze your lats at the top.
5. Return slowly to the stretch.

## Cues

- Slight elbow bend
- Pull with the lats
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Bending the elbows
- Arching the back
- Using too heavy a weight

## Safety

Use a moderate weight; stop with shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
