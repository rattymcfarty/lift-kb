# Pull Apart (Cable)

A cable pull-apart that trains the rear delts and upper back with steady tension.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts

## Instructions

1. Set two cable handles at chest height and cross them so your right hand holds the left handle and vice versa.
2. Step back with a slight bend in your elbows.
3. Pull your arms apart until they are in line with your shoulders.
4. Squeeze your shoulder blades together.
5. Return slowly.

## Cues

- Arms long
- Squeeze shoulder blades
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging
- Using the arms to row
- Leaning back

## Safety

Use a light-to-moderate weight; stop with shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
