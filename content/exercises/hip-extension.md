# Hip Extension

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes, Lower Back

## Overview

A floor or bench exercise that extends the hips to train the glutes and lower back.

## Instructions

1. Lie face down on a bench or the floor with hips at the edge if on a bench.
2. Keep your torso stable and brace your core.
3. Raise your straight leg or legs until they are in line with your body.
4. Squeeze your glutes at the top.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Squeeze the glutes
- Don't arch the back
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Overarching the lower back
- Swinging the legs
- Using momentum

## Safety

Stop at a straight line; skip with acute low-back pain.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
