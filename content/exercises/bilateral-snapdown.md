# Bilateral Snapdown

A fast core exercise where you drive a held overhead position down to your hips, training the abs to produce force.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Stand or kneel with arms overhead holding a light weight or a band with both hands.
2. Brace your abs and snap your arms down quickly toward your hips.
3. Finish with a strong ab contraction and ribs pulled down.
4. Return slowly to the overhead position.
5. Keep your lower back from arching at the top.

## Cues

- Snap with the abs
- Ribs down at the bottom
- Slow return overhead

## Common mistakes

- Swinging with the arms only
- Arching the lower back
- Letting the weight yank your shoulders

## Safety

Start light; do not force the overhead position if your shoulders feel tight.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
