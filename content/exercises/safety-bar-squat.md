# Safety Bar Squat

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Safety Bar
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings

## Overview

An intermediate safety-bar squat-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Safety Bar Squat develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Instructions

1. Set the safety squat bar on your upper back, holding the front handles.
2. Unrack and take your squat stance.
3. Descend with control, staying more upright than a straight-bar squat allows.
4. Drive through the whole foot to stand. The cambered bar is gentler on the shoulders.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
