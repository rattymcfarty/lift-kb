# Single Leg Plate Hop

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Adductors

## Overview

A single-leg hop over or onto a plate that builds ankle stiffness and single-leg power.

## Instructions

1. Place a weight plate on the floor and stand on one foot beside it.
2. Hop sideways over the plate and land softly on the same foot.
3. Hop back immediately.
4. Keep your knee aligned over your toes.
5. Switch legs after the set.

## Cues

- Land soft and stick
- Knee over toes
- Quick hops

## Common mistakes

- Landing stiff
- Knee caving in
- Hopping too far too soon

## Safety

Use a flat plate on a non-slip surface; skip with ankle instability.
