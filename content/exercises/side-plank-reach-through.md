# Side Plank with Reach-Through

Side Plank with Reach-Through is an intermediate core exercise targeting the abs, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Forearms

## Instructions

1. Set up in a side plank — elbow under shoulder, body straight, feet stacked.
2. Brace hard and reach the top arm under your torso, rotating the trunk.
3. Open the chest back up, reaching the arm to the ceiling.
4. Keep the hips lifted throughout. Complete all reps, then switch sides.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
