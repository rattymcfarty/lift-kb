# Supine Spinal Twist Stretch

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

Supine Spinal Twist Stretch is a beginner core exercise targeting the other, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back with arms out in a T position.
2. Bend one knee and let it fall across your body to the opposite side.
3. Keep both shoulders on the floor and breathe deeply.
4. Hold 30-60 seconds per side. A classic thoracic and lumbar mobility stretch.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
