# Reverse Nordic Curl

Reverse Nordic Curl is an advanced core exercise targeting the quadriceps, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps

## Instructions

1. Kneel on a pad with a partner holding your ankles or feet hooked under something sturdy.
2. Keeping a straight line from knees to head, lean back as far as you can control.
3. Use the quads to brake the descent — don't just fall.
4. Pull yourself back up. An intense bodyweight quad builder.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
