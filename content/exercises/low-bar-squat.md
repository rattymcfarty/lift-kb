# Squat (Low Bar)

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Lower Back

## Overview

Squat (Low Bar) is an intermediate lower-body exercise that builds the quadriceps, usually performed with a barbell. It trains the deep knee-and-hip bend you use every time you sit, stand, or climb stairs.

## Instructions

1. Rest the barbell low on your rear delts, below the traps.
2. Take a slightly wider stance and sit back more than a high-bar squat.
3. Descend until hip crease is below the knee.
4. Drive up, keeping the chest forward-inclined. Lets most lifters move maximal weight.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
