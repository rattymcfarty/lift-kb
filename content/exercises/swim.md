# Swim

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

Swimming for full-body, low-impact cardiovascular fitness.

## Instructions

1. Warm up with easy laps.
2. Swim at a steady pace using your preferred stroke.
3. Breathe rhythmically.
4. Rest between sets as needed.
5. Cool down with easy laps.

## Cues

- Relaxed breathing
- Long strokes
- Steady pace

## Common mistakes

- Holding your breath
- Swimming too hard too soon
- Poor body position

## Safety

Swim where a lifeguard is present; stop with chest pain.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
