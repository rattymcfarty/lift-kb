# Lying Tricep Extension (Barbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A skull-crusher style barbell extension performed lying on a bench that isolates the triceps.

## Instructions

1. Lie on a bench holding a barbell above your chest with arms extended and a narrow grip.
2. Keep your upper arms still and bend your elbows to lower the bar toward your forehead.
3. Stop when the bar is just above your forehead.
4. Extend your arms back to the start.
5. Keep your elbows pointing at the ceiling.

## Cues

- Elbows stay still
- Control the lowering
- Squeeze the triceps

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbows flare
- Lowering too fast
- Using too heavy a weight

## Safety

Use a spotter or an EZ bar; avoid with elbow pain.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
