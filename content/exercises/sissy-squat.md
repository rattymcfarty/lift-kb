# Sissy Squat

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Abs

## Overview

A bodyweight squat leaning back on the toes to heavily load the quads.

## Instructions

1. Stand holding a support with feet hip-width apart.
2. Rise onto your toes.
3. Bend your knees forward while leaning your torso back.
4. Lower until you feel a strong quad stretch.
5. Return to standing.

## Cues

- Hips forward
- Torso leans back
- Slow control

## Common mistakes

- Going too deep
- Rushing
- Letting the knees cave

## Safety

Advanced for knees; use support and a small range.

## Longevity notes

Squatting is the foundation of getting up, sitting down, and climbing stairs. Keeping leg strength and knee range preserves independence decades longer than any single other movement.
