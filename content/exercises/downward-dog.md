# Downward Dog

Downward Dog is a beginner mobility exercise for the hamstrings, performed with just your bodyweight. Regular mobility work keeps joints moving freely and makes strength training safer.

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Calves, Upper Back

## Instructions

1. From all fours, tuck your toes and lift your hips high into an inverted V.
2. Press your chest toward your thighs and your heels toward the floor.
3. Keep a slight knee bend if needed — a long spine matters more than straight legs.
4. Breathe deeply and hold 30-60 seconds.

## Cues

- Move slowly, no forcing
- Breathe into the stretch
- Stay relaxed outside the target area

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
