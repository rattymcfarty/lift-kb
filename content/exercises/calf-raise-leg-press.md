# Calf Raise (Leg Press)

A calf raise performed on a leg press machine, letting you load the calves heavily with a supported back.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Calves

## Instructions

1. Sit in the leg press with your back flat on the pad and place the balls of your feet on the lower edge of the platform.
2. Extend your legs to unlock the sled, keeping your knees nearly straight but not locked.
3. Press through the balls of your feet to push the platform up as far as possible.
4. Pause, then lower slowly until you feel a calf stretch.
5. Do not let the sled move by bending your knees.

## Cues

- Knees almost straight
- Full stretch, full squeeze
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Bouncing at the bottom
- Bending the knees to move the sled
- Using too much weight and a tiny range

## Safety

Make sure the safety stops are set and re-rack the sled securely when done.

## Longevity notes

Squatting is the foundation of getting up, sitting down, and climbing stairs. Keeping leg strength and knee range preserves independence decades longer than any single other movement.
