# Partial Incline Press (Dumbbell)

A shortened-range incline dumbbell press that focuses on the upper chest without going deep at the shoulders.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Lie on an incline bench holding dumbbells at your shoulders.
2. Press them up to full extension.
3. Lower only partway, about halfway down.
4. Press back up.
5. Keep your shoulder blades pinned back.

## Cues

- Shoulder blades back
- Partial range
- Control the lowering

## Common mistakes

- Dropping too fast
- Flaring the elbows
- Arching the back

## Safety

Use a spotter or safe set-down; use a moderate weight.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
