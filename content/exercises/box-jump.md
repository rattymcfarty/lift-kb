# Box Jump

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Other, Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Overview

A plyometric jump onto a box that builds lower-body power and coordination.

## Instructions

1. Stand facing a sturdy box, feet shoulder-width apart, a comfortable distance away.
2. Swing your arms back and dip into a quarter squat.
3. Explode upward, swinging your arms and jumping onto the box with both feet.
4. Land softly in a squat position with your whole foot on the box.
5. Stand tall, then step back down one foot at a time.

## Cues

- Soft quiet landing
- Whole foot on the box
- Step down, don't jump down

## Common mistakes

- Jumping off the box and landing hard
- Landing with straight, locked knees
- Using a box that is too tall

## Safety

Choose a box height you can land on comfortably; avoid on bad knees or ankles and never jump onto an unstable surface.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
