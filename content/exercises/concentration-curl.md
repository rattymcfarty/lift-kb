# Concentration Curl

A seated single-arm curl that braces the elbow against the thigh to remove swinging and isolate the biceps.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps

## Instructions

1. Sit on a bench with your legs apart, holding a dumbbell in one hand and resting that elbow on your inner thigh.
2. Let the arm hang fully extended.
3. Curl the dumbbell up toward your shoulder, keeping your upper arm still.
4. Squeeze at the top.
5. Lower slowly until the arm is straight.

## Cues

- Upper arm stays still
- Squeeze at the top
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the torso
- Letting the elbow move
- Cutting the range short

## Safety

Use a light weight; stop if you feel elbow or wrist pain.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
