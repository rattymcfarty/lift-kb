# Swiss Ball Plank

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

An intermediate minimal equipment core exercise for the abs. Swiss Ball Plank builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Instructions

1. Place your forearms on a Swiss ball in a plank, body in a straight line.
2. Brace hard — the unstable surface forces constant correction.
3. Keep the hips level; don't sag or pike.
4. Breathe steadily and hold 20-40 seconds.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
