# Lat Prayer

An intermediate cable pull-pattern exercise for the lats. Lat Prayer strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Kneel facing a cable machine with the pulley at the top, holding a rope or straight bar.
2. Hinge at the hips with arms extended overhead in line with your torso.
3. Pull the handle down toward the floor in an arc, arms straight.
4. Squeeze the lats at the bottom, then return slowly.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
