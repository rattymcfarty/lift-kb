# Hip Abductor (Machine)

A seated machine exercise that presses the knees outward to train the glute medius and hip abductors.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Abductors, Glutes

## Instructions

1. Sit in the machine with your back against the pad and your legs against the outer pads.
2. Set a light-to-moderate weight and sit tall.
3. Push your knees apart as far as is comfortable.
4. Pause at the end of the range.
5. Return slowly without letting the weights slam.

## Cues

- Push through the outer knees
- Slow return
- Sit tall

## Common mistakes

- Using momentum
- Leaning forward
- Using too heavy a weight

## Safety

Keep the range within comfort; avoid if you have a hip injury.

## Longevity notes

Squatting is the foundation of getting up, sitting down, and climbing stairs. Keeping leg strength and knee range preserves independence decades longer than any single other movement.
