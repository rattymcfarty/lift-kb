# Leg Curl

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings

## Overview

A machine hamstring curl that isolates the back of the thighs.

## Instructions

1. Set the machine so your knees line up with the pivot and the pad rests just above your heels.
2. Lie or sit as the machine requires and grip the handles.
3. Curl your heels toward your glutes.
4. Squeeze your hamstrings at the end.
5. Return slowly under control.

## Cues

- Squeeze the hamstrings
- Hips stay down
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the hips
- Using momentum
- Letting the weights slam

## Safety

Use a moderate weight; stop if you feel cramping or a sharp pain in the hamstring.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
