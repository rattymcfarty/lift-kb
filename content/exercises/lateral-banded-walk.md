# Lateral Banded Walk

A side-stepping exercise with a band around the legs that trains the glute medius and hip abductors.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes, Abductors

## Instructions

1. Place a resistance band around your legs just above the knees or ankles.
2. Stand with feet hip-width apart, knees slightly bent in a quarter squat.
3. Step sideways, keeping tension on the band.
4. Bring your trailing foot in without letting the feet come fully together.
5. Take steps in one direction, then return.

## Cues

- Stay low
- Keep tension on the band
- Knees out

## Common mistakes

- Letting the knees cave in
- Standing too tall
- Leaning the torso

## Safety

Use a band with comfortable resistance; keep the movement slow.

## Longevity notes

Squatting is the foundation of getting up, sitting down, and climbing stairs. Keeping leg strength and knee range preserves independence decades longer than any single other movement.
