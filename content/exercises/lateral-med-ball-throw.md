# Lateral Medicine Ball Throw

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest, Full-Body

## Overview

Lateral Medicine Ball Throw is an intermediate pushing exercise for the abs, performed with minimal equipment. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Instructions

1. Stand sideways to a wall, holding a medicine ball with both hands.
2. Load by rotating the torso away from the wall.
3. Explosively rotate toward the wall and release the ball at chest height.
4. Follow through with the hips. A rotational power developer.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
