# Glute Bridge (Bodyweight)

A beginner bodyweight hinge-pattern exercise for the glutes. Glute Bridge (Bodyweight) builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back with knees bent, feet flat on the floor hip-width apart.
2. Drive through your heels to lift your hips until knees, hips, and shoulders form a line.
3. Squeeze the glutes hard at the top for 1-2 seconds.
4. Lower with control. Keep the ribs down — don't overarch the lower back.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
