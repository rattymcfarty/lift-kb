# Tempo Deadlift

Tempo Deadlift is an intermediate hip-hinge exercise targeting the hamstrings, performed with a barbell. The hinge is the foundation of safe lifting — it teaches you to load the hips, not the spine.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Lower Back

## Instructions

1. Set up for a conventional deadlift.
2. Pull the bar off the floor over a slow 3-count, staying perfectly positioned.
3. Lock out, then lower over another 3-count.
4. The slow tempo builds positional strength and control.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
