# Snatch Deadlift

Snatch Deadlift is an intermediate hip-hinge exercise targeting the hamstrings, performed with a barbell. The hinge is the foundation of safe lifting — it teaches you to load the hips, not the spine.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Forearms, Glutes, Lower Back

## Instructions

1. The snatch deadlift strengthens the first pull of the snatch. Begin with a wide snatch grip with the barbell placed on the platform. The feet should be directly under the hips, with the feet turned out. Squat down to the bar, keeping the back in absolute extension with the head facing forward.
2. Initiate the movement by driving through the heels, raising the hips. The back angle should remain the same until the bar passes the knees.
3. At that point, drive your hips through the bar as you lay back. Return the bar to the platform by reversing the motion.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
