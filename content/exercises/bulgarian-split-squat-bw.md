# Bulgarian Split Squat (Bodyweight)

An intermediate bodyweight squat-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Bulgarian Split Squat (Bodyweight) develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Instructions

1. Stand a stride in front of a bench, rear foot resting on it laces-down.
2. Lower straight down until the front thigh is parallel to the floor.
3. Keep the torso upright and front knee tracking over the toes.
4. Drive through the front heel to stand. Add dumbbells when bodyweight is easy.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
