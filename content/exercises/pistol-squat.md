# Pistol Squat

Pistol Squat is an advanced lower-body exercise that builds the quadriceps, usually performed with just your bodyweight. It trains the deep knee-and-hip bend you use every time you sit, stand, or climb stairs.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Instructions

1. Stand on one leg with the other extended forward.
2. Squat down on the standing leg, keeping the free leg off the floor.
3. Descend until your hip is below your knee, torso upright.
4. Drive through the heel to stand. The king of bodyweight single-leg strength.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
