# Single-Leg Hip Thrust

An intermediate bodyweight hinge-pattern exercise for the glutes. Single-Leg Hip Thrust builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings

## Instructions

1. Set up for a hip thrust with your upper back on a bench, one foot planted.
2. Extend the other leg straight out as a counterbalance.
3. Drive through the planted heel, lifting the hips to full extension.
4. Lower with control. Unilateral glute strength with high carryover to sprinting.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
