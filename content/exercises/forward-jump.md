# Forward Jump

A beginner bodyweight hinge-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Forward Jump builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Calves

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Dip into a partial squat, swinging your arms back.
3. Jump forward for distance, swinging the arms forward.
4. Land softly with bent knees. A fundamental horizontal power drill.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
