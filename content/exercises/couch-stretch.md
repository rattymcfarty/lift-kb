# Couch Stretch

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

A deep stretch for the hip flexors and quadriceps using a couch or bench to hold the back foot.

## Instructions

1. Kneel facing away from a couch or bench and place the top of one foot against it behind you.
2. Step your other foot forward into a lunge position.
3. Raise your torso upright and tuck your pelvis by squeezing your back glute.
4. Hold the stretch for 20 to 30 seconds.
5. Switch sides.

## Cues

- Squeeze the back glute
- Torso tall
- Breathe

## Common mistakes

- Overarching the low back
- Bouncing
- Letting the front knee collapse

## Safety

Pad the knee; back off if you feel sharp knee or hip pain.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
