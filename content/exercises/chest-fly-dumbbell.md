# Chest Fly (Dumbbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Overview

A dumbbell chest fly on a flat bench that stretches and shortens the chest through an arc.

## Instructions

1. Lie on a flat bench holding a dumbbell in each hand over your chest, palms facing each other, elbows slightly bent.
2. Open your arms out to the sides in a wide arc until you feel a stretch across your chest.
3. Keep a constant slight bend in your elbows.
4. Squeeze your chest to bring the dumbbells back together over your chest.
5. Keep your shoulder blades pinned back and down.

## Cues

- Slightly bent elbows
- Open wide like a hug
- Squeeze chest to close

## Common mistakes

- Turning it into a press by bending the elbows
- Dropping too low and stressing the shoulders
- Using too heavy a weight

## Safety

Use a light-to-moderate weight; stop at the point of a comfortable stretch.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
