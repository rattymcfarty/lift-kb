# Dragon Flag

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

An advanced core exercise where you lower your straight body from a bench using your abs, hips and upper back.

## Instructions

1. Lie on a bench and grip the bench behind your head with both hands.
2. Lift your body into a straight line with only your upper back on the bench.
3. Keep your body rigid and slowly lower it toward the bench without bending at the hips.
4. Stop before your lower back arches.
5. Raise back up by squeezing your abs.

## Cues

- Rigid body
- Slow lowering
- Abs braced

## Common mistakes

- Bending at the hips
- Arching the lower back
- Dropping quickly

## Safety

Advanced exercise; use bent-knee versions first. Avoid if you have neck or lower-back problems.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
