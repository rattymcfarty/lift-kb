# One-Arm Kettlebell Swings

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Calves, Glutes, Lower Back, Front Delts

## Overview

One-Arm Kettlebell Swings is an intermediate hip-hinge exercise targeting the hamstrings, performed with a kettlebell. The hinge is the foundation of safe lifting — it teaches you to load the hips, not the spine.

## Instructions

1. Perform the movement with control through a full range of motion.
2. Repeat for the prescribed number of reps, resting as needed between sets.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
