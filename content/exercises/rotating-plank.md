# Rotating Plank

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs, Front Delts

## Overview

Rotating Plank is an intermediate core exercise targeting the abs, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. Start in a forearm plank.
2. Rotate onto one side into a side plank, arm reaching skyward.
3. Return to center, then rotate to the other side.
4. Keep the hips from sagging throughout. Dynamic core stability.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
