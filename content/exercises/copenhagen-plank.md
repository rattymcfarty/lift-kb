# Copenhagen Plank

An advanced just your bodyweight core exercise for the adductors. Copenhagen Plank builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Adductors
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs, Glutes

## Instructions

1. Lie on your side with your top foot resting on a bench, bottom leg under it.
2. Plant your forearm on the floor and lift your hips into a side plank.
3. Hold a straight line from head to heels, squeezing the top inner thigh down into the bench.
4. Hold for time, then switch sides. An elite adductor and lateral-core strengthener.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
