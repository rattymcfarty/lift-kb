# Nordic Curl

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Abs

## Overview

An advanced hamstring exercise where you lower your torso forward from kneeling under control against your own bodyweight.

## Instructions

1. Kneel on a pad with your ankles anchored under a bar or held by a partner.
2. Keep your body in a straight line from knees to head.
3. Lower your torso forward as slowly as you can using your hamstrings.
4. Catch yourself with your hands at the bottom.
5. Push off the floor lightly and use your hamstrings to return up.

## Cues

- Straight line from knees to head
- Lower as slowly as possible
- Hips extended

## Common mistakes

- Bending at the hips
- Dropping fast
- Using hands too early

## Safety

Advanced; start with band-assisted or short-range versions. Avoid with a hamstring injury.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
