# Run Strides

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

Short controlled accelerations at about 80 to 90 percent of top speed that improve running mechanics.

## Instructions

1. Warm up with an easy jog.
2. Accelerate smoothly over about 20 seconds up to a fast but relaxed pace.
3. Hold a tall posture and relaxed face.
4. Decelerate gradually.
5. Walk back to recover, then repeat several times.

## Cues

- Smooth acceleration
- Relaxed shoulders
- Tall posture

## Common mistakes

- Sprinting all-out
- Overstriding
- Tensing the upper body

## Safety

Warm up first; skip with hamstring or calf tightness.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
