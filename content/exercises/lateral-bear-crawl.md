# Lateral Bear Crawl

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps, Front Delts

## Overview

An intermediate just your bodyweight core exercise for the abs. Lateral Bear Crawl builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Instructions

1. Start on all fours with knees hovering just off the ground, back flat.
2. Step sideways, moving hand and opposite foot together.
3. Keep the hips low and level — don't let them sway.
4. Take small, controlled steps for the prescribed distance, then crawl back.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
