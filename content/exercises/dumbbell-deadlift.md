# Dumbbell Deadlift

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Quadriceps, Lower Back

## Overview

A beginner dumbbell hinge-pattern exercise for the glutes. Dumbbell Deadlift builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet hip-width apart, dumbbells at your sides.
2. Hinge at the hips, lowering the dumbbells along your legs with a flat back.
3. Descend until the dumbbells reach mid-shin or you feel a hamstring stretch.
4. Drive through the heels and squeeze the glutes to stand.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
