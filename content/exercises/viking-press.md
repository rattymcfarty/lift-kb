# Viking Press

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

An intermediate machine press-pattern exercise targeting the front delts. Viking Press builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Instructions

1. Stand in the Viking press machine, gripping the handles at shoulder height.
2. Press the handles up and slightly forward to full extension.
3. The converging path feels natural on the shoulders.
4. Lower with control. A strongman-style overhead press.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
