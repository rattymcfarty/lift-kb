# Snatch (Kettlebell)

## Details

- **Category:** Olympic
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Full-Body
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps, Front Delts

## Overview

An advanced kettlebell hinge-pattern exercise for the full-body. Snatch (Kettlebell) builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Instructions

1. Swing a kettlebell between your legs, then explosively extend the hips.
2. Punch your hand through as the bell travels overhead in one motion.
3. Catch it locked out with a straight arm — don't bang the wrist.
4. Lower to the swing and repeat. A full-body power developer.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
