# Stair Climber

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

A stair-climbing machine for lower-body cardio.

## Instructions

1. Step onto the machine and hold the rails lightly.
2. Climb at a steady pace.
3. Place your whole foot on each step.
4. Stand tall.
5. Keep a steady breathing rhythm.

## Cues

- Stand tall
- Whole foot on the step
- Light hands

## Common mistakes

- Leaning on the rails
- Hunching
- Using tiny steps

## Safety

Hold the rails for balance; start slowly.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
