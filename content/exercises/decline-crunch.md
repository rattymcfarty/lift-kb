# Decline Crunch

A crunch on a decline bench that increases the range and resistance on the abs.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Secure your feet on a decline bench and lie back with arms crossed on your chest.
2. Curl your torso up, bringing your ribs toward your pelvis.
3. Pause for a moment at the top.
4. Lower slowly until your shoulder blades are near the bench.
5. Keep your neck relaxed throughout.

## Cues

- Ribs to hips
- Slow lowering
- Neck relaxed

## Common mistakes

- Yanking the head forward
- Swinging with momentum
- Lowering too fast

## Safety

Use a gentler incline if you have low-back sensitivity.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
