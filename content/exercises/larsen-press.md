# Larsen Press

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell, Smith Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Front Delts

## Overview

Larsen Press is an intermediate pushing exercise for the chest, performed with a barbell. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Instructions

1. Set up for a bench press but lift your feet off the floor — legs extended or crossed in the air.
2. Unrack and lower the bar to your chest with control.
3. Press back up, bracing your core hard to stay stable without leg drive.
4. The missing leg drive forces pure upper-body pressing strength.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
