# Band Hip Abduction

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Abductors
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Overview

A beginner minimal-equipment squat-pattern exercise for the abductors. Band Hip Abduction develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Instructions

1. Place a mini band just above your knees and stand with feet hip-width apart, knees slightly bent.
2. Keeping your torso still, press one knee outward against the band.
3. Pause briefly at full abduction.
4. Return slowly without letting the knees collapse inward. Complete all reps, then switch sides.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
