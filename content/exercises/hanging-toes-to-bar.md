# Hanging Toes To Bar

An advanced hanging core exercise where you lift your straight legs until your feet touch the bar.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Hang from a pull-up bar with an overhand grip.
2. Pull your shoulders down and brace your core.
3. Raise your legs, keeping them as straight as you can, until your toes touch the bar.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Lower slowly under control without swinging.

## Cues

- Pull the bar down
- Legs straight
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Kipping out of control
- Bending the knees to cheat the range
- Losing grip and dropping

## Safety

Advanced; build up with hanging knee raises first and avoid with shoulder issues.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
