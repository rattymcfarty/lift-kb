# Romanian Deadlift (Dumbbell)

A beginner dumbbell hinge-pattern exercise for the hamstrings. Romanian Deadlift (Dumbbell) builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Instructions

1. Stand holding dumbbells at your thighs, feet hip-width apart.
2. Push the hips back, lowering the dumbbells along your legs.
3. Descend until you feel a strong hamstring stretch, back flat.
4. Drive the hips forward to stand. The dumbbells allow a natural path.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
