# Legs Up Bench Press (Barbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Overview

A flat bench press with the feet up on the bench or in the air, reducing leg drive and lower-back arch.

## Instructions

1. Lie on a flat bench with your feet raised and knees bent, holding the bar with an overhand grip slightly wider than shoulder-width.
2. Retract your shoulder blades and brace your core.
3. Lower the bar to your mid-chest.
4. Press the bar back up to full extension.
5. Re-rack the bar when done.

## Cues

- Shoulder blades pinned
- Ribs down
- Bar touches mid-chest

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back
- Bouncing the bar off the chest
- Flaring the elbows

## Safety

Use a spotter or safeties; use a lighter weight than a normal bench press.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
