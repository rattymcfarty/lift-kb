# High Row

A beginner machine pull-pattern exercise for the upper back. High Row strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Lats, Rear Delts

## Instructions

1. Sit at the high row machine with chest against the pad.
2. Pull the handles toward your upper chest, elbows flaring slightly.
3. Squeeze the shoulder blades together at full contraction.
4. Extend slowly. Targets the upper back and rear delts.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
