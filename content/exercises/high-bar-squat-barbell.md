# High Bar Squat (Barbell)

A barbell back squat with the bar high on the traps, emphasising the quads and an upright torso.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Abs, Adductors

## Instructions

1. Set the bar on your upper traps and un-rack it, stepping back with feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Brace your core and take a breath.
3. Squat down by bending your hips and knees, keeping your torso upright and knees tracking over your toes.
4. Descend until your thighs are at least parallel.
5. Drive up through your whole foot to stand.

## Cues

- Chest tall
- Knees over toes
- Drive through the whole foot

## Common mistakes

- Letting the knees cave
- Rounding the lower back
- Rising hips-first

## Safety

Set safety pins at the right height and use a spotter for heavy loads.

## Longevity notes

Squatting is the foundation of getting up, sitting down, and climbing stairs. Keeping leg strength and knee range preserves independence decades longer than any single other movement.
