# I, Y, T (IYT) Raise

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Middle Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Rear Delts

## Overview

A prone sequence of arm raises that strengthens the lower traps, rear delts and shoulder stabilisers.

## Instructions

1. Lie face down on a bench or floor with arms hanging or extended and thumbs up.
2. Raise your arms straight overhead to form an I, squeezing your shoulder blades.
3. Lower, then raise your arms at about 45 degrees to form a Y.
4. Lower, then raise your arms out to the sides to form a T.
5. Repeat the sequence under control.

## Cues

- Thumbs up
- Squeeze shoulder blades
- Light and slow

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging
- Arching the back
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use very light weights or none; stop if you feel shoulder pinching.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
