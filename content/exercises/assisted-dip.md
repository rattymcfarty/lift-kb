# Assisted Dip

A beginner machine press-pattern exercise targeting the triceps. Assisted Dip builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Set up at the assisted dip machine or loop a band over the dip bars.
2. Kneel on the platform or place your feet in the band, gripping the bars.
3. Lower your body until shoulders are below elbows.
4. Press back up to straight arms. Reduce assistance as you get stronger.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
