# Kettlebell Hang Clean

Kettlebell Hang Clean is an intermediate hip-hinge exercise targeting the hamstrings, performed with a kettlebell. The hinge is the foundation of safe lifting — it teaches you to load the hips, not the spine.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Calves, Glutes, Lower Back, Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Place kettlebell between your feet. To get in the starting position, push your butt back and look straight ahead.
2. Clean kettlebell to your shoulder. Clean the kettlebell to your shoulders by extending through the legs and hips as you raise the kettlebell towards your shoulder. The wrist should rotate as you do so.
3. Lower kettlebell to a hanging position between your legs while keeping the hamstrings loaded. Keep your head up at all times.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
