# Pull-Up (Bodyweight)

A classic bodyweight vertical pull that trains the lats, upper back and biceps.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Instructions

1. Hang from a bar with an overhand grip slightly wider than shoulder-width.
2. Pull your shoulder blades down and back.
3. Pull your chest toward the bar by driving your elbows down.
4. Pause with your chin above the bar.
5. Lower slowly to a full hang.

## Cues

- Chest to the bar
- Elbows down
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Kipping
- Half reps
- Shrugging

## Safety

Use assistance if needed; stop with elbow or shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
