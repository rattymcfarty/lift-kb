# Long Pause Bench Press

An advanced barbell press-pattern exercise targeting the chest. Long Pause Bench Press builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Unrack a barbell and lower it to your chest with control.
2. Pause for a full 3-5 seconds — the bar rests dead on your chest.
3. Press explosively to lockout without bouncing.
4. The extended pause builds exceptional starting strength and control.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
