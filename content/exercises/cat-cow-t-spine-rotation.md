# Cat-Cow with T-Spine Rotation

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

Cat-Cow with T-Spine Rotation is a beginner mobility exercise for the upper back, performed with just your bodyweight. Regular mobility work keeps joints moving freely and makes strength training safer.

## Instructions

1. Start on all fours, hands under shoulders, knees under hips.
2. Inhale: drop the belly, lift the chest and tailbone (cow). Exhale: round the spine, tuck chin and pelvis (cat).
3. After several cycles, thread one arm under your body, rotating through the mid-back.
4. Return and open the same arm to the ceiling, following your hand with your eyes. Alternate sides.

## Cues

- Move slowly, no forcing
- Breathe into the stretch
- Stay relaxed outside the target area

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
