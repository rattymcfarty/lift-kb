# Belt Squat

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Overview

An intermediate machine squat-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Belt Squat develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Instructions

1. Strap into the belt-squat machine and stand on the platform with feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Release the handles and squat down, keeping the torso upright and knees tracking over toes.
3. Descend until thighs are at least parallel.
4. Drive through the heels to stand. The belt loads the legs without spinal compression.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
