# Side Plank

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs, Glutes

## Overview

A beginner just your bodyweight core exercise for the abs. Side Plank builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your side with your forearm on the floor, elbow under shoulder.
2. Lift your hips so your body forms a straight line from head to feet.
3. Hold, keeping the hips from sagging or rotating.
4. Breathe steadily and switch sides. Builds lateral core stability.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
