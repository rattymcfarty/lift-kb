# Farmer's Walk (Weighted)

A loaded carry holding heavy dumbbells at your sides that builds grip, upper back, core and overall stability.

## Details

- **Category:** Carry
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** carry
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Forearms, Upper Back

## Instructions

1. Stand between two heavy dumbbells and pick them up by hinging at the hips with a flat back.
2. Stand tall with your shoulders pulled back and down, arms at your sides.
3. Walk with short controlled steps for a set distance or time.
4. Keep your core braced and avoid leaning to either side.
5. Set the weights down under control.

## Cues

- Stand tall
- Shoulders back and down
- Short quick steps

## Common mistakes

- Leaning to one side
- Looking down and rounding
- Dropping the weights

## Safety

Pick up and set down with good hinge form; use a load you can hold with good posture.

## Longevity notes

Carrying is the most functional strength there is — groceries, luggage, a grandchild. Loaded carries build grip, posture, and core stability in one movement.
