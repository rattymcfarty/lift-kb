# 90/90 Hip Flow

90/90 Hip Flow is a beginner mobility exercise for the glutes, performed with just your bodyweight. Regular mobility work keeps joints moving freely and makes strength training safer.

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Abductors

## Instructions

1. Sit with both knees bent 90 degrees — one leg in front, one behind, shins roughly parallel.
2. Lift both knees slightly and rotate your hips to switch sides, keeping your chest tall.
3. Move slowly and continuously, flowing side to side.
4. Keep both sit bones grounded — don't hike a hip. Continue 30-60 seconds, breathing steadily.

## Cues

- Move slowly, no forcing
- Breathe into the stretch
- Stay relaxed outside the target area

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
