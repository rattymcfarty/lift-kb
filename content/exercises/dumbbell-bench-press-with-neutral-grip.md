# Dumbbell Bench Press with Neutral Grip

A beginner dumbbell press-pattern exercise targeting the chest. Dumbbell Bench Press with Neutral Grip builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts, Triceps

## Instructions

1. Take a dumbbell in each hand and lay back onto a flat bench. Your feet should be flat on the floor and your shoulder blades retracted.
2. Maintaining a neutral grip, palms facing each other, begin with your arms extended directly above you, perpendicular to the floor. This will be your starting position.
3. Begin the movement by flexing the elbow, lowering the upper arms to the side. Descend until the dumbbells are to your torso.
4. Pause, then extend the elbow and return to the starting position.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
