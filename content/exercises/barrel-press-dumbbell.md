# Barrel Press (Dumbbell)

A neutral-grip dumbbell chest press performed with the dumbbells held close, emphasising chest squeeze and the triceps.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Lie on a bench with a dumbbell in each hand, palms facing each other and the weights held over your chest.
2. Keep the dumbbells touching or very close together as you lower them toward your sternum.
3. Lower until your elbows are just below the bench line.
4. Press the dumbbells straight up, squeezing them together.
5. Keep your shoulder blades pinched against the bench.

## Cues

- Keep the dumbbells together
- Shoulder blades pinched
- Press straight up

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbows flare out wide
- Losing the squeeze between the dumbbells
- Lifting the hips off the bench

## Safety

Use a weight you can control; have a spotter or set the dumbbells down safely when finished.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
