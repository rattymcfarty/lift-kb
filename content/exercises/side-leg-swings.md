# Side Leg Swings

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abductors
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Overview

Side Leg Swings is a beginner core exercise targeting the abductors, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. Stand sideways to a wall, holding it for balance.
2. Swing the outside leg laterally across your body and back.
3. Keep the torso tall and the swinging leg straight.
4. Do all reps, then switch sides. A dynamic hip mobility drill.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
