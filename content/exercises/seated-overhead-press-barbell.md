# Seated Overhead Press (Barbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Middle Delts, Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A seated barbell overhead press that removes leg drive and focuses on the shoulders and triceps.

## Instructions

1. Sit on a bench with back support and hold the bar at shoulder height.
2. Brace your core.
3. Press the bar up until your arms are extended.
4. Lower the bar slowly to your chin or upper chest.
5. Re-rack safely.

## Cues

- Ribs down
- Press straight up
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Arching the back
- Pressing in front of the face
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a rack with safeties; stop with shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
