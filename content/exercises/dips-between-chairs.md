# Dips Between Chairs

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps, Front Delts

## Overview

A tricep dip using two stable chairs when no dip station is available.

## Instructions

1. Place two sturdy chairs facing each other and rest your hands on the edges, with your body between them and your feet on the floor or on a third chair.
2. Keep your chest up and shoulders away from your ears.
3. Lower your body by bending your elbows until they are at about 90 degrees.
4. Press back up to straight arms.
5. Keep your elbows pointing back.

## Cues

- Chest up
- Elbows back
- Shoulders down

## Common mistakes

- Letting the shoulders roll forward
- Going too low
- Using unstable chairs

## Safety

Make sure the chairs are stable and will not slide; stop if your shoulders feel pinched.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
