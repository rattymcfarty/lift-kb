# Block Pull (Barbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back, Abs

## Overview

A deadlift from an elevated start (blocks), shortening the range so you can train the upper portion of the pull heavy.

## Instructions

1. Set the barbell on blocks so it sits just below knee height and stand with your mid-foot under it.
2. Hinge down and grip the bar just outside your legs with a flat back.
3. Take the slack out of the bar by pulling your shoulders slightly back.
4. Drive through your feet and extend your hips and knees together to stand tall.
5. Lower the bar back to the blocks under control.

## Cues

- Chest up, back flat
- Push the floor away
- Squeeze glutes at the top

## Common mistakes

- Jerking the bar off the blocks
- Rounding the lower back
- Overextending at the top

## Safety

Use a belt and appropriate load; avoid if you have acute back pain.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
