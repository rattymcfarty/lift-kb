# B-Stance Romanian Deadlift (Dumbbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Lower Back, Adductors, Abs

## Overview

A Romanian deadlift variation with a staggered stance that shifts most of the load onto the front leg while the back foot only stabilises.

## Instructions

1. Stand holding a dumbbell in each hand, one foot flat and the other a half-step behind with only the toes lightly touching the floor.
2. Soften the front knee and push your hips straight back, sliding the dumbbells down along your front thigh.
3. Lower until you feel a strong hamstring stretch while your back stays flat.
4. Drive your front foot through the floor and squeeze the glutes to stand tall.
5. Complete all reps, then switch the staggered foot.

## Cues

- Hips back, not down
- Most of the weight on the front foot
- Flat back, long spine

## Common mistakes

- Putting too much weight on the back foot
- Rounding the lower back at the bottom
- Turning it into a squat by bending the knees too much

## Safety

Use a load you can control on one leg; stop if you feel pinching in the low back.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
