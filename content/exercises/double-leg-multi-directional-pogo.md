# Double Leg Multi-Directional Pogo

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Calves, Quadriceps

## Overview

A series of small, quick two-footed hops in different directions that builds ankle stiffness and reactive strength.

## Instructions

1. Stand tall with feet together and knees soft.
2. Hop forward and back in small quick jumps using mostly your ankles.
3. Then hop side to side, then diagonally.
4. Keep your contacts short and quiet.
5. Keep your chest up and your core braced.

## Cues

- Quick and light
- Stiff ankles
- Quiet landings

## Common mistakes

- Bending the knees deeply
- Landing heavy
- Looking down

## Safety

Skip if you have Achilles or ankle problems; surface should be firm and non-slip.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
