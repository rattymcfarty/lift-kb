# Kelso Shrug (Chest Supported)

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back

## Overview

A chest-supported shrug that targets the mid and lower traps by squeezing the shoulder blades together.

## Instructions

1. Lie chest-down on an incline bench holding dumbbells with arms hanging.
2. Keep your arms straight and retract your shoulder blades.
3. Squeeze your shoulder blades together without bending your elbows.
4. Pause for a second.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Squeeze shoulder blades
- Arms straight
- Slow tempo

## Common mistakes

- Bending the elbows
- Shrugging toward the ears
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use light weights; stop with neck or shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
