# Calf Raise (Bodyweight)

A bodyweight standing calf raise for the gastrocnemius and soleus.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Calves

## Instructions

1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart, holding a wall or rail for balance if needed.
2. Press through the balls of your feet and rise as high as possible.
3. Pause at the top, squeezing your calves.
4. Lower slowly until your heels are just below the level of your toes if you are on a step, or to the floor.
5. Keep your knees straight but not locked.

## Cues

- Rise tall
- Pause at the top
- Slow down on the way down

## Common mistakes

- Bouncing at the bottom
- Rolling the ankles outward
- Cutting the range short

## Safety

Use a step edge only if your balance is steady; hold a support.

## Longevity notes

Squatting is the foundation of getting up, sitting down, and climbing stairs. Keeping leg strength and knee range preserves independence decades longer than any single other movement.
