# Decline Crunch (Weighted)

A crunch on a decline bench with added weight for a greater abdominal challenge.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Secure your feet on a decline bench and lie back holding a plate against your chest.
2. Cross your arms over the weight.
3. Curl your torso up, bringing your ribs toward your pelvis.
4. Pause briefly at the top.
5. Lower slowly without letting your lower back arch.

## Cues

- Ribs to hips
- Slow lowering
- Weight on the chest

## Common mistakes

- Pulling on the neck
- Using momentum
- Sitting all the way up like a sit-up

## Safety

Keep the weight modest; avoid with acute back pain.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
