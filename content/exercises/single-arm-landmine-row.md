# Single-Arm Landmine Row

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Upper Back

## Overview

An intermediate barbell pull-pattern exercise for the lats. Single-Arm Landmine Row strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Wedge a barbell into a landmine base.
2. Hinge forward and row the free end one-handed to your hip.
3. The angled bar path hits the lats from a unique angle.
4. Lower slowly and repeat. Brace the core to resist rotation.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
