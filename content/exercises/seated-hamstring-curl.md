# Seated Hamstring Curl

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings

## Overview

A machine seated hamstring curl that isolates the hamstrings.

## Instructions

1. Sit in the machine with the pad above your heels and the thigh pad secured.
2. Grip the handles.
3. Curl your heels back and down toward your glutes.
4. Squeeze your hamstrings.
5. Return slowly.

## Cues

- Squeeze at the bottom
- Slow return
- Hips stay down

## Common mistakes

- Using momentum
- Lifting the hips
- Dropping the weights

## Safety

Use a moderate weight; stop with cramping or sharp pain.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
