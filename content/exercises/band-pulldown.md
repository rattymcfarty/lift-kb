# Pulldown (Band)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

Pulldown (Band) is a beginner pulling exercise for the lats, performed with minimal equipment. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Instructions

1. Anchor a band overhead and grip it with both hands.
2. Pull the band down to your upper chest, driving the elbows down and back.
3. Squeeze the lats at full contraction.
4. Return slowly. A portable lat pulldown alternative.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
