# Rolling Triceps Extension

An intermediate dumbbell press-pattern exercise targeting the triceps. Rolling Triceps Extension builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps

## Instructions

1. Lie on a bench holding dumbbells, arms extended over your chest.
2. Lower the weights toward your shoulders by bending the elbows, letting them drift slightly past your head.
3. Reverse by pulling the elbows forward and extending back to the top.
4. The rolling action keeps constant tension on the triceps.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
