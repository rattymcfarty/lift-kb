# Meadows Curl

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Forearms

## Overview

An intermediate dumbbell pull-pattern exercise for the biceps. Meadows Curl strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Hold a dumbbell with a neutral grip at your side.
2. Curl the weight up while externally rotating — finish with palm facing you.
3. Squeeze the bicep hard at the top.
4. Lower slowly, rotating back to neutral. John Meadows' bicep peak builder.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
