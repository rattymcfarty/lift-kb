# Lying Lateral Raise (Cable)

An intermediate cable press-pattern exercise targeting the middle delts. Lying Lateral Raise (Cable) builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Middle Delts

## Instructions

1. Lie sideways on a bench with the low cable behind you, holding the handle.
2. Keeping a slight elbow bend, raise your arm out to the side.
3. Lift until your arm is vertical, feeling the side delt contract.
4. Lower slowly. The cable maintains tension through the full range.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
