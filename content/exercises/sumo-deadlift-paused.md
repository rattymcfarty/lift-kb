# Sumo Deadlift (Paused)

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Adductors

## Overview

A sumo deadlift with a pause just off the floor that builds position and strength at the start.

## Instructions

1. Set a wide stance with toes turned out and grip the bar inside your legs.
2. Brace your core and pull the slack out of the bar.
3. Lift the bar a couple of inches and pause for 1 to 2 seconds.
4. Continue pulling to lockout.
5. Lower under control.

## Cues

- Knees out
- Chest up
- Hold the pause tight

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the back
- Losing tension in the pause
- Hips shooting up

## Safety

Use a lighter load than normal; use a belt if needed.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
