# Reverse Abs Crunch (Bodyweight)

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A reverse crunch that lifts the hips off the floor to train the lower abs.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back with knees bent at 90 degrees and arms at your sides.
2. Press your lower back into the floor.
3. Curl your hips up toward your ribs.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Curl the pelvis
- Slow lowering
- Don't swing

## Common mistakes

- Using momentum
- Pulling with the arms
- Arching the lower back on the way down

## Safety

Keep the motion small and controlled.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
