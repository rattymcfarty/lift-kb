# Goblet Duck Walk

An intermediate dumbbell squat-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Goblet Duck Walk develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Calves

## Instructions

1. Hold a dumbbell vertically at your chest, elbows tucked (goblet position).
2. Sink into a deep squat and stay low.
3. Walk forward in the deep squat with small, controlled steps — no bouncing.
4. Keep your chest tall and heels down for the prescribed distance.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
