# Pogo Jump

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Calves
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps

## Overview

Pogo Jump is a beginner conditioning exercise performed with just your bodyweight, engaging the calves. It raises the heart rate while building work capacity and coordination.

## Instructions

1. Stand tall with feet hip-width apart.
2. Bounce lightly off the balls of your feet, keeping the knees nearly straight.
3. Use quick ankle action with minimal ground contact time.
4. Stay relaxed through the shoulders and continue 20-30 seconds, staying springy.

## Cues

- Land soft and quiet
- Stay tall, pump the arms
- Breathe rhythmically

## Longevity notes

Cardiorespiratory fitness is one of the strongest known predictors of longevity. Short, regular conditioning sessions compound enormously over a lifetime.
