# Ball Slam

Ball Slam is a beginner core exercise targeting the abs, performed with minimal equipment. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Full-Body

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, holding a medicine ball overhead.
2. Slam the ball into the floor as hard as you can, hinging at the hips.
3. Catch it on the bounce (or pick it up) and immediately reload.
4. A full-body power and conditioning exercise. Use a non-bouncing slam ball.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
