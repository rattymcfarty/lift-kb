# A-Skip

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Calves

## Overview

A beginner just your bodyweight conditioning drill for the quadriceps and glutes. A-Skip improves cardiovascular fitness and athletic coordination.

## Instructions

1. Begin jogging lightly in place.
2. Drive one knee up to hip height while hopping slightly off the planted foot.
3. Strike the ground under your hips with a quick pawing motion, pumping the opposite arm.
4. Stay tall and rhythmic — this is a coordination drill, not a sprint.

## Cues

- Land soft and quiet
- Stay tall, pump the arms
- Breathe rhythmically

## Longevity notes

Cardiorespiratory fitness is one of the strongest known predictors of longevity. Short, regular conditioning sessions compound enormously over a lifetime.
