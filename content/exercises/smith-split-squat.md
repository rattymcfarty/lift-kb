# Split Squat (Smith Machine)

Split Squat (Smith Machine) is a beginner lower-body exercise that builds the quadriceps, usually performed with a Smith machine. It trains the deep knee-and-hip bend you use every time you sit, stand, or climb stairs.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Instructions

1. Set the Smith bar at shoulder height and stand in a split stance under it.
2. Unrack and lower straight down until the front thigh is parallel.
3. The fixed bar path lets you focus purely on the legs.
4. Drive through the front heel to stand. Alternate legs each set.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
