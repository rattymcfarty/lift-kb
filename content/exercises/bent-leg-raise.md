# Bent Leg Raise

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A floor-based lower-abdominal exercise where you raise bent legs by tilting your pelvis.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back with your hands under your hips or at your sides and knees bent to 90 degrees.
2. Press your lower back into the floor and tilt your pelvis up.
3. Lift your hips slightly off the floor by bringing your knees toward your chest.
4. Lower your legs slowly until your feet hover near the floor.
5. Keep your lower back pressed down throughout.

## Cues

- Lower back stays flat
- Curl the pelvis up
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the legs
- Arching the lower back as the legs lower
- Using the hip flexors only

## Safety

Stop the lowering before your lower back arches; skip it with acute back pain.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
