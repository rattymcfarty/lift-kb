# Jammer Press

Jammer Press is an intermediate pushing exercise for the chest, performed with a machine. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts, Triceps

## Instructions

1. Stand facing the jammer arms, gripping the handles at chest height.
2. Drive the handles forward and up in one explosive motion.
3. Extend fully, as if throwing the weight.
4. Return with control. Trains explosive pressing for football linemen.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
