# SkiErg

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

A cardio machine that mimics the double-pole motion of skiing for full-body conditioning.

## Instructions

1. Stand on the footplates holding the handles above your head.
2. Pull the handles down and back while hinging slightly at the hips.
3. Let the handles rise as you stand tall.
4. Keep a smooth rhythm.
5. Set the damper to a comfortable level.

## Cues

- Hinge and pull
- Smooth rhythm
- Tall at the top

## Common mistakes

- Pulling with only the arms
- Rounding the back
- Going too hard too soon

## Safety

Warm up; stop for chest pain or dizziness.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
