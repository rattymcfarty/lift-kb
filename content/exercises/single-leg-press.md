# Single Leg Press

A machine leg press done with one leg at a time to train the quads and glutes while correcting side-to-side imbalances.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings

## Instructions

1. Sit in the leg press with one foot on the platform and the other leg relaxed or off.
2. Unlock the safeties.
3. Lower the sled by bending your knee toward your chest.
4. Press through your whole foot to extend.
5. Re-lock the safeties and switch legs.

## Cues

- Knee tracks over toes
- Back flat
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips lift
- Locking the knee
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a lighter weight than two-leg presses; use safeties.

## Longevity notes

Squatting is the foundation of getting up, sitting down, and climbing stairs. Keeping leg strength and knee range preserves independence decades longer than any single other movement.
