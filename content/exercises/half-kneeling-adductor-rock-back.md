# Half-Kneeling Adductor Rock Back

A beginner just your bodyweight mobility drill targeting the adductors. Half-Kneeling Adductor Rock Back restores range of motion that desk life and hard training both tend to steal.

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Adductors
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Instructions

1. Start in a half-kneeling position with the front knee bent 90 degrees and the back leg extended straight out to the side, foot flat on the floor.
2. Shift your hips gently toward the extended leg until you feel a stretch in the inner thigh.
3. Keep your torso tall and core braced — don't let the lower back round.
4. Hold 2-3 seconds, rock back to center, and repeat, gradually increasing range as the tissue warms up.

## Cues

- Move slowly, no forcing
- Breathe into the stretch
- Stay relaxed outside the target area

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
