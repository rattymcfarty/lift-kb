# Band Pull Apart

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts

## Overview

A simple resistance-band exercise for the rear shoulders and upper back that counters the forward-hunched posture of desk work.

## Instructions

1. Hold a resistance band in front of you at chest height, hands shoulder-width apart, arms straight.
2. Pull the band apart by moving your hands out to the sides, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
3. Keep your arms nearly straight and your ribs down.
4. Pause when the band touches your chest or you reach full range.
5. Return slowly to the start.

## Cues

- Pinch shoulder blades together
- Ribs down, arms long
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging the shoulders
- Arching the lower back
- Letting the band snap back

## Safety

Anchor the band securely in your hands; avoid bands with visible tears.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
