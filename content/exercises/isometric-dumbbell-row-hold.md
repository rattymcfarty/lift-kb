# Isometric Dumbbell Row Hold

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

An intermediate dumbbell pull-pattern exercise for the lats and upper back. Isometric Dumbbell Row Hold strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Hinge forward with a dumbbell in each hand, back flat.
2. Row both dumbbells to your ribs, squeezing the shoulder blades together.
3. Hold the top position 10-20 seconds, keeping the torso still.
4. Lower with control and rest before the next hold.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
