# Dragon Fly

A dragon-flag style exercise where the legs sweep down and up while your upper back is supported.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Lie on a bench and hold the bench behind your head.
2. Lift your legs and hips up so your body is in a near-vertical line.
3. Slowly lower your straight legs as far as you can control.
4. Keep your lower back from arching.
5. Raise your legs back up by squeezing your abs.

## Cues

- Straight legs
- Slow and controlled
- Abs braced

## Common mistakes

- Letting the back arch
- Using momentum
- Going lower than you can control

## Safety

Advanced; use a modified version to build up and skip it with back pain.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
