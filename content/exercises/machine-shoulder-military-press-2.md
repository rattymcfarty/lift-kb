## Overview

A beginner machine press-pattern exercise targeting the front delts. Machine Shoulder (Military) Press builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Instructions

1. Sit down on the Shoulder Press Machine and select the weight.
2. Grab the handles to your sides as you keep the elbows bent and in line with your torso. This will be your starting position.
3. Now lift the handles as you exhale and you extend the arms fully. At the top of the position make sure that you hold the contraction for a second.
4. Lower the handles slowly back to the starting position as you inhale.
5. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
