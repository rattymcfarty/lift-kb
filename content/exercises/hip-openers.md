# Hip Openers

A sequence of dynamic movements that mobilise the hips in multiple directions.

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Instructions

1. Stand tall with feet hip-width apart.
2. Lift one knee and circle it out to the side and back down.
3. Reverse the direction, circling forward.
4. Repeat 8 to 10 times on each leg.
5. Keep your standing leg stable and your torso upright.

## Cues

- Slow big circles
- Tall posture
- Stay balanced

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the leg too fast
- Leaning to one side
- Holding your breath

## Safety

Hold a wall for balance; move within a pain-free range.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
