# Step-Up (Weighted)

A beginner dumbbell squat-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Step-Up (Weighted) develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Instructions

1. Hold dumbbells at your sides, facing a knee-high box or bench.
2. Step up with one foot, driving through the heel to stand on the box.
3. Bring the trailing foot up, then step back down with control.
4. Alternate legs. Don't push off the trailing foot — the working leg does it all.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
