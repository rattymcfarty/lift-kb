# Decline Sit Up (Weighted)

A decline sit-up with added load for more abdominal and hip-flexor strength.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Hook your feet under the pads of a decline bench and hold a plate against your chest.
2. Brace your core.
3. Curl up and sit all the way up.
4. Pause briefly at the top.
5. Lower slowly back down.

## Cues

- Hug the weight
- Curl up
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Using too much weight
- Jerking up
- Holding the weight behind the neck

## Safety

Keep the weight light; skip with low-back issues.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
