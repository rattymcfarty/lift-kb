# Reverse Pec Deck

A machine reverse fly for the rear delts and upper back.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts, Upper Back

## Instructions

1. Sit facing the pad of a pec-deck machine and grip the handles.
2. Keep a slight bend in your elbows and your chest against the pad.
3. Pull your arms back until they are in line with your shoulders.
4. Squeeze your shoulder blades together.
5. Return slowly.

## Cues

- Chest on the pad
- Squeeze shoulder blades
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging
- Using momentum
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a light-to-moderate weight.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
