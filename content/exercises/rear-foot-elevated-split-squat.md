# Rear Foot Elevated Split Squat

A split squat with the back foot on a bench that trains the quads and glutes one side at a time.

## Details

- **Category:** Lunge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** lunge
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Abs

## Instructions

1. Stand a couple of feet in front of a bench and place your back foot on it, laces down.
2. Hold dumbbells at your sides.
3. Lower your back knee toward the floor by bending your front knee.
4. Keep your torso upright and your front foot flat.
5. Drive through your front foot to stand.

## Cues

- Torso tall
- Front foot flat
- Back knee drops straight down

## Common mistakes

- Front knee caving
- Standing too close to the bench
- Leaning forward

## Safety

Start with bodyweight; stop with knee pain.

## Longevity notes

Single-leg strength and balance are what keep you steady on uneven ground and stairs. Lunge work trains the stability that prevents the falls that change lives.
