# Behind-the-Neck Push Press

An explosive overhead press from behind the neck that uses a small leg drive to move the bar overhead.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Triceps

## Instructions

1. Stand with a barbell resting on the back of your shoulders, hands wide, feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Dip a few inches by bending your knees, keeping your torso upright.
3. Drive up through your legs and press the bar overhead in one motion.
4. Lock out your arms with your head slightly forward of the bar.
5. Lower the bar back to your shoulders under control.

## Cues

- Dip, drive, press
- Torso stays upright
- Bar finishes over your mid-foot

## Common mistakes

- Leaning back to push the bar
- Lowering the bar below the shoulders
- Dipping too deep

## Safety

Requires good shoulder mobility and a light load; stop if the shoulders pinch.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
