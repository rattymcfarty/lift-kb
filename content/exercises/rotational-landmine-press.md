# Rotational Landmine Push Press

An advanced barbell press-pattern exercise targeting the abs. Rotational Landmine Push Press builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Stand perpendicular to a landmine bar, holding the free end at your shoulder.
2. Rotate the hips and press the bar up and across your body.
3. Pivot on the feet as you rotate — power comes from the hips.
4. Return with control. A rotational power exercise for athletes.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
