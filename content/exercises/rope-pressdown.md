# Rope Pressdown

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A cable triceps pressdown with a rope that lets you spread the ends at the bottom.

## Instructions

1. Attach a rope to a high pulley and grip the ends.
2. Keep your elbows pinned at your sides.
3. Press the rope down until your arms are straight, spreading the ends.
4. Squeeze your triceps.
5. Return slowly.

## Cues

- Elbows pinned
- Spread the rope at the bottom
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Using your body weight
- Flaring the elbows
- Rushing

## Safety

Use a moderate load; stop with elbow pain.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
