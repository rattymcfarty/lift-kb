# Chin-Up (Weighted)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

A chin-up with added load to progress upper-body pulling strength.

## Instructions

1. Hang from a bar with a supinated (palms toward you) grip at shoulder width, with a weight belt or dumbbell between your feet.
2. Pull your shoulder blades down and back.
3. Pull your chin above the bar by driving your elbows toward your ribs.
4. Pause briefly at the top.
5. Lower under control to a full hang.

## Cues

- Full hang to chin over bar
- Ribs down, no swinging
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Kipping or swinging
- Half reps
- Loading before you can do strict bodyweight reps

## Safety

Be able to do 8 to 10 strict bodyweight chin-ups first; stop if elbows or shoulders hurt.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
