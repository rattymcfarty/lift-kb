# Alternating Dumbbell Curl

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

A standing biceps curl where you alternate arms, giving each side time to rest and letting you focus on form.

## Instructions

1. Stand holding a dumbbell in each hand at your sides, palms facing forward.
2. Curl one dumbbell up toward your shoulder, keeping your elbow close to your torso.
3. Squeeze at the top, then lower the weight under control until the arm is straight.
4. Repeat with the other arm, alternating sides.
5. Keep your torso still the whole time.

## Cues

- Elbow pinned to your side
- No swinging
- Full range, slow down

## Common mistakes

- Rocking the body to lift the weight
- Letting the elbow slide forward
- Dropping the weight quickly

## Safety

Choose a weight you can lift without swinging. Stop if you feel sharp elbow or wrist pain.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
