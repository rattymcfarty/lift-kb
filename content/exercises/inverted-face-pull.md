# Inverted Face Pull

Inverted Face Pull is an intermediate pulling exercise for the rear delts, performed with just your bodyweight. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Upper Back, Biceps

## Instructions

1. Set a barbell low in a rack or use rings at chest height.
2. Hang underneath with straight body, heels on the floor.
3. Pull your face to the bar, driving the elbows high and wide.
4. Lower with control. A bodyweight face pull for the rear delts.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
