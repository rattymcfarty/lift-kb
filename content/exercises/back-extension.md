# Back Extension

A bodyweight hip-extension exercise that trains the lower back, glutes and hamstrings.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Lower Back, Glutes

## Instructions

1. Set the pad just below your hip crease and hook your heels under the rollers.
2. Cross your arms over your chest and hang down from the hips with a neutral spine.
3. Raise your torso by squeezing your glutes until your body is in a straight line.
4. Pause briefly at the top without arching further.
5. Lower slowly back to the start.

## Cues

- Move from the hips
- Straight line at the top
- Slow and smooth

## Common mistakes

- Overarching at the top
- Using speed instead of control
- Letting the neck crane up

## Safety

Stop at a straight line; avoid it if you have an acute back injury.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
