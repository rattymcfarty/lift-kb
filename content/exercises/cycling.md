# Cycling

Steady cycling on a stationary or road bike for low-impact cardiovascular fitness.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Instructions

1. Adjust the saddle so your knee is slightly bent at the bottom of the pedal stroke.
2. Sit tall with your hands relaxed on the handlebars.
3. Pedal at a steady cadence of about 70 to 90 rpm at a conversational effort.
4. Increase resistance or speed gradually for intervals or longer rides.
5. Cool down for several minutes at an easy pace.

## Cues

- Smooth circles
- Relaxed shoulders
- Steady breathing

## Common mistakes

- Saddle set too low or high
- Gripping the bars tightly
- Bouncing in the saddle

## Safety

Warm up before pushing hard; stop and seek advice for chest pain or dizziness.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
