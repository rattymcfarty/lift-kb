# Battle Ropes

A high-intensity conditioning drill where you whip heavy ropes in waves to elevate your heart rate and challenge your shoulders and core.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Middle Delts

## Instructions

1. Stand facing the anchor holding one rope end in each hand, feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent.
2. Hinge slightly at the hips and brace your core.
3. Alternately raise and snap each arm up and down to create continuous waves in the ropes.
4. Keep your chest tall and your shoulders relaxed away from your ears.
5. Work in timed intervals, resting when your waves get small.

## Cues

- Stay low and athletic
- Waves from the shoulders, not the wrists
- Breathe steadily

## Common mistakes

- Standing upright and stiff
- Rounding the lower back
- Gripping so hard the forearms fail early

## Safety

Anchor the rope securely; start with short intervals if you have shoulder or back issues.
