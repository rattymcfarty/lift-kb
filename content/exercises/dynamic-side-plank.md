# Dynamic Side Plank

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A side plank with hip raises and dips that trains the obliques and hip stabilisers.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your side and prop yourself on your forearm, elbow under your shoulder, legs stacked.
2. Lift your hips so your body forms a straight line from head to feet.
3. Lower your hips toward the floor a few inches under control.
4. Raise your hips back to the straight line.
5. Complete the reps, then switch sides.

## Cues

- Straight line
- Hips up
- Slow dips

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag
- Rolling the torso forward
- Rushing the reps

## Safety

Stay within a pain-free range; reduce to a knee-supported version if your shoulder hurts.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
