# Overhead Slam

Overhead Slam is a beginner pulling exercise for the lats, performed with minimal equipment. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Lats

## Instructions

1. Hold a medine ball with both hands and stand with your feet at shoulder width. This will be your starting position.
2. Initiate the countermovement by raising the ball above your head and fully extending your body.
3. Reverse the motion, slamming the ball into the ground directly in front of you as hard as you can.
4. Receive the ball with both hands on the bounce and repeat the movement.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
