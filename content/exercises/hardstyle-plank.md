# Hardstyle Plank

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Overview

An intermediate just your bodyweight core exercise for the abs. Hardstyle Plank builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Instructions

1. Set up in a forearm plank, elbows under shoulders.
2. Actively pull your elbows toward your toes and your toes toward your elbows to create full-body tension.
3. Squeeze the glutes hard and brace the abs as if for a punch.
4. Breathe shallowly behind the brace and hold 10-20 seconds — tension matters more than duration.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
