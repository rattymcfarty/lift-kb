# Pike Push-Up

Pike Push-Up is an intermediate pushing exercise for the front delts, performed with just your bodyweight. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Chest

## Instructions

1. Start in a push-up position, then walk your feet toward your hands, lifting hips high.
2. Your body forms an inverted V — this is the start position.
3. Bend the elbows to lower the top of your head toward the floor.
4. Press back up. A bodyweight shoulder press progression toward handstand push-ups.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
