# Cable Low Row

A seated cable row to a low pulley that trains the lats, mid-back and biceps with steady tension.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Instructions

1. Sit at a low-row station with your feet on the platform, knees slightly bent, and hold the handle with arms extended.
2. Sit tall with your chest up and shoulders back.
3. Pull the handle toward your lower abdomen, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
4. Pause briefly at the contraction.
5. Extend your arms slowly, letting your shoulders protract slightly without rounding your back.

## Cues

- Chest tall
- Elbows back
- Control the return

## Common mistakes

- Leaning back and swinging the torso
- Shrugging the shoulders
- Rounding the lower back

## Safety

Keep a neutral spine; use a load that doesn't force momentum.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
