# Cable Pullover

Cable Pullover is an intermediate pulling exercise for the lats, performed with a cable machine. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest

## Instructions

1. Set a cable to the highest position with a straight bar or rope.
2. Face the cable, hinge slightly at the hips, and grip the handle with straight arms.
3. Pull the handle down in an arc to your thighs, arms straight throughout.
4. Squeeze the lats hard at the bottom, then return slowly along the same arc.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
