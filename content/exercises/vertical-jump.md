# Vertical Jump

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Calves

## Overview

Vertical Jump is a beginner lower-body exercise that builds the quadriceps, usually performed with just your bodyweight. It trains the deep knee-and-hip bend you use every time you sit, stand, or climb stairs.

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Dip into a quarter squat, swinging your arms back.
3. Jump as high as possible, reaching for the sky.
4. Land softly on the balls of your feet with bent knees. The fundamental power test.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
