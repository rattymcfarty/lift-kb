# Kelso Shrug

An intermediate dumbbell pull-pattern exercise for the upper back. Kelso Shrug strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Rear Delts

## Instructions

1. Lie chest-down on an incline bench (30-45 degrees) holding dumbbells, arms hanging straight.
2. Without bending the elbows, shrug your shoulder blades up and together.
3. Squeeze the upper back hard at the top.
4. Lower slowly to a full stretch. This targets the mid-traps and rhomboids.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
