# Sprint

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

A maximal-effort short run that trains speed and power.

## Instructions

1. Warm up thoroughly with jogging and drills.
2. Accelerate from a stationary or rolling start.
3. Drive your knees and arms powerfully.
4. Run for 5 to 15 seconds at maximum effort.
5. Walk to recover between reps.

## Cues

- Drive the knees
- Powerful arms
- Tall posture

## Common mistakes

- Starting without a warm-up
- Overstriding
- Tensing the face

## Safety

Warm up fully; stop with any tightness in the hamstrings.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
