# Pull-Up (Neutral Grip, Weighted)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

A weighted neutral-grip pull-up for advanced upper-body pulling strength.

## Instructions

1. Attach a weight belt or hold a dumbbell between your feet.
2. Hang from neutral-grip handles with arms straight.
3. Pull your shoulder blades down and then pull your chest toward the handles.
4. Pause at the top.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Shoulders down
- Smooth rep
- No swinging

## Common mistakes

- Swinging with the weight
- Half reps
- Loading before you have strict bodyweight reps

## Safety

Build strict bodyweight reps first; stop with elbow or shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
