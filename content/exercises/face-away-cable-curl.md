# Face Away Cable Curl

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps, Forearms

## Overview

A cable curl with the body facing away from the stack that keeps the biceps under tension in the stretched position.

## Instructions

1. Set a cable pulley at about shoulder height and stand facing away, holding the handle with your arm extended behind or beside you.
2. Stand with one foot slightly forward for balance.
3. Curl your hand toward your head, keeping your upper arm in line with your shoulder.
4. Squeeze your biceps at the top.
5. Lower slowly until the arm is straight.

## Cues

- Upper arm stays still
- Squeeze at the top
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging the shoulder
- Letting the elbow drop
- Using momentum

## Safety

Use a light-to-moderate load; stop with elbow pain.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
