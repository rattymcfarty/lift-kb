# Overhead Extension (Dumbbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A dumbbell overhead triceps extension that stretches and loads the long head of the triceps.

## Instructions

1. Stand or sit holding one dumbbell with both hands overhead, elbows pointing forward.
2. Keep your upper arms close to your head.
3. Lower the dumbbell behind your head by bending your elbows.
4. Stop when you feel a stretch in your triceps.
5. Extend your arms back to the top.

## Cues

- Elbows point forward
- Upper arms still
- Full stretch and squeeze

## Common mistakes

- Flaring the elbows
- Arching the back
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a moderate weight; stop with elbow pain.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
