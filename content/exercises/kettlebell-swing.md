# Kettlebell Swing

A ballistic hip-hinge exercise that swings a kettlebell to chest height, training the glutes, hamstrings and posterior chain.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart and the kettlebell on the floor in front of you.
2. Hinge at the hips and grab the handle with both hands, flat back.
3. Hike the kettlebell back between your legs.
4. Snap your hips forward powerfully, standing tall and swinging the kettlebell to chest height.
5. Let the kettlebell fall back and hinge again to repeat.

## Cues

- Hinge, don't squat
- Snap the hips
- Let the arms float

## Common mistakes

- Squatting the movement
- Lifting with the arms
- Hyperextending the back at the top

## Safety

Learn the hip hinge first; keep a flat back and use a manageable weight.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
