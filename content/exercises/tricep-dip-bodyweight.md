# Tricep Dip (Bodyweight)

A bodyweight dip that trains the triceps and chest.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps, Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Middle Delts, Rear Delts

## Instructions

1. Support yourself on parallel bars or a bench with arms straight.
2. Lower your body by bending your elbows.
3. Stop when your elbows are about 90 degrees.
4. Press back up.
5. Keep your chest up.

## Cues

- Chest up
- Elbows back
- Shoulders down

## Common mistakes

- Going too deep
- Shrugging
- Flaring the elbows

## Safety

Stop if shoulders feel pinched; use assistance if needed.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
