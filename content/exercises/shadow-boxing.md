# Shadow Boxing

Punching and movement drills without a target for conditioning and coordination.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Instructions

1. Stand in a boxing stance with hands up.
2. Throw jabs and crosses at an imaginary opponent.
3. Move your feet and rotate your hips.
4. Work in timed rounds.
5. Rest and repeat.

## Cues

- Hands up
- Rotate hips
- Stay light on your feet

## Common mistakes

- Locking the elbows on punches
- Dropping the guard
- Flat feet

## Safety

Keep the elbows slightly soft to protect them.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
