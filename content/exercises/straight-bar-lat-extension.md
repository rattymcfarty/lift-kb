# Straight Bar Lat Extension

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back

## Overview

A straight-bar lat isolation movement using a cable.

## Instructions

1. Face a high pulley holding a straight bar.
2. Hinge forward slightly.
3. Pull the bar down in an arc to your thighs.
4. Squeeze your lats.
5. Return slowly.

## Cues

- Arms long
- Squeeze lats
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Bending the elbows
- Swinging
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a moderate load.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
