# Partial Lateral Raise (Cable)

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Middle Delts

## Overview

Partial Lateral Raise (Cable) is an intermediate pushing exercise for the middle delts, performed with a cable machine. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Instructions

1. Stand sideways to a low pulley holding the handle.
2. Raise your arm through the top half of the range only — from 45 degrees to parallel.
3. Keep constant tension; don't rest at the bottom.
4. Lower to 45 degrees and repeat. Partial reps overload the contracted position.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
