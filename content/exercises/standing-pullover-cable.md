# Standing Pullover (Cable)

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest, Triceps

## Overview

An intermediate cable pull-pattern exercise for the lats. Standing Pullover (Cable) strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Stand facing a high cable pulley, holding a straight bar with both hands.
2. With a slight elbow bend, pull the bar down in an arc to your thighs.
3. Keep the arms straight and feel the lats contract.
4. Return slowly to the start. Isolates the lats without biceps involvement.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
