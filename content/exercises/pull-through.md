# Pull Through

A beginner cable squat-pattern exercise for the glutes. Pull Through develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back

## Instructions

1. Begin standing a few feet in front of a low pulley with a rope or handle attached. Face away from the machine, straddling the cable, with your feet set wide apart.
2. Begin the movement by reaching through your legs as far as possible, bending at the hips. Keep your knees slightly bent. Keeping your arms straight, extend through the hip to stand straight up. Avoid pulling upward through the shoulders; all of the motion should originate through the hips.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
