# French Press

French Press is an intermediate pushing exercise for the triceps, performed with a barbell. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps

## Instructions

1. Lie on a bench holding an EZ bar or barbell with a narrow overhand grip, arms extended over your chest.
2. Bend only at the elbows, lowering the bar toward your forehead.
3. Keep the upper arms vertical and still throughout.
4. Extend the elbows to press back up. Also called a lying triceps extension or skull crusher.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
