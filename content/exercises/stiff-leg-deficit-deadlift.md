# Stiff Leg Deficit Deadlift

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Lower Back, Abs

## Overview

A stiff-leg deadlift from a raised platform that increases the stretch on the hamstrings.

## Instructions

1. Stand on a low platform with the bar over your mid-foot.
2. Hinge and grip the bar with nearly straight legs.
3. Lift the bar by extending your hips.
4. Lower the bar below the platform level under control.
5. Keep your back flat.

## Cues

- Hips back
- Flat back
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the back
- Locking the knees
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a lighter weight; stop with hamstring strain.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
