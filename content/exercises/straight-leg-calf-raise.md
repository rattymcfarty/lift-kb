# Straight Leg Calf Raise

A beginner just your bodyweight core exercise for the calves. Straight Leg Calf Raise builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Calves

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet hip-width apart, knees straight but not locked.
2. Rise onto the balls of your feet as high as possible.
3. Pause and squeeze the calves at the top.
4. Lower slowly. Straight knees emphasize the gastrocnemius.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
