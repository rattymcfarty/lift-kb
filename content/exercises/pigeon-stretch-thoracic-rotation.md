# Pigeon Stretch with Thoracic Rotation

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Upper Back

## Overview

A beginner just your bodyweight mobility drill targeting the glutes. Pigeon Stretch with Thoracic Rotation restores range of motion that desk life and hard training both tend to steal.

## Instructions

1. From a lunge, lower your front shin to the floor for pigeon pose, back leg extended.
2. Square your hips as best you can.
3. Place the hand opposite the front leg down and open the other arm to the ceiling, rotating the mid-back.
4. Follow your hand with your eyes and hold 30 seconds per side.

## Cues

- Move slowly, no forcing
- Breathe into the stretch
- Stay relaxed outside the target area

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
