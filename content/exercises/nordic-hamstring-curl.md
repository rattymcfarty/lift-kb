# Nordic Hamstring Curl

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Overview

An advanced bodyweight hinge-pattern exercise for the hamstrings. Nordic Hamstring Curl builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Instructions

1. Kneel with your feet firmly anchored (partner, bench, or foot hooks).
2. Keep your body in a straight line from knees to head — hips extended.
3. Lower yourself slowly toward the floor, resisting with the hamstrings.
4. Go as far as you can control, catch yourself with your hands, and push back up.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
