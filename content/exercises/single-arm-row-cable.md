# Single-Arm Row (Cable)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Upper Back, Biceps

## Overview

A beginner cable pull-pattern exercise for the lats. Single-Arm Row (Cable) strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Sit or stand at a cable station, holding a single handle.
2. Row the handle to your torso, driving the elbow back past your side.
3. Squeeze the lat hard at full contraction.
4. Extend slowly. The cable keeps constant tension on the back.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
