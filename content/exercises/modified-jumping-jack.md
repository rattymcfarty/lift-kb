# Modified Jumping Jack

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

A low-impact jumping jack variation where you step instead of jump.

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet together and arms at your sides.
2. Step one foot out to the side while raising your arms overhead.
3. Step it back and lower your arms.
4. Repeat with the other foot.
5. Keep a steady rhythm.

## Cues

- Steady rhythm
- Tall posture
- Arms overhead

## Common mistakes

- Rushing
- Slouching
- Holding your breath

## Safety

A good low-impact option for joint issues.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
