# Partial Calf Raise

Partial Calf Raise is a beginner core exercise targeting the calves, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Calves

## Instructions

1. Stand on the edge of a step with heels hanging off.
2. Rise onto your toes through the top half of the range only.
3. Squeeze the calves hard at the peak.
4. Lower to mid-range and repeat. Overloads the contracted position.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
