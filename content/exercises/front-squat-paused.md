# Front Squat (Paused)

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back

## Overview

A front squat with a one to two second pause at the bottom, which builds strength and control out of the hole.

## Instructions

1. Rest a barbell on the front of your shoulders with elbows high (clean grip or crossed arms).
2. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart and brace your core.
3. Squat down until your thighs are at least parallel, keeping your chest tall.
4. Pause for 1 to 2 seconds at the bottom without relaxing.
5. Drive up through your whole foot to stand.

## Cues

- Elbows high
- Chest tall
- Hold the pause tight

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbows drop
- Rounding the upper back
- Bouncing out of the bottom

## Safety

Use a lighter load than your normal front squat; set safety bars in a rack.

## Longevity notes

Squatting is the foundation of getting up, sitting down, and climbing stairs. Keeping leg strength and knee range preserves independence decades longer than any single other movement.
