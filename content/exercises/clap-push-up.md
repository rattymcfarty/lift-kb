# Clap Push Up

An explosive plyometric push-up where your hands leave the floor and clap at the top.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Start in a high plank position with hands under your shoulders and body in a straight line.
2. Lower your chest toward the floor with control.
3. Explode upward fast enough that your hands leave the floor, and clap.
4. Land with soft elbows and absorb the impact.
5. Immediately lower into the next rep or reset.

## Cues

- Straight body
- Explode up
- Land soft

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag
- Landing with locked elbows
- Rushing when fatigued

## Safety

Needs good wrist and shoulder health; start from your knees or an elevated surface if it is too hard.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
