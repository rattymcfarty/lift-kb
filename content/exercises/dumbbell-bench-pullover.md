# Dumbbell Bench Pullover

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A lying dumbbell pullover that stretches and loads the lats and chest through a long overhead arc.

## Instructions

1. Lie across or along a flat bench holding one dumbbell with both hands over your chest, arms nearly straight.
2. Take a breath and lower the dumbbell in an arc back over your head until you feel a stretch in your lats.
3. Keep a slight bend in your elbows and your ribs down.
4. Pull the dumbbell back over your chest using your lats.
5. Keep your hips low and your core braced.

## Cues

- Slight elbow bend
- Ribs down
- Pull with the lats, not the arms

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back
- Letting the weight drop behind the head
- Bending the elbows into a triceps extension

## Safety

Use a moderate weight and keep a firm grip on the dumbbell; stop at the stretch, not past it.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
