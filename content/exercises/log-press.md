# Log Press

A strongman-style overhead press with a log that trains the shoulders, triceps and whole-body stability.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Middle Delts, Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Upper Back, Abs

## Instructions

1. Lift the log to your chest using a clean, with your elbows under it.
2. Set your feet shoulder-width apart and brace your core.
3. Dip slightly and drive the log upward, pressing it overhead.
4. Lock out your arms with the log over your mid-foot.
5. Lower the log back to your chest under control.

## Cues

- Brace the core
- Drive with the legs
- Lock out overhead

## Common mistakes

- Leaning back too far
- Pressing with the arms only
- Dropping the log

## Safety

Use light logs while learning; use a spotter and clear space.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
