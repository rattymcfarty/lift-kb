# Flexion Row

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back, Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

A cable row performed with a slightly rounded upper back to train the mid-back through spinal flexion.

## Instructions

1. Sit at a cable row station with your feet on the platform and hold the handle with arms extended.
2. Lean forward slightly, letting your upper back round a little as your shoulders protract.
3. Pull the handle toward your torso while extending your spine and squeezing your shoulder blades.
4. Pause at the end.
5. Return slowly while allowing your upper back to round slightly again.

## Cues

- Slow smooth tempo
- Squeeze shoulder blades
- Control the stretch

## Common mistakes

- Over-rounding the lower back
- Using momentum
- Shrugging

## Safety

Keep the lower back neutral; only the upper back rounds slightly. Skip if you have disc problems.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
