# Mountain Climbers

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest, Hamstrings, Front Delts

## Overview

Mountain Climbers is a beginner conditioning exercise performed with minimal equipment, engaging the quadriceps. It raises the heart rate while building work capacity and coordination.

## Instructions

1. Begin in a pushup position, with your weight supported by your hands and toes. Flexing the knee and hip, bring one leg until the knee is approximately under the hip. This will be your starting position.
2. Explosively reverse the positions of your legs, extending the bent leg until the leg is straight and supported by the toe, and bringing the other foot up with the hip and knee flexed. Repeat in an alternating fashion for 20-30 seconds.

## Cues

- Land soft and quiet
- Stay tall, pump the arms
- Breathe rhythmically

## Longevity notes

Cardiorespiratory fitness is one of the strongest known predictors of longevity. Short, regular conditioning sessions compound enormously over a lifetime.
