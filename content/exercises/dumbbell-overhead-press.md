# Overhead Press (Dumbbell)

A beginner dumbbell press-pattern exercise targeting the front delts. Overhead Press (Dumbbell) builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Middle Delts

## Instructions

1. Stand or sit with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward.
2. Press the weights overhead until your arms are straight, without shrugging.
3. Keep the core braced and ribs down — don't lean back.
4. Lower slowly to shoulder height. Dumbbells allow a natural range of motion.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
