# Elliptical

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

Low-impact cardio on an elliptical trainer that spares the joints while raising your heart rate.

## Instructions

1. Step onto the pedals and hold the handles lightly.
2. Start moving in a smooth, fluid motion, standing tall.
3. Set a resistance that lets you keep a steady pace.
4. Keep your weight through your whole foot and your shoulders relaxed.
5. Finish with a few minutes of easy movement to cool down.

## Cues

- Stand tall
- Smooth strides
- Light grip

## Common mistakes

- Leaning heavily on the handles
- Rounding the shoulders
- Using too much resistance and jerking

## Safety

Hold a handle when stepping on or off; stop if you feel dizzy or chest pain.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
