# Shoulder Press (Plate Loaded)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Middle Delts

## Overview

A beginner machine press-pattern exercise targeting the front delts. Shoulder Press (Plate Loaded) builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Instructions

1. Sit at the plate-loaded shoulder press machine, back flat against the pad.
2. Grip the handles at shoulder height with a neutral or overhand grip.
3. Press the handles overhead until your arms are straight.
4. Lower with control. The plate-loaded path provides a natural pressing arc.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
