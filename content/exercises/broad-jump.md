# Broad Jump

An intermediate bodyweight hinge-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Broad Jump builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, arms at your sides.
2. Dip into a quarter squat, swinging your arms back.
3. Explode forward, swinging arms overhead and jumping for maximum horizontal distance.
4. Land softly on both feet with bent knees. Reset and repeat. Measures explosive power.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
