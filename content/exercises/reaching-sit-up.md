# Reaching Sit-Up

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

Reaching Sit-Up is a beginner core exercise targeting the abs, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back, knees bent, arms extended overhead.
2. Sit up, reaching your hands past your knees toward the ceiling.
3. Keep the motion smooth — don't yank with your neck.
4. Lower with control, returning arms overhead. The reach adds range and workload.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
