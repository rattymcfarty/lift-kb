# Machine Bicep Curl

Machine Bicep Curl is a beginner pulling exercise for the biceps, performed with a machine. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps

## Instructions

1. Adjust the seat to the appropriate height and make your weight selection. Place your upper arms against the pads and grasp the handles. This will be your starting position.
2. Perform the movement by flexing the elbow, pulling your lower arm towards your upper arm.
3. Pause at the top of the movement, and then slowly return the weight to the starting position.
4. Avoid returning the weight all the way to the stops until the set is complete to keep tension on the muscles being worked.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
