# Seated Anterior Delt Press (AD)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Middle Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A seated shoulder press variant that emphasises the front delts.

## Instructions

1. Sit on a bench with back support and hold the handles or dumbbells at shoulder height.
2. Brace your core.
3. Press the weight up and slightly forward.
4. Pause at the top.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Ribs down
- Press smoothly
- Control the lowering

## Common mistakes

- Arching the back
- Shrugging
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a moderate weight; stop with shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
