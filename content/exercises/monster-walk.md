# Monster Walk

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Abductors

## Overview

A beginner minimal equipment mobility drill targeting the abductors. Monster Walk restores range of motion that desk life and hard training both tend to steal.

## Instructions

1. Place a band around both ankles and another around both knees. There should be enough tension that they are tight when your feet are shoulder width apart.
2. To begin, take short steps forward alternating your left and right foot.
3. After several steps, do just the opposite and walk backward to where you started.

## Cues

- Move slowly, no forcing
- Breathe into the stretch
- Stay relaxed outside the target area

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
