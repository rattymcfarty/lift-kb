# Straight Arm Pulldown

A cable pulldown with straight arms that isolates the lats.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Rear Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Instructions

1. Stand facing a high cable with a bar or rope.
2. Hinge slightly forward with arms extended.
3. Pull the bar down to your thighs keeping your arms straight.
4. Squeeze your lats.
5. Return slowly.

## Cues

- Arms straight
- Squeeze lats
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Bending the elbows
- Using the body
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a moderate load.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
