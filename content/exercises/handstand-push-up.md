# Handstand Push Up

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Middle Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Rear Delts, Triceps

## Overview

A vertical pressing exercise performed upside down against a wall that builds shoulder and triceps strength.

## Instructions

1. Face a wall and kick up into a handstand with your hands about shoulder-width apart.
2. Brace your core and keep your body in a straight line.
3. Lower your head toward the floor under control.
4. Press back up until your arms are fully extended.
5. Come down from the handstand safely.

## Cues

- Straight body
- Controlled descent
- Press away from the floor

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back
- Flaring the elbows
- Dropping the head quickly

## Safety

Advanced; practise against a wall with a mat, and avoid if you have neck, shoulder or wrist issues.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
