# Plate Hop

Plate Hop is a beginner core exercise targeting the calves, performed with minimal equipment. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Calves
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps

## Instructions

1. Stand beside a weight plate on the floor.
2. Hop laterally over the plate, landing softly on both feet.
3. Immediately hop back to the start side.
4. Keep the hops quick and low. A lateral agility and calf drill.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
