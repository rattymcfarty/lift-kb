# Diamond Push Up

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Overview

A push-up with the hands close together under the chest that shifts more work to the triceps.

## Instructions

1. Start in a high plank and place your hands under your chest with thumbs and index fingers forming a diamond.
2. Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
3. Lower your chest toward your hands, elbows close to your body.
4. Press back up to straight arms.
5. Keep your core braced throughout.

## Cues

- Elbows close to the body
- Straight body
- Chest to hands

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag
- Flaring the elbows
- Cutting the range short

## Safety

Drop to your knees if the wrists or elbows feel strained.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
