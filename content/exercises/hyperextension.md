# Hyperextension

A bodyweight hyperextension that trains the lower back, glutes and hamstrings on a 45-degree bench.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Lower Back, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings

## Instructions

1. Position your hips on the pad of a 45-degree bench with your feet secured.
2. Cross your arms over your chest and hinge forward with a straight spine.
3. Raise your torso until your body is in a straight line.
4. Squeeze your glutes at the top.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Hinge at the hips
- Stop at a straight line
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Hyperextending the lower back
- Rounding the upper back
- Swinging with momentum

## Safety

Avoid with acute back problems; keep range moderate.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
