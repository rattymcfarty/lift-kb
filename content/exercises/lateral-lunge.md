# Lateral Lunge

## Details

- **Category:** Lunge
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** lunge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Adductors, Glutes

## Overview

Lateral Lunge is a beginner single-leg exercise for the quadriceps, performed with just your bodyweight. Training one leg at a time builds balance and evens out side-to-side differences.

## Instructions

1. Stand tall with feet together.
2. Take a big step to the side, sitting the hips back and bending the trailing knee.
3. Keep the stepping leg's foot flat and the trailing leg straight.
4. Push off the bent leg to return to center, then alternate. Hold dumbbells to load it.

## Cues

- Torso tall over hips
- Front knee over ankle
- Push through the front heel

## Longevity notes

Single-leg strength is balance insurance — the ability to catch yourself on one leg prevents falls. Train lunges for control, not maximal load.
