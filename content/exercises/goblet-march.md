# Goblet March

## Details

- **Category:** Carry
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Abs

## Overview

A beginner a kettlebell core exercise for the quadriceps. Goblet March builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Instructions

1. Hold a kettlebell or dumbbell at your chest in the goblet position.
2. Stand tall and march in place, driving each knee to hip height.
3. Brace the core and keep the torso vertical — don't lean back.
4. Continue for time or steps. Builds hip flexor strength and marching coordination.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
