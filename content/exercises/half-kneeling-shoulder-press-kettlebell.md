# Half Kneeling Shoulder Press (Kettlebell)

A single-arm kettlebell overhead press in a half-kneeling stance that demands core and hip stability.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Middle Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Instructions

1. Half-kneel with the knee down on the opposite side of the kettlebell arm, and hold the kettlebell at your shoulder in the rack position.
2. Brace your core and squeeze the glute of the down leg.
3. Press the kettlebell straight overhead.
4. Lock out with your biceps by your ear and ribs down.
5. Lower to the rack position with control.

## Cues

- Tall spine
- Squeeze the glute
- Ribs down at lockout

## Common mistakes

- Leaning sideways
- Arching the lower back
- Letting the wrist bend back

## Safety

Start light; stop if you feel pinching in the shoulder.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
