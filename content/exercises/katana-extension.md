# Katana Extension

A cable overhead triceps extension performed with a rotation of the arm to emphasise the triceps.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps

## Instructions

1. Set a cable pulley low and hold the handle in one hand.
2. Raise your arm overhead with the elbow bent and pointing up.
3. Extend your arm up and across your body, as if drawing a sword.
4. Squeeze your triceps at the top.
5. Return slowly under control.

## Cues

- Elbow stays high
- Squeeze at the top
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Flaring the elbow
- Using the shoulder
- Using too heavy a weight

## Safety

Use a lighter weight; stop with shoulder or elbow pain.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
