# Reverse Wrist Curl (Dumbbell)

A beginner dumbbell pull-pattern exercise for the forearms. Reverse Wrist Curl (Dumbbell) strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Forearms

## Instructions

1. Rest your forearm on a bench, holding a dumbbell with an overhand grip, wrist hanging off.
2. Extend the wrist upward, lifting the dumbbell.
3. Pause at the top contraction.
4. Lower slowly. Strengthens the wrist extensors.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
