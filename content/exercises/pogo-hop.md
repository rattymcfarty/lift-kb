# Pogo Hop / Jump

A beginner just your bodyweight core exercise for the calves. Pogo Hop / Jump builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Calves
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps

## Instructions

1. Stand tall with feet together.
2. Hop repeatedly on the balls of your feet, using only the ankles.
3. Keep the knees nearly straight — bounce like a pogo stick.
4. Land quietly and rebound instantly. Builds ankle stiffness for running and jumping.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
