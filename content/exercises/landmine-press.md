# Landmine Press

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Chest

## Overview

A beginner barbell press-pattern exercise targeting the front delts. Landmine Press builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Instructions

1. Wedge one end of a barbell into a landmine base.
2. Hold the free end at shoulder height with one or both hands.
3. Press up and slightly forward to full extension.
4. Lower with control. Shoulder-friendly pressing with a natural arc.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
