# Paused Bench Press

An intermediate barbell press-pattern exercise targeting the chest. Paused Bench Press builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Set up for a standard barbell bench press and unrack the bar.
2. Lower the bar to your chest with control.
3. Pause for a full 1-2 seconds with the bar resting lightly on your chest — no bouncing.
4. Press explosively to lockout. The pause kills momentum and builds starting strength.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
