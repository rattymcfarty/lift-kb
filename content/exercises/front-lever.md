# Front Lever

An advanced bodyweight pull-pattern exercise for the lats. Front Lever strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs, Biceps

## Instructions

1. Hang from a pull-up bar with an overhand grip.
2. Engage the lats and pull your body horizontal, keeping the arms straight.
3. Hold your body rigid and parallel to the ground, toes pointed.
4. Start with tuck or single-leg progressions — never sacrifice the straight-body line. Lower with control.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
