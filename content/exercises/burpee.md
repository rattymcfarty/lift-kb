# Burpee

A full-body conditioning move that combines a squat, plank and jump into one fluid rep.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Squat down and place your hands on the floor in front of you.
3. Jump or step your feet back into a high plank.
4. Lower your chest to the floor, then press back up (optional), and bring your feet back toward your hands.
5. Jump up explosively with arms overhead and repeat.

## Cues

- Keep a tight plank
- Jump and land softly
- Smooth rhythm

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag in the plank
- Landing hard on stiff legs
- Rushing until form breaks

## Safety

Step instead of jump if your wrists, knees or back need a lower-impact option.
