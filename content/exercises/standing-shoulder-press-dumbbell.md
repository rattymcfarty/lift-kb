# Standing Shoulder Press (Dumbbell)

A standing dumbbell overhead press that trains shoulders, triceps and core.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Middle Delts, Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Upper Back

## Instructions

1. Hold dumbbells at your shoulders and stand with feet hip-width.
2. Brace your core and squeeze your glutes.
3. Press the dumbbells overhead.
4. Lower slowly to your shoulders.
5. Keep your ribs down.

## Cues

- Glutes squeezed
- Ribs down
- Press straight up

## Common mistakes

- Arching the back
- Pressing in front
- Using leg drive

## Safety

Use a moderate weight; stop with shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
