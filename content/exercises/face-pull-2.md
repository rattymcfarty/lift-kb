# Face Pull

> **Deprecated**, use [Face Pull](face-pull.md).

An intermediate cable pull-pattern exercise for the rear delts. Face Pull strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Upper Back

## Instructions

1. Facing a high pulley with a rope or dual handles attached, pull the weight directly towards your face, separating your hands as you do so.
2. Keep your upper arms parallel to the ground.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
