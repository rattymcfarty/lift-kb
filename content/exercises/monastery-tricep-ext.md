# Monastery Tricep Extension

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

An intermediate cable press-pattern exercise targeting the triceps. Monastery Tricep Extension builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Instructions

1. Kneel facing a cable machine, holding a rope or bar overhead.
2. Extend the elbows to press the weight up and slightly forward.
3. Squeeze the triceps at full extension.
4. Lower slowly behind the head. An overhead cable triceps builder.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
