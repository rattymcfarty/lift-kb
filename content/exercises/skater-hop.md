# Skater Hop

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Adductors

## Overview

A lateral bounding jump that trains single-leg power and balance.

## Instructions

1. Stand on one foot with a slight knee bend.
2. Hop sideways to the other foot.
3. Swing your free leg behind you for balance.
4. Land softly and stick it.
5. Bound back to the other side.

## Cues

- Land softly
- Stick the landing
- Swing arms

## Common mistakes

- Landing stiff
- Knee caving in
- Rushing

## Safety

Skip with knee problems.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
