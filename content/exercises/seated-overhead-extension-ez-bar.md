# Seated Overhead Extension (EZ Bar)

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A seated overhead triceps extension with an EZ bar.

## Instructions

1. Sit on a bench holding an EZ bar overhead with a narrow grip.
2. Keep your upper arms close to your head.
3. Lower the bar behind your head by bending your elbows.
4. Stop at a comfortable stretch.
5. Extend your arms back up.

## Cues

- Elbows pointing up
- Upper arms still
- Full extension

## Common mistakes

- Flaring the elbows
- Arching the back
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a moderate weight and a spotter if needed.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
