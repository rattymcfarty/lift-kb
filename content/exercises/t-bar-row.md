# T-Bar Row

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back, Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

A landmine or T-bar row that builds the upper back and lats.

## Instructions

1. Straddle the bar and hinge at the hips with a flat back.
2. Grip the handle.
3. Pull the bar toward your chest.
4. Squeeze your shoulder blades.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Flat back
- Pull to the chest
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Standing up
- Rounding the back
- Jerking

## Safety

Use a moderate weight.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
