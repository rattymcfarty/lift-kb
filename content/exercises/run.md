# Run

Steady running for cardiovascular fitness.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Instructions

1. Warm up with a brisk walk or easy jog.
2. Settle into a pace you can sustain.
3. Land with your foot under your hips.
4. Keep your arms relaxed.
5. Finish with a cooldown walk.

## Cues

- Relaxed shoulders
- Quick light steps
- Steady breathing

## Common mistakes

- Starting too fast
- Overstriding
- Tight shoulders

## Safety

Increase distance gradually; stop for chest pain or dizziness.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
