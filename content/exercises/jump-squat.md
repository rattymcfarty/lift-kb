# Jump Squat

Jump Squat is an intermediate lower-body exercise that builds the quadriceps, usually performed with just your bodyweight. It trains the deep knee-and-hip bend you use every time you sit, stand, or climb stairs.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Calves

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Dip into a quarter squat, then explode upward, jumping as high as you can.
3. Land softly on the balls of your feet, sinking into the next squat.
4. Keep the landings quiet and the knees tracking over toes. Builds explosive leg power.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
