# Alternating Lunge with Rotation

## Details

- **Category:** Lunge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** lunge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

An intermediate bodyweight lunge-pattern exercise targeting the quadriceps and glutes. Alternating Lunge with Rotation develops single-leg strength and stability that transfers to walking, stairs, and sport.

## Instructions

1. Stand tall and step forward into a lunge, lowering until both knees approach 90 degrees.
2. At the bottom, rotate your torso over the front leg with arms extended.
3. Rotate back to center, then drive through the front heel to stand.
4. Alternate legs, rotating over each front leg.

## Cues

- Torso tall over hips
- Front knee over ankle
- Push through the front heel

## Longevity notes

Single-leg strength is balance insurance — the ability to catch yourself on one leg prevents falls. Train lunges for control, not maximal load.
