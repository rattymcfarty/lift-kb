# Snapdown

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps

## Overview

An intermediate just your bodyweight core exercise for the abs. Snapdown builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Instructions

1. Stand tall with arms overhead.
2. Jump slightly and snap into a tight hollow position — ribs down, legs together, toes pointed.
3. Land in a controlled quarter squat with arms down, absorbing quietly through the legs.
4. Reset fully before each rep; quality over speed.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
