# Treadmill

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

Walking or running on a treadmill for cardio.

## Instructions

1. Step on with feet on the side rails and start the belt slowly.
2. Hold the rails as you step on.
3. Walk or run at a comfortable pace.
4. Keep your posture tall.
5. Cool down gradually.

## Cues

- Tall posture
- Relaxed shoulders
- Light steps

## Common mistakes

- Holding the rails
- Overstriding
- Looking down

## Safety

Use the safety clip; start slowly.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
