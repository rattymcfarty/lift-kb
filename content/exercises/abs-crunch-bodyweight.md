# Abs Crunch (Bodyweight)

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A basic floor crunch that shortens the rectus abdominis through a small, controlled range of motion.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat, hands lightly behind your head or crossed on your chest.
2. Exhale and curl your ribs toward your pelvis, lifting your head and shoulder blades off the floor.
3. Pause for a second at the top without pulling on your neck.
4. Lower slowly until your shoulder blades touch the floor again.
5. Keep your lower back in contact with the floor throughout.

## Cues

- Ribs toward hips
- Chin off the chest, eyes on the ceiling
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Yanking the head forward with the hands
- Using momentum rather than the abs
- Lifting the whole torso like a sit-up

## Safety

Keep the neck neutral. If you feel neck strain, cross your arms over your chest instead.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
