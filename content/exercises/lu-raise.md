# Lu Raise

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Middle Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Rear Delts

## Overview

A barbell raise with a slight lean that targets the front and side deltoids.

## Instructions

1. Stand holding a light barbell or plates in front of your thighs.
2. Raise the bar in an arc up and out until it is overhead.
3. Keep a slight bend in the elbows and your core braced.
4. Pause briefly at the top.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Light weight
- Controlled arc
- Ribs down

## Common mistakes

- Swinging with the back
- Using too much weight
- Shrugging

## Safety

Use a very light load; stop with shoulder pinching.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
