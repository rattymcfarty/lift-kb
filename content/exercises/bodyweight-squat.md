# Bodyweight Squat

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings

## Overview

Bodyweight Squat is a beginner lower-body exercise that builds the quadriceps, usually performed with just your bodyweight. It trains the deep knee-and-hip bend you use every time you sit, stand, or climb stairs.

## Instructions

1. Stand with your feet shoulder width apart. You can place your hands behind your head. This will be your starting position.
2. Begin the movement by flexing your knees and hips, sitting back with your hips.
3. Continue down to full depth if you are able,and quickly reverse the motion until you return to the starting position. As you squat, keep your head and chest up and push your knees out.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
