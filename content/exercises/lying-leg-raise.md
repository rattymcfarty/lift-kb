# Lying Leg Raise

A lying leg raise that trains the lower abs and hip flexors.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back with legs straight and hands under your hips or at your sides.
2. Press your lower back into the floor.
3. Raise your legs until they are vertical.
4. Lower slowly until your feet hover above the floor.
5. Keep your lower back flat.

## Cues

- Lower back flat
- Slow lowering
- Legs long

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back
- Swinging
- Dropping the legs

## Safety

Bend your knees to make it easier; stop if your back arches.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
