# Suitcase Carry March

A single-side loaded carry with a march that trains the obliques and hip stability.

## Details

- **Category:** Carry
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** carry
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes, Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Adductors

## Instructions

1. Hold a heavy dumbbell in one hand at your side.
2. Stand tall.
3. March in place or walk, lifting the knees.
4. Keep your shoulders level.
5. Switch sides.

## Cues

- Stand tall
- Shoulders level
- Slow march

## Common mistakes

- Leaning
- Hiking the shoulder
- Rushing

## Safety

Use a weight you can carry upright.

## Longevity notes

Carrying is the most functional strength there is — groceries, luggage, a grandchild. Loaded carries build grip, posture, and core stability in one movement.
