# Single-Arm Overhead Tricep Extension

A beginner dumbbell press-pattern exercise targeting the triceps. Single-Arm Overhead Tricep Extension builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps

## Instructions

1. Hold a dumbbell overhead with one arm fully extended.
2. Lower the dumbbell behind your head by bending only the elbow.
3. Keep the upper arm vertical and still.
4. Extend back to the top. Stretches the long head under load.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
