# AD Press (Smith Machine)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Middle Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A seated or standing Smith-machine press with the bar in front of the face that emphasises the front and side deltoids on a guided path.

## Instructions

1. Set a bench or stand so the bar starts at about upper-chest height and you can press without leaning back.
2. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder width and unrack it with your ribs down.
3. Press the bar up in the guided track until your arms are fully extended without shrugging.
4. Lower slowly until the bar is near upper-chest level.
5. Re-rack the bar before releasing your grip.

## Cues

- Ribs down, no arching
- Press straight up
- Shoulders away from your ears

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back to push the bar up
- Flaring the elbows far behind the body
- Locking the elbows hard at the top

## Safety

Position yourself so the bar path stays in line with your shoulders; set the safety stops before you start.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
