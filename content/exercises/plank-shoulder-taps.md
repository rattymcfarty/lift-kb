# Plank with Shoulder Taps

An intermediate just your bodyweight core exercise for the abs. Plank with Shoulder Taps builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Start in a high plank, hands under shoulders, feet slightly wider than usual.
2. Brace hard and, without rotating the hips, tap your left shoulder with your right hand.
3. Return, then tap the right shoulder with the left hand.
4. Keep the hips dead still and alternate for reps or time.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
