## Overview

Plank is a beginner core exercise targeting the abs, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. Get into a prone position on the floor, supporting your weight on your toes and your forearms. Your arms are bent and directly below the shoulder.
2. Keep your body straight at all times, and hold this position as long as possible. To increase difficulty, an arm or leg can be raised.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
