# Shadow Kick Boxing

Punching and kicking drills without a target for conditioning.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Instructions

1. Stand in a fighting stance with hands up.
2. Throw punches and add controlled kicks.
3. Rotate hips with each strike.
4. Keep balanced and light.
5. Work in timed rounds.

## Cues

- Hands up
- Balance on the standing leg
- Rotate hips

## Common mistakes

- Kicking too high without mobility
- Losing balance
- Locking joints

## Safety

Warm up hips and hamstrings first.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
