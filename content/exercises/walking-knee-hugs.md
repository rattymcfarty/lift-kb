# Walking Knee Hugs

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps

## Overview

Walking Knee Hugs is a beginner mobility exercise for the glutes, performed with just your bodyweight. Regular mobility work keeps joints moving freely and makes strength training safer.

## Instructions

1. Walk forward; with each step, pull one knee to your chest with both hands.
2. Stand tall on the supporting leg, squeezing the glute.
3. Hold the hug for a second, feeling the glute and hip open up.
4. Step through and alternate legs as you walk.

## Cues

- Move slowly, no forcing
- Breathe into the stretch
- Stay relaxed outside the target area

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
