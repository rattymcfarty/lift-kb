# Hands on Hips Countermovement Jump

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Calves
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings

## Overview

A jump that begins with a quick dip, with the hands on the hips so power comes from the legs.

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart and your hands on your hips.
2. Quickly dip into a quarter squat.
3. Immediately jump as high as you can.
4. Land softly on the balls of your feet, bending your knees to absorb the impact.
5. Reset and repeat.

## Cues

- Dip and explode
- Quiet landing
- Hands stay on hips

## Common mistakes

- Landing stiff-legged
- Letting the knees cave in
- Jumping from a deep squat

## Safety

Do it on a firm, non-slip surface; skip if you have knee or ankle injuries.
