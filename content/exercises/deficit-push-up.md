# Deficit Push-Up

Deficit Push-Up is an intermediate pushing exercise for the chest, performed with just your bodyweight. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Place your hands on two low platforms or dumbbells, slightly wider than shoulders.
2. Assume a plank position with a straight line from head to heels.
3. Lower your chest below hand level, getting a deep stretch.
4. Press back up to full arm extension. The deficit increases range of motion.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
