# Batwing Row

A chest-supported row done with the elbows flared that targets the rear delts and upper back.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts

## Instructions

1. Lie chest-down on an incline bench set to about 30 degrees, holding a dumbbell in each hand with arms hanging.
2. Pull the dumbbells toward your ribs, leading with your elbows and squeezing your shoulder blades together.
3. Pause when your elbows are just past your torso.
4. Lower the weights slowly until your arms are straight.
5. Keep your head and chest against the bench.

## Cues

- Lead with the elbows
- Squeeze shoulder blades
- Chest stays on the pad

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging the shoulders
- Jerking the weights up
- Lifting the chest off the bench

## Safety

Pick a light-to-moderate load; stop if you feel shoulder pinching.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
