# Fly Press (Dumbbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Overview

A hybrid dumbbell movement that begins as a fly to stretch the chest and finishes as a press.

## Instructions

1. Lie on a flat bench holding dumbbells over your chest with a slight bend in your elbows.
2. Open your arms wide in a fly until you feel a chest stretch.
3. As you bring the dumbbells back, rotate into a press by bending the elbows and driving them together.
4. Finish with the dumbbells locked out over your chest.
5. Keep your shoulder blades pinned back.

## Cues

- Fly down, press up
- Keep the chest tall
- Squeeze at the top

## Common mistakes

- Dropping too low
- Losing shoulder-blade position
- Using too heavy dumbbells

## Safety

Use light-to-moderate weights; stop at the stretch.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
