# Y Raise

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts, Upper Back

## Overview

A Y-shaped raise that strengthens the lower traps and rear delts.

## Instructions

1. Lie chest-down on an incline bench holding light dumbbells.
2. Raise your arms overhead in a Y with thumbs up.
3. Squeeze your shoulder blades.
4. Pause.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Thumbs up
- Squeeze shoulder blades
- Light weight

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging
- Using momentum
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use very light weights.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
