# Tricep Extension (Cable)

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A cable triceps extension for the long head.

## Instructions

1. Attach a rope or bar to a pulley.
2. Keep your elbows pinned.
3. Extend your arms.
4. Squeeze your triceps.
5. Return slowly.

## Cues

- Elbows pinned
- Squeeze triceps
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Flaring the elbows
- Using the body
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a moderate load.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
