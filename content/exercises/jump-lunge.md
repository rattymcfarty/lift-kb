# Jump Lunge

Jump Lunge is an intermediate single-leg exercise for the quadriceps, performed with just your bodyweight. Training one leg at a time builds balance and evens out side-to-side differences.

## Details

- **Category:** Lunge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** lunge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Calves

## Instructions

1. Start in a lunge with the front knee at 90 degrees.
2. Explode upward, switching legs mid-air.
3. Land softly in the opposite lunge, absorbing through both legs.
4. Keep the torso tall and alternate continuously for reps or time.

## Cues

- Torso tall over hips
- Front knee over ankle
- Push through the front heel

## Longevity notes

Single-leg strength is balance insurance — the ability to catch yourself on one leg prevents falls. Train lunges for control, not maximal load.
