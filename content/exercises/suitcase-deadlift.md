# Suitcase Deadlift

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Abs

## Overview

An intermediate barbell hinge-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Suitcase Deadlift builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Instructions

1. Stand beside a loaded barbell as if holding a suitcase.
2. Squat down and grip the bar with one hand, the other arm out for balance.
3. Drive through the legs to stand, resisting the urge to lean.
4. Lower with control. Brutal on the obliques and grip.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
