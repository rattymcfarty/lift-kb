# Chest Supported Lateral Raise

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Middle Delts, Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Rear Delts

## Overview

A lateral raise performed chest-down on an incline bench to remove momentum and isolate the side delts.

## Instructions

1. Lie chest-down on an incline bench set to about 30 degrees, holding a dumbbell in each hand with arms hanging.
2. With a slight bend in your elbows, raise the dumbbells out to your sides.
3. Lift until your arms are roughly in line with your torso.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Lower slowly back down.

## Cues

- Lead with the elbows
- Slow and controlled
- Chest on the pad

## Common mistakes

- Using momentum
- Shrugging
- Lifting too high

## Safety

Use light weights; stop if you feel pinching at the top of the shoulder.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
