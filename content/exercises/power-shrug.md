# Power Shrug

An explosive shrug from the hang position that trains the traps and the power pull.

## Details

- **Category:** Olympic
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back

## Instructions

1. Stand holding a barbell at mid-thigh with an overhand grip.
2. Dip slightly and extend your hips explosively.
3. Shrug your shoulders up toward your ears as the bar rises.
4. Allow your elbows to bend slightly at the top.
5. Lower the bar under control.

## Cues

- Jump tall
- Shrug high
- Bar close to the body

## Common mistakes

- Pulling with the arms
- Leaning back
- Using too heavy a load

## Safety

Use moderate weight; avoid with neck or shoulder problems.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
