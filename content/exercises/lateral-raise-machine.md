# Lateral Raise (Machine)

A beginner machine press-pattern exercise targeting the middle delts. Lateral Raise (Machine) builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Middle Delts

## Instructions

1. Adjust the machine seat so the pivot aligns with your shoulders.
2. Grip the handles or place forearms on the pads, arms slightly bent.
3. Raise your arms out to the sides until they're parallel to the floor.
4. Lower slowly. The machine keeps constant tension on the side delts.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
