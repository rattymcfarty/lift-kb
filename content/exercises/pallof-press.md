# Pallof Press

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

An anti-rotation core exercise where you press a cable away from your chest without letting your torso twist.

## Instructions

1. Set a cable at chest height and stand sideways to the machine, holding the handle at your chest with both hands.
2. Step away to create tension and stand with feet shoulder-width apart.
3. Brace your core and press the handle straight out in front of you.
4. Hold for a second without rotating.
5. Bring the handle back to your chest.

## Cues

- Resist the rotation
- Ribs down
- Slow press

## Common mistakes

- Letting the torso twist
- Shrugging
- Leaning away

## Safety

Use a moderate load; keep your spine neutral.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
