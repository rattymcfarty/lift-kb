# Ring Push-Up

Ring Push-Up is an advanced pushing exercise for the chest, performed with minimal equipment. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Set gymnastics rings to a low height and grip them in a push-up position.
2. Stabilize the rings — they'll want to shake — then lower your chest between them.
3. Keep the elbows at 45 degrees and the body rigid.
4. Press back up, squeezing the rings together at the top. Demanding on the stabilizers.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
