# Incline Chest Press (Machine)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A machine press at an incline that targets the upper chest, shoulders and triceps.

## Instructions

1. Adjust the seat so the handles are at upper-chest height and sit with your back against the pad.
2. Grip the handles and set your shoulder blades back.
3. Press the handles up and forward until your arms are almost extended.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Return slowly under control.

## Cues

- Shoulder blades pinned
- Press up and out
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging
- Locking the elbows
- Letting the weights drop

## Safety

Set the seat correctly; avoid pressing through shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
