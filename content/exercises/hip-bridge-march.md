# Hip Bridge March

Hip Bridge March is a beginner hip-hinge exercise targeting the glutes, performed with just your bodyweight. The hinge is the foundation of safe lifting — it teaches you to load the hips, not the spine.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs, Hamstrings

## Instructions

1. Lift into a glute bridge with hips fully extended.
2. Keeping the hips level, lift one foot a few inches off the floor.
3. Hold briefly, then set it down and lift the other foot.
4. Alternate like a slow march. Don't let the hips drop or rotate.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
