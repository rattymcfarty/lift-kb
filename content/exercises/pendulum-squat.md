# Pendulum Squat

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Overview

An intermediate machine squat-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Pendulum Squat develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Instructions

1. Stand on the pendulum squat machine with back against the pad, feet forward.
2. Unrack and lower into a deep squat, knees tracking over toes.
3. The machine's arc keeps the load on the quads with minimal back stress.
4. Drive up to full extension. A quad-dominant squat variation.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
