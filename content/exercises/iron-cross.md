# Iron Cross

Iron Cross is an intermediate pushing exercise for the front delts, performed with dumbbells. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest, Glutes, Hamstrings, Lower Back

## Instructions

1. Perform the movement with control through a full range of motion.
2. Repeat for the prescribed number of reps, resting as needed between sets.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
