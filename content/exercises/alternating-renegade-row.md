# Alternating Renegade Row

Alternating Renegade Row is an advanced pulling exercise for the upper back, performed with a kettlebell. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs, Biceps, Chest, Lats

## Instructions

1. Place two kettlebells on the floor about shoulder width apart. Position yourself on your toes and your hands as though you were doing a pushup, with the body straight and extended. Use the handles of the kettlebells to support your upper body. You may need to position your feet wide for support.
2. Push one kettlebell into the floor and row the other kettlebell, retracting the shoulder blade of the working side as you flex the elbow, pulling it to your side.
3. Then lower the kettlebell to the floor and begin the kettlebell in the opposite hand. Repeat for several reps.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
