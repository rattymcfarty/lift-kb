# Pull-Up (Assisted)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps, Upper Back

## Overview

Pull-Up (Assisted) is a beginner pulling exercise for the lats, performed with a machine. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Instructions

1. Set up at the assisted pull-up machine or loop a band over the bar.
2. Hang with arms fully extended, using the assistance.
3. Pull your chest to the bar, driving the elbows down.
4. Lower slowly to full extension. Reduce assistance as you get stronger.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
