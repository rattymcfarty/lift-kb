# Standing Behind Neck Shoulder Press (Barbell)

A standing barbell press from behind the neck for the shoulders.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Middle Delts, Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Upper Back

## Instructions

1. Hold a barbell on your upper back with a wide grip.
2. Brace your core.
3. Press the bar overhead.
4. Lower to ear level.
5. Re-rack carefully.

## Cues

- Stop at ear level
- Ribs down
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Lowering past the ears
- Arching the back
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Requires shoulder mobility; use light weights and skip with impingement.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
