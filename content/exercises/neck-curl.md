# Neck Curl

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Neck

## Overview

A gentle neck-flexion exercise that strengthens the muscles at the front of the neck.

## Instructions

1. Lie face up on a bench with your head just off the end, or sit and place your hand on your forehead for light resistance.
2. Keep your shoulders relaxed.
3. Slowly tuck your chin toward your chest against the light resistance.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Lower slowly back to neutral.

## Cues

- Very light resistance
- Slow and smooth
- Shoulders relaxed

## Common mistakes

- Using too much load
- Jerking
- Holding the breath

## Safety

Neck exercises need very light loads; stop with any dizziness, tingling or pain.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
