# Seated Shoulder Press (Dumbbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Middle Delts, Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

A seated dumbbell shoulder press that builds the delts and triceps.

## Instructions

1. Sit on a bench with back support holding dumbbells at shoulder height.
2. Brace your core.
3. Press the dumbbells up until your arms are extended.
4. Lower slowly.
5. Keep your wrists stacked over elbows.

## Cues

- Ribs down
- Press up and slightly in
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Arching the back
- Using momentum
- Flaring too wide

## Safety

Use a moderate weight and set the dumbbells down safely.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
