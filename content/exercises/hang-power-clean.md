# Hang Power Clean

## Details

- **Category:** Olympic
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Lower Back

## Overview

An Olympic lift where you pull the bar from the hang position and catch it at shoulder height in a partial squat.

## Instructions

1. Stand holding a barbell at mid-thigh with an overhand grip, hips pushed back and torso slightly forward.
2. Jump powerfully by extending your hips, knees and ankles.
3. Shrug and pull your elbows up and out as the bar rises.
4. Drop quickly under the bar and catch it on your front shoulders in a quarter squat.
5. Stand tall, then lower the bar to the hang position with control.

## Cues

- Jump tall
- Elbows high and fast
- Catch with a soft knee bend

## Common mistakes

- Pulling with the arms too early
- Letting the bar swing away
- Catching with the wrists bent back

## Safety

Learn from a coach and use bumper plates; start with a very light load.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
