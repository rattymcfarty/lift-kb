# Military Press (Barbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Middle Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Upper Back

## Overview

A strict standing barbell overhead press without leg drive, building shoulder and triceps strength.

## Instructions

1. Hold a barbell at your upper chest with an overhand grip just outside shoulder width, feet together or hip-width.
2. Squeeze your glutes and brace your core.
3. Press the bar straight up, moving your head back slightly then forward.
4. Lock out overhead with the bar over your mid-foot.
5. Lower the bar under control to your chest.

## Cues

- Glutes squeezed
- Ribs down
- Bar over the mid-foot

## Common mistakes

- Leaning back
- Using the legs
- Flaring the ribs

## Safety

Use a lighter load than a push press; stop with shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
