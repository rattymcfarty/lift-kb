# Bench T-Spine Stretch

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

A kneeling stretch that opens the upper back and shoulders by sitting your hips back while your arms rest on a bench.

## Instructions

1. Kneel in front of a bench and place both elbows on top, palms together or holding a light dowel.
2. Sit your hips back toward your heels while keeping your arms on the bench.
3. Let your chest sink toward the floor until you feel a stretch through your upper back.
4. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds while breathing slowly.
5. Return to the start and repeat.

## Cues

- Hips back, chest down
- Breathe slowly
- Relax the neck

## Common mistakes

- Overarching the lower back
- Holding your breath
- Forcing the stretch with a bounce

## Safety

Move into a gentle stretch only; ease off if you feel pinching in the shoulders.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
