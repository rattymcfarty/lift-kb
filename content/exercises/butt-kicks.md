# Butt Kicks

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps, Calves

## Overview

Butt Kicks is a beginner core exercise targeting the hamstrings, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. Stand tall with feet hip-width apart.
2. Jog in place, kicking your heels up toward your glutes on each step.
3. Stay on the balls of your feet with a quick, light cadence.
4. Keep the torso upright and pump your arms. A classic dynamic warm-up drill.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
