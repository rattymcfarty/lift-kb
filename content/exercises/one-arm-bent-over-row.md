# One-Arm Bent Over Row

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Upper Back, Biceps

## Overview

A beginner dumbbell pull-pattern exercise for the lats. One-Arm Bent Over Row strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Hinge forward with a flat back, bracing one hand on a bench.
2. Row the dumbbell to your hip, driving the elbow toward the ceiling.
3. Squeeze the back at the top for a full second.
4. Lower slowly. Unilateral rows build thickness and fix imbalances.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
