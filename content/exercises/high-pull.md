# High Pull

## Details

- **Category:** Olympic
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back, Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Middle Delts, Front Delts

## Overview

A power movement where you pull a barbell explosively to chest height with the elbows high.

## Instructions

1. Stand with a barbell at mid-thigh in a hang position, hips back and chest up.
2. Extend your hips, knees and ankles explosively.
3. Shrug your shoulders and pull the bar up with your elbows leading, high and outside.
4. Bring the bar to about chest height.
5. Lower the bar under control back to the hang position.

## Cues

- Jump tall
- Elbows high
- Bar close to the body

## Common mistakes

- Pulling with the arms before the legs
- Swinging the bar away
- Leaning back

## Safety

Use a light-to-moderate load; avoid if you have shoulder or wrist problems.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
