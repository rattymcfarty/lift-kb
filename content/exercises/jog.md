# Jog

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

A relaxed steady-state run that builds aerobic base fitness.

## Instructions

1. Warm up with a few minutes of brisk walking.
2. Start jogging at a pace where you can hold a conversation.
3. Land softly under your hips with a quick cadence.
4. Keep your shoulders relaxed and arms swinging naturally.
5. Finish with a cooldown walk.

## Cues

- Conversational pace
- Light quick steps
- Relaxed shoulders

## Common mistakes

- Starting too fast
- Overstriding
- Tensing the shoulders

## Safety

Build volume gradually; stop for chest pain or dizziness.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
