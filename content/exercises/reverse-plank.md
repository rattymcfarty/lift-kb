# Reverse Plank

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A supported hold facing the ceiling that trains the glutes, hamstrings and back.

## Instructions

1. Sit with your legs extended and your hands behind you, fingers pointing toward your feet.
2. Press through your hands and heels to lift your hips.
3. Create a straight line from head to heels.
4. Hold the position.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Straight line
- Squeeze glutes
- Chest open

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag
- Shrugging
- Hyperextending the neck

## Safety

Stop with wrist or shoulder discomfort.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
