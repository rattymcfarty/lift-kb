# Kneeling Single-Arm High Pulley Row

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps, Upper Back

## Overview

A beginner cable pull-pattern exercise for the lats. Kneeling Single-Arm High Pulley Row strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Attach a single handle to a high pulley and make your weight selection.
2. Kneel in front of the cable tower, taking the cable with one hand with your arm extended. This will be your starting position.
3. Starting with your palm facing forward, pull the weight down to your torso by flexing the elbow and retract the shoulder blade. As you do so, rotate the wrist so that at the completion of the movement, your palm is now facing you.
4. After a brief pause, return to the starting position.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
