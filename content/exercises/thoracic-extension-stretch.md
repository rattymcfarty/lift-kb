# Thoracic Extension Stretch

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back

## Overview

A beginner just your bodyweight mobility drill targeting the upper back. Thoracic Extension Stretch restores range of motion that desk life and hard training both tend to steal.

## Instructions

1. Sit or kneel and place your mid-back over a foam roller or bench edge.
2. Support your head with your hands.
3. Extend backward over the roller, opening the chest — ribs down, movement from the mid-back.
4. Work different segments of the mid-back and repeat.

## Cues

- Move slowly, no forcing
- Breathe into the stretch
- Stay relaxed outside the target area

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
