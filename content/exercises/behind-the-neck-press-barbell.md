# Behind-the-Neck Press (Barbell)

A barbell overhead press with the bar lowered behind the head. It targets the shoulders and triceps but demands excellent shoulder mobility.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts, Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Middle Delts, Rear Delts

## Instructions

1. Sit or stand with a barbell resting on your upper back and hold it with a wide overhand grip.
2. Brace your core and keep your head slightly forward of the bar path.
3. Press the bar straight up until your arms are fully extended overhead.
4. Lower the bar slowly to about ear level, no lower.
5. Re-rack the bar carefully when finished.

## Cues

- Stop at ear level
- Ribs down
- Slow, controlled path

## Common mistakes

- Lowering the bar to the neck or shoulders
- Arching the lower back
- Using a weight that forces a push from the legs

## Safety

This lift stresses the shoulders and is not for everyone; use a light load, a spotter, and skip it if you have shoulder impingement.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
