# Decline Push-Up

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts, Triceps

## Overview

Decline Push-Up is a beginner pushing exercise for the chest, performed with minimal equipment. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Instructions

1. Lie on the floor face down and place your hands about 36 inches apart while holding your torso up at arms length. Move your feet up to a box or bench. This will be your starting position.
2. Next, lower yourself downward until your chest almost touches the floor as you inhale.
3. Now breathe out and press your upper body back up to the starting position while squeezing your chest.
4. After a brief pause at the top contracted position, you can begin to lower yourself downward again for as many repetitions as needed.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
