# Bear Crawl

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Full-Body
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps, Front Delts

## Overview

An intermediate just your bodyweight core exercise for the full-body. Bear Crawl builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Instructions

1. Start on all fours with knees hovering an inch off the floor, back flat.
2. Crawl forward by moving opposite hand and foot together — right hand with left foot.
3. Keep hips low and level; don't let them rock side to side.
4. Crawl for distance or time, then rest. A full-body coordination and core builder.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
