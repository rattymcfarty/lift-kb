# Leg Press (45 Degrees)

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Abductors

## Overview

A machine leg press on a 45-degree sled that trains the quads, glutes and hamstrings with back support.

## Instructions

1. Sit in the seat with your back flat against the pad and your feet shoulder-width on the platform.
2. Unlock the safety handles and extend your legs without locking your knees.
3. Lower the sled slowly by bending your knees until they are at about 90 degrees or your lower back is about to lift.
4. Press the platform away through your whole foot.
5. Re-lock the safeties when done.

## Cues

- Back stays flat
- Knees track over toes
- Don't lock the knees

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips curl up off the pad
- Locking the knees hard
- Bouncing at the bottom

## Safety

Set safeties; never go so deep that your lower back lifts from the pad.

## Longevity notes

Squatting is the foundation of getting up, sitting down, and climbing stairs. Keeping leg strength and knee range preserves independence decades longer than any single other movement.
