# Pec Deck (Machine)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Overview

A machine fly that isolates the chest by bringing the arms together in front of you.

## Instructions

1. Adjust the seat so the handles are at chest height and sit with your back against the pad.
2. Grip the handles with a slight bend in your elbows.
3. Bring your arms together in front of your chest.
4. Squeeze your chest at the centre.
5. Return slowly until you feel a stretch.

## Cues

- Slight elbow bend
- Squeeze at the centre
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Letting the shoulders roll forward
- Using momentum
- Going back too far

## Safety

Set the range so the stretch is comfortable; avoid over-stretching the shoulders.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
