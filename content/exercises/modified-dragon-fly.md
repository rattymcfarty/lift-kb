# Modified Dragon Fly

A regressed dragon fly with bent knees that builds the core strength for the full movement.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Lie on a bench and hold the bench behind your head.
2. Lift your hips and bend your knees.
3. Slowly lower your bent legs as far as you can control.
4. Keep your lower back from arching.
5. Raise back up using your abs.

## Cues

- Knees bent
- Slow control
- Abs braced

## Common mistakes

- Arching the back
- Using momentum
- Going too low too soon

## Safety

Use the bent-knee version until you can control the full movement.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
