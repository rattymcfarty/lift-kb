# Alternating Lateral Ball Slams

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Abs, Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts, Quadriceps

## Overview

An explosive core and shoulder exercise that slams a medicine ball diagonally down to one side then the other.

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart holding a slam ball overhead with both hands.
2. Rotate slightly and slam the ball down hard on one side of your feet, bending your knees and hinging your hips.
3. Catch or pick the ball up under control and raise it overhead again.
4. Slam to the opposite side on the next rep, alternating.
5. Keep your core braced throughout.

## Cues

- Hips and knees help the slam
- Abs braced on every rep
- Alternate sides evenly

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the lower back to pick up the ball
- Using only the arms
- Standing too close so the ball bounces up into you

## Safety

Use a dead-weight slam ball, not a bouncy ball, and clear the space around you.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
