# Glute Bridge Walkout

An intermediate bodyweight hinge-pattern exercise for the glutes. Glute Bridge Walkout builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Calves

## Instructions

1. Start in a glute bridge with hips fully extended.
2. Keeping the hips lifted, take small steps outward with alternating feet.
3. Walk out as far as you can while keeping the hips level — no sagging.
4. Walk the feet back in. The further you walk, the harder the hamstrings work.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
