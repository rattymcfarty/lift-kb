# Platz Squat

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Adductors, Abs

## Overview

A quad-focused squat with the feet close and heels elevated, named after a bodybuilder's style.

## Instructions

1. Place the bar on your back with your feet close and heels elevated on plates.
2. Brace your core and keep your torso upright.
3. Squat down deep, with your knees travelling far forward.
4. Keep your whole foot on the plates.
5. Drive up through your feet to stand.

## Cues

- Torso upright
- Knees forward
- Control the depth

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the back
- Losing control at the bottom
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use light loads; avoid with knee pain.

## Longevity notes

Squatting is the foundation of getting up, sitting down, and climbing stairs. Keeping leg strength and knee range preserves independence decades longer than any single other movement.
