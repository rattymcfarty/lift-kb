# Behind-the-Neck Lat Pulldown

A lat pulldown variation that brings the bar behind the head. It demands good shoulder mobility and is not suitable for everyone.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back

## Instructions

1. Sit at a lat pulldown station with your thighs secured under the pads and hold the bar with a wide overhand grip.
2. Lean your torso very slightly forward and keep your head tipped forward.
3. Pull the bar down behind your head to about ear level, driving your elbows toward the floor.
4. Squeeze your lats at the bottom, then let the bar rise under control.
5. Stop immediately if you feel shoulder or neck discomfort.

## Cues

- Chin tucked, torso slightly forward
- Elbows down and back
- Stop at ear level

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the bar below the neck
- Jerking the bar with momentum
- Leaning back and using body swing

## Safety

This variation stresses the shoulder joint; use a light load and skip it if you lack overhead mobility. The front-pulldown is a safer alternative.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
