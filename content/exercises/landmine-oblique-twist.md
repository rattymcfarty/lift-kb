# Landmine Oblique Twist

An intermediate a barbell core exercise for the abs. Landmine Oblique Twist builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Wedge one end of a barbell into a landmine base or a corner.
2. Hold the free end with both hands at chest height.
3. Rotate the bar down to one hip, pivoting the feet, arms fairly straight.
4. Drive it back up and across to the other hip. The trunk does the work, not the arms.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
