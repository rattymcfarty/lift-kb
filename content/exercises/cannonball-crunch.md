# Cannonball Crunch

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

Cannonball Crunch is a beginner core exercise targeting the abs, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. Sit on the floor hugging your knees to your chest, arms wrapped around shins.
2. Roll back slightly, then crunch forward, tightening the abs hard at the top.
3. Keep the knees tucked throughout — like a cannonball.
4. Control the tempo; avoid yanking with the arms.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
