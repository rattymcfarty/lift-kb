# Lat Pulldown (Neutral Grip)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

A lat pulldown with a neutral (palms facing each other) grip that is often more comfortable on the shoulders.

## Instructions

1. Sit at the pulldown station with your thighs secured and hold the neutral grip handle with arms extended.
2. Lean back slightly and keep your chest tall.
3. Pull the handle down to your upper chest, driving your elbows toward your ribs.
4. Squeeze your lats at the bottom.
5. Let the handle rise slowly until your arms are straight.

## Cues

- Chest tall
- Elbows to ribs
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Leaning back too far
- Pulling with the arms only
- Shrugging

## Safety

Use a moderate load; avoid jerking the bar down.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
