# Pec Fly (Dumbbell)

A dumbbell chest fly that stretches and squeezes the chest.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Lie on a flat bench holding dumbbells over your chest, palms facing each other.
2. Keep a slight bend in your elbows.
3. Open your arms out to the sides until you feel a stretch.
4. Squeeze your chest to bring the dumbbells back together.
5. Keep your shoulder blades pinned.

## Cues

- Slight elbow bend
- Open wide
- Squeeze together

## Common mistakes

- Pressing instead
- Dropping too low
- Too much weight

## Safety

Use a moderate weight; stop at the stretch.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
