# Ring Dips

An intermediate minimal-equipment press-pattern exercise targeting the triceps. Ring Dips builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Grip a ring in each hand, and then take a small jump to help you get into the starting position with your arms locked out.
2. Begin by flexing the elbow, lowering your body until your arms break 90 degrees. Avoid swinging, and maintain good posture throughout the descent.
3. Reverse the motion by extending the elbow, pushing yourself back up into the starting position.
4. Repeat for the desired number of repetitions.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
