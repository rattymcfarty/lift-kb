# Split Squat with Dumbbells

> **Deprecated**, use [Split Squat with Dumbbells](split-squat-with-dumbbells.md).

A beginner dumbbell squat-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Split Squat with Dumbbells develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings

## Instructions

1. Position yourself into a staggered stance with the rear foot elevated and front foot forward.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand, letting them hang at the sides. This will be your starting position.
3. Begin by descending, flexing your knee and hip to lower your body down. Maintain good posture throughout the movement. Keep the front knee in line with the foot as you perform the exercise.
4. At the bottom of the movement, drive through the heel to extend the knee and hip to return to the starting position.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
