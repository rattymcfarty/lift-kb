# World's Greatest Stretch

A dynamic lunge-based stretch for the hips, hamstrings, thoracic spine and shoulders.

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Instructions

1. Step into a deep lunge.
2. Place both hands inside your front foot.
3. Rotate your torso and reach one arm to the ceiling.
4. Return and straighten the front leg to stretch the hamstring.
5. Switch sides.

## Cues

- Open the chest
- Slow breathing
- Hips low

## Common mistakes

- Rushing
- Letting the knee cave
- Rounding the back

## Safety

Move gently; ease off with groin tightness.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
