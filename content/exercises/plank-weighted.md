# Plank (Weighted)

A plank with added load for advanced core stability.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Get into a forearm plank with elbows under shoulders and a straight body.
2. Have a partner place a plate on your upper back, or carefully place it there.
3. Brace your abs and squeeze your glutes.
4. Hold the position without letting your hips sag.
5. Set the weight down at the end.

## Cues

- Straight line
- Glutes squeezed
- Breathe steadily

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag
- Holding your breath
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a modest weight and a spotter; stop if your lower back sags.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
