# Floor Chest Fly (Dumbbell)

A dumbbell fly performed on the floor, which limits the stretch by stopping the elbows at the floor.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back on the floor with knees bent, holding a dumbbell in each hand over your chest.
2. Keep a slight bend in your elbows and open your arms out to the sides.
3. Stop when your upper arms touch the floor.
4. Squeeze your chest to bring the dumbbells back together over your chest.
5. Keep your shoulder blades pulled back.

## Cues

- Slight elbow bend
- Triceps touch the floor
- Squeeze at the top

## Common mistakes

- Bending the elbows and pressing
- Dropping heavy dumbbells
- Using too much weight

## Safety

The floor limits the range, which is gentle on the shoulders; still choose a weight you can control.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
