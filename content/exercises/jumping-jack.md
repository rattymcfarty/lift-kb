# Jumping Jack

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

A classic full-body cardio exercise performed in place.

## Instructions

1. Stand with your feet together and arms at your sides.
2. Jump your feet out wide while raising your arms overhead.
3. Jump back to the start.
4. Keep your landings soft.
5. Continue at a steady rhythm.

## Cues

- Soft landings
- Steady rhythm
- Tall posture

## Common mistakes

- Landing hard
- Letting the knees cave
- Slouching forward

## Safety

Use the modified step version if you have joint issues.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
