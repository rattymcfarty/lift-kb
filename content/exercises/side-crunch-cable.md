# Side Crunch (Cable)

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A cable side crunch that trains the obliques with resistance.

## Instructions

1. Attach a rope to a high pulley and kneel sideways, holding the rope near your head.
2. Brace your core.
3. Crunch sideways, bringing your ribs toward your hip.
4. Squeeze your oblique.
5. Return slowly.

## Cues

- Bend sideways
- Hips stay still
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the torso
- Using the arms
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a moderate load.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
