# Windshield Wipers

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

An advanced just your bodyweight core exercise for the abs. Windshield Wipers builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Instructions

1. Hang from a bar or lie on the floor with arms out, legs raised vertical.
2. Keeping the legs straight, rotate them to one side.
3. Lower as far as you can control, then sweep to the other side.
4. Move like a windshield wiper. Elite oblique and core control.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
