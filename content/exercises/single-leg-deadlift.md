# Single-Leg Deadlift

An intermediate bodyweight hinge-pattern exercise for the hamstrings. Single-Leg Deadlift builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Lower Back

## Instructions

1. Stand on one leg with a soft knee, arms at your sides.
2. Hinge at the hips, extending the free leg straight behind you as your torso lowers.
3. Reach toward the floor, keeping the hips square and the back flat.
4. Drive the standing heel into the floor to return. Add a dumbbell or kettlebell when bodyweight is easy.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
