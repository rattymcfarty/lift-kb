# Spoto Press

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Front Delts

## Overview

An advanced barbell press-pattern exercise targeting the chest. Spoto Press builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Instructions

1. Set up for a bench press and unrack the bar.
2. Lower the bar to just above your chest — hover 1-2 inches off, don't touch.
3. Pause briefly in the hover, staying tight.
4. Press to lockout. Named for Eric Spoto; builds control off the chest.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
