# Leg Raise (Captain's Chair)

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A straight-leg raise in a captain's chair that trains the lower abs and hip flexors.

## Instructions

1. Support yourself on the arm rests with your back against the pad.
2. Let your legs hang straight.
3. Raise your legs, keeping them straight, until they are parallel to the floor or higher.
4. Pause.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Curl the pelvis
- Keep the legs long
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Swinging
- Arching the lower back
- Dropping the legs

## Safety

Bend your knees to make it easier; avoid if you have lower-back pain.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
