# Pull-Up (Weighted)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps, Upper Back

## Overview

Pull-Up (Weighted) is an advanced pulling exercise for the lats, performed with just your bodyweight. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Instructions

1. Attach weight with a dip belt or hold a dumbbell between your feet.
2. Hang from the bar with arms fully extended.
3. Pull until your chin clears the bar, keeping the core tight.
4. Lower with control. The gold standard for relative upper-body strength.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
