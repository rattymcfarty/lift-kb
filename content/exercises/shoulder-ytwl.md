# Shoulder YTWL

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Middle Delts, Upper Back

## Overview

A beginner bodyweight pull-pattern exercise for the rear delts. Shoulder YTWL strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Lie face-down on an incline bench or the floor with arms hanging.
2. Raise your arms into a Y shape, thumbs up, squeezing the shoulder blades.
3. Lower, then raise into a T shape out to the sides.
4. Lower, then bend elbows into a W, then straighten into an L. A rotator-cuff health series.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
