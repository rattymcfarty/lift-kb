# Suitcase Carry

A beginner loaded carry with dumbbells for the abs. Suitcase Carry builds the grip, core, and postural strength used every time you haul groceries or luggage.

## Details

- **Category:** Carry
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** carry
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Forearms, Glutes, Quadriceps

## Instructions

1. Hold a heavy dumbbell in one hand at your side.
2. Stand tall — resist leaning away from the weight.
3. Brace your core hard, especially the obliques on the loaded side.
4. Walk slowly with controlled steps for the prescribed distance, then switch hands.

## Cues

- Tall posture, ribs down
- Grip hard, breathe behind the brace
- Short, quick steps

## Longevity notes

Grip strength and loaded walking correlate strongly with longevity. Carries are simple, safe, and brutally effective — do them often.
