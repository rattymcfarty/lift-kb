# Bayesian Curl

An intermediate cable pull-pattern exercise for the biceps. Bayesian Curl strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Forearms

## Instructions

1. Set a cable pulley to the lowest position and stand facing away, holding the handle behind you.
2. Step forward so the cable pulls your arm slightly behind your torso — this lengthens the biceps.
3. Curl the handle up toward your shoulder, keeping the upper arm still.
4. Lower slowly to full extension. The behind-body angle keeps tension on the long head.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
