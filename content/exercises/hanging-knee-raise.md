# Hanging Knee Raise

A hanging core exercise where you lift your knees toward your chest.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Hang from a pull-up bar with an overhand grip, arms straight.
2. Pull your shoulder blades down slightly to engage your lats.
3. Raise your knees toward your chest by curling your pelvis up.
4. Pause briefly at the top.
5. Lower slowly without swinging.

## Cues

- Curl the pelvis
- No swinging
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Swinging with momentum
- Using only the hip flexors
- Letting the shoulders shrug up

## Safety

Stop if you feel shoulder pain; use straps or an assisted option if your grip fails first.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
