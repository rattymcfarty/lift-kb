# Chest Supported Row (Barbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back, Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

A barbell row done lying chest-down on a bench, which takes the lower back out of the exercise and isolates the upper back.

## Instructions

1. Lie chest-down on an incline bench with a barbell on the floor below you and grab it with an overhand grip.
2. Let your arms hang straight with the bar just off the floor.
3. Pull the bar toward your lower chest, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
4. Pause with the bar at the bench.
5. Lower slowly until your arms are straight.

## Cues

- Pull to the lower chest
- Squeeze the shoulder blades
- Chest on the pad

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the chest off the bench
- Jerking the bar
- Shrugging

## Safety

Use a bench with a stable pad; ensure the bar can clear the floor.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
