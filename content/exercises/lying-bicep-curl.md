# Lying Bicep Curl

Lying Bicep Curl is a beginner pulling exercise for the biceps, performed with dumbbells. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps

## Instructions

1. Lie on a flat bench holding dumbbells with arms extended toward the ceiling.
2. Curl the weights toward your shoulders, keeping the upper arms vertical.
3. Squeeze the biceps hard at the top.
4. Lower slowly to full extension. The lying position prevents cheating.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
