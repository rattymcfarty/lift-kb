# Seal Row

An intermediate barbell pull-pattern exercise for the lats. Seal Row strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Upper Back, Biceps

## Instructions

1. Lie face-down on an elevated bench with a barbell hanging below you.
2. Row the bar to your lower chest, squeezing the shoulder blades together.
3. The chest-supported position removes all momentum.
4. Lower to full arm extension. Strict, heavy rows for the mid-back.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
