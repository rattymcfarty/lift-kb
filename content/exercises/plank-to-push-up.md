# Plank to Push-Up

Plank to Push-Up is an intermediate core exercise targeting the abs, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Chest

## Instructions

1. Start in a forearm plank with a straight body line.
2. Place one hand on the floor, then the other, pressing up to a push-up position.
3. Lower back down to your forearms, one arm at a time.
4. Alternate the leading arm each rep. Keep the hips level — no rocking.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
