# Stiff-Leg Deadlift

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Lower Back

## Overview

Stiff-Leg Deadlift is an intermediate hip-hinge exercise targeting the hamstrings, performed with a barbell. The hinge is the foundation of safe lifting — it teaches you to load the hips, not the spine.

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet hip-width apart, holding a barbell at thigh level.
2. With a slight knee bend, hinge at the hips and lower the bar along your legs.
3. Descend until you feel a deep hamstring stretch, back flat.
4. Drive the hips forward to stand, squeezing the glutes. Distinct from Romanian — knees stay nearly straight.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
