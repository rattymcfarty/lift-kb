# Single-Leg Calf Raise (Weighted)

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Calves

## Overview

Single-Leg Calf Raise (Weighted) is an intermediate core exercise targeting the calves, performed with dumbbells. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. Hold a dumbbell in one hand, standing on one foot at a step's edge.
2. Rise as high as possible on the ball of your foot.
3. Pause at the top for a full second.
4. Lower slowly past parallel for a deep stretch. Unilateral calf strength.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
