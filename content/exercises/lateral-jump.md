# Lateral Jump

A side-to-side plyometric jump that trains lateral power and landing control.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Calves

## Instructions

1. Stand on one foot next to a line or marker with a slight knee bend.
2. Jump sideways off that foot to the other side.
3. Land softly on the opposite foot and stick the landing.
4. Immediately jump back to the other side.
5. Keep your hips and knees aligned.

## Cues

- Land soft and stick
- Knee over toes
- Quick but controlled

## Common mistakes

- Letting the knee cave in
- Landing stiff
- Rushing

## Safety

Skip with knee or ankle instability; use a firm surface.
