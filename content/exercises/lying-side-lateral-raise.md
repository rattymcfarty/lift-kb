# Lying Side Lateral Raise

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Middle Delts, Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Rear Delts

## Overview

A side-lying lateral raise that isolates the side delt through a different angle of resistance.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your side on a bench or the floor, holding a light dumbbell in your top hand at your side.
2. Keep your arm slightly bent.
3. Raise the dumbbell out and up until your arm is above shoulder level.
4. Pause.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Light weight
- Slow and controlled
- Elbow slightly bent

## Common mistakes

- Using momentum
- Rolling the torso
- Using too heavy a weight

## Safety

Use a very light weight; stop with shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
