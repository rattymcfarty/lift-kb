# Pull-Up (Neutral Grip, Bodyweight)

A pull-up with palms facing each other, which is often easier on the shoulders and elbows.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Instructions

1. Hang from neutral-grip handles with arms straight.
2. Pull your shoulder blades down.
3. Pull your chest toward the handles.
4. Pause at the top.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Chest up
- Elbows down
- Control the lowering

## Common mistakes

- Swinging
- Half reps
- Shrugging

## Safety

Use assistance if needed.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
