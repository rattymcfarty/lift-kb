# Turkish Get Up

A multi-step movement from lying to standing with a weight overhead that trains stability and mobility.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** lunge
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Full-Body

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back holding a kettlebell overhead in one hand with the knee on that side bent.
2. Roll onto your elbow, then your hand.
3. Bridge your hips and sweep your leg back into a half-kneeling position.
4. Stand up, keeping the weight overhead.
5. Reverse the steps to return to the floor.

## Cues

- Eyes on the weight
- Move slowly
- Arm vertical

## Common mistakes

- Rushing
- Losing the arm lockout
- Using too heavy a weight

## Safety

Practise with no weight or a shoe on the fist first.

## Longevity notes

Single-leg strength and balance are what keep you steady on uneven ground and stairs. Lunge work trains the stability that prevents the falls that change lives.
