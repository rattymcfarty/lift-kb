# Lying Reverse Fly

A rear-delt fly performed lying on a bench that isolates the upper back.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts, Upper Back

## Instructions

1. Lie chest-down on an incline bench holding dumbbells with arms hanging.
2. Keep a slight bend in your elbows.
3. Raise your arms out to the sides until they are in line with your torso.
4. Squeeze your shoulder blades together.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Lead with the elbows
- Squeeze shoulder blades
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging
- Using momentum
- Lifting too heavy

## Safety

Use light weights; stop with shoulder pinching.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
