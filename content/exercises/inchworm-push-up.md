# Inchworm + Push-Up

Inchworm + Push-Up is a beginner core exercise targeting the abs, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest, Hamstrings

## Instructions

1. From standing, fold forward and walk your hands out to a plank.
2. Perform one push-up at full extension.
3. Walk the hands back to your feet and stand up.
4. Repeat. A full-body warm-up combining stretch, core, and pressing.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
