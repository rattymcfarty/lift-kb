# Hollow Hold

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A beginner just your bodyweight core exercise for the abs. Hollow Hold builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back, arms extended overhead, legs straight.
2. Press your lower back firmly into the floor — no gap.
3. Lift shoulders and legs slightly off the ground into a shallow banana shape.
4. Keep ribs down and hold 15-30 seconds; bend the knees if the back starts to arch.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
