# Chest Expander Pull-Apart (Vertical)

A chest-expander pull-apart performed overhead to train the rear delts, upper back and shoulder external rotators.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Lats

## Instructions

1. Hold the chest expander overhead or at forehead height with arms mostly straight.
2. Pull the handles apart, moving your hands out and back.
3. Squeeze your shoulder blades together and keep your ribs down.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Return under control.

## Cues

- Ribs down
- Pinch the shoulder blades
- Don't shrug

## Common mistakes

- Arching the back
- Shrugging
- Dropping the handles quickly

## Safety

Skip overhead positions that cause shoulder pain; use a lighter resistance.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
