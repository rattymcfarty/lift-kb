# Plank Walkout

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Overview

Plank Walkout is a beginner core exercise targeting the abs, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet hip-width apart.
2. Hinge forward and place your hands on the floor.
3. Walk your hands out to a full plank, keeping the core braced.
4. Walk the hands back to your feet and stand up. A dynamic core and hamstring mover.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
