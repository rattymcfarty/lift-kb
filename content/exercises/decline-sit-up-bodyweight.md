# Decline Sit Up (Bodyweight)

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A sit-up on a decline bench that trains the abs and hip flexors through a full range.

## Instructions

1. Hook your feet under the pads of a decline bench and lie back with arms crossed on your chest.
2. Brace your core.
3. Curl up and sit all the way up, bringing your chest toward your thighs.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Lower slowly back to the start.

## Cues

- Curl up
- Slow lowering
- Breathe out on the way up

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the head forward
- Using momentum
- Arching the back on the way down

## Safety

Reduce the decline if it strains your lower back.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
