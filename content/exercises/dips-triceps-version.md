# Dips - Triceps Version

A beginner bodyweight press-pattern exercise targeting the triceps. Dips - Triceps Version builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Instructions

1. To get into the starting position, hold your body at arm's length with your arms nearly locked above the bars.
2. Now, inhale and slowly lower yourself downward. Your torso should remain upright and your elbows should stay close to your body. This helps to better focus on tricep involvement. Lower yourself until there is a 90 degree angle formed between the upper arm and forearm.
3. Then, exhale and push your torso back up using your triceps to bring your body back to the starting position.
4. Repeat the movement for the prescribed amount of repetitions.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
