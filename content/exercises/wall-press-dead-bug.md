# Wall Press Dead Bug

A beginner just your bodyweight core exercise for the abs. Wall Press Dead Bug builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back with your head near a wall, knees bent 90 degrees over hips.
2. Press your hands into the wall overhead to fire the core.
3. Flatten your lower back into the floor.
4. Slowly extend one leg toward the floor without letting the back arch. Alternate legs, keeping wall pressure.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
