# Single Arm Pushdown

A single-arm cable pushdown that isolates one triceps at a time.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps

## Instructions

1. Attach a single handle to a high pulley.
2. Hold it with one hand and pin your elbow at your side.
3. Press down until your arm is straight.
4. Squeeze your triceps.
5. Return slowly.

## Cues

- Elbow pinned
- Squeeze at the bottom
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Leaning into it
- Flaring the elbow
- Using momentum

## Safety

Use a moderate load.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
