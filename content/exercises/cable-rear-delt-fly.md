# Cable Rear Delt Fly

Cable Rear Delt Fly is a beginner pulling exercise for the rear delts, performed with a cable machine. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts

## Instructions

1. Adjust the pulleys to the appropriate height and adjust the weight. The pulleys should be above your head.
2. Grab the left pulley with your right hand and the right pulley with your left hand, crossing them in front of you. This will be your starting position.
3. Initiate the movement by moving your arms back and outward, keeping your arms straight as you execute the movement.
4. Pause at the end of the motion before returning the handles to the start position.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
