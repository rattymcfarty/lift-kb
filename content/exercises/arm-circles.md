# Arm Circles

Arm Circles is a beginner mobility exercise for the front delts, performed with minimal equipment. Regular mobility work keeps joints moving freely and makes strength training safer.

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Upper Back

## Instructions

1. Stand up and extend your arms straight out by the sides. The arms should be parallel to the floor and perpendicular (90-degree angle) to your torso. This will be your starting position.
2. Slowly start to make circles of about 1 foot in diameter with each outstretched arm. Breathe normally as you perform the movement.
3. Continue the circular motion of the outstretched arms for about ten seconds. Then reverse the movement, going the opposite direction.

## Cues

- Move slowly, no forcing
- Breathe into the stretch
- Stay relaxed outside the target area

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
