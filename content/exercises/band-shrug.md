# Shrug (Band)

Shrug (Band) is a beginner pulling exercise for the upper back, performed with minimal equipment. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Forearms

## Instructions

1. Stand on a band, holding the ends at your sides.
2. Shrug your shoulders straight up toward your ears.
3. Squeeze hard at the top for a full second.
4. Lower slowly. The band keeps tension on the traps throughout.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
