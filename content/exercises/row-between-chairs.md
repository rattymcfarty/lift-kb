# Row Between Chairs

A bodyweight inverted row using two chairs and a broom or sturdy bar.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Instructions

1. Place two chairs a bit apart and lay a sturdy bar or broomstick across them.
2. Lie underneath and grip the bar.
3. Keep your body straight and pull your chest to the bar.
4. Squeeze your shoulder blades.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Straight body
- Pull to the chest
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag
- Using an unstable bar
- Shrugging

## Safety

Make sure the setup is stable before pulling.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
