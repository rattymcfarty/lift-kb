# Bicycle Crunch

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A beginner just your bodyweight core exercise for the abs. Bicycle Crunch builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back with hands behind your head and legs lifted, knees bent 90 degrees.
2. Bring your right elbow toward your left knee while extending the right leg straight.
3. Switch sides in a pedaling motion — left elbow to right knee.
4. Keep the lower back pressed into the floor and move with control, not speed.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
