# Push Up (Weighted)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Overview

A push-up with added load for progressing chest and triceps strength.

## Instructions

1. Place a plate on your upper back or wear a weighted vest.
2. Start in a high plank with hands under shoulders.
3. Lower your chest toward the floor while keeping your body straight.
4. Press back up to straight arms.
5. Keep your core braced.

## Cues

- Straight body
- Elbows at 45 degrees
- Full range

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag
- Flaring the elbows
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Use a weighted vest or a spotter for the plate; stop with wrist or shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
