# Incline Fly Press (Dumbbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Overview

An incline dumbbell fly that transitions into a press to work the upper chest.

## Instructions

1. Lie on an incline bench holding dumbbells over your upper chest.
2. Open your arms in a fly until you feel a chest stretch.
3. As you come back, bend your elbows and press the dumbbells up and together.
4. Lock out with the dumbbells over your chest.
5. Lower under control.

## Cues

- Fly down, press up
- Chest tall
- Shoulder blades pinned

## Common mistakes

- Using too heavy dumbbells
- Dropping low
- Arching the lower back

## Safety

Use light weights; stop at the stretch.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
