# Squat to Press (Dumbbell)

Squat to Press (Dumbbell) is an intermediate lower-body exercise that builds the quadriceps, usually performed with dumbbells. It trains the deep knee-and-hip bend you use every time you sit, stand, or climb stairs.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts, Glutes

## Instructions

1. Hold dumbbells at shoulder height, feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Squat down until thighs are parallel.
3. Drive up explosively, pressing the dumbbells overhead as you stand.
4. Lower the weights to your shoulders. A thruster — full-body power and conditioning.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
