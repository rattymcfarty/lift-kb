# Scapular Pull-Up

A beginner minimal-equipment pull-pattern exercise for the upper back. Scapular Pull-Up strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Lats

## Instructions

1. Take a pronated grip on a pull-up bar.
2. From a hanging position, raise yourself a few inches without using your arms. Do this by depressing your shoulder girdle in a reverse shrugging motion.
3. Pause at the completion of the movement, and then slowly return to the starting position before performing more repetitions.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
