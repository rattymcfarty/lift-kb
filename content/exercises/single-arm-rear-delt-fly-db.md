# Single-Arm Rear Delt Fly (Dumbbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Upper Back

## Overview

A beginner dumbbell pull-pattern exercise for the rear delts. Single-Arm Rear Delt Fly (Dumbbell) strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Hinge forward with a flat back, holding a dumbbell in one hand, the other braced on your knee.
2. Raise the dumbbell out to the side with a slight elbow bend.
3. Lift until your arm is parallel to the floor, squeezing the rear delt.
4. Lower slowly. Unilateral work evens out side-to-side imbalances.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
