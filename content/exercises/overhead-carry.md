# Overhead Carry

## Details

- **Category:** Carry
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** carry
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs, Middle Delts

## Overview

An intermediate loaded carry with dumbbells for the front delts. Overhead Carry builds the grip, core, and postural strength used every time you haul groceries or luggage.

## Instructions

1. Press dumbbells overhead, locking the elbows with active, shrugged-up shoulders.
2. Brace your core hard — ribs down, no leaning to either side.
3. Walk slowly with short, controlled steps, weights stacked over your shoulders.
4. Set the weights down safely when the distance or time is complete.

## Cues

- Tall posture, ribs down
- Grip hard, breathe behind the brace
- Short, quick steps

## Longevity notes

Grip strength and loaded walking correlate strongly with longevity. Carries are simple, safe, and brutally effective — do them often.
