# Push-Up (Knees)

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Chest
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Front Delts

## Overview

Push-Up (Knees) is a beginner pushing exercise for the chest, performed with just your bodyweight. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Instructions

1. Kneel on the floor with hands under your shoulders, body in a straight line from knees to head.
2. Lower your chest toward the floor, keeping elbows at 45 degrees.
3. Descend until your chest nearly touches.
4. Press back up. The knee position reduces the load — a perfect push-up progression.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
