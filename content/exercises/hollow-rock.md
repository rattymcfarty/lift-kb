# Hollow Rock

An intermediate just your bodyweight core exercise for the abs. Hollow Rock builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Start in a solid hollow hold — lower back pressed down, shoulders and legs lifted.
2. Initiate a gentle rocking motion from the shoulders, not by kicking the legs.
3. Rock forward and back smoothly, maintaining the hollow shape throughout.
4. Keep the rhythm controlled and continue 15-30 seconds.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
