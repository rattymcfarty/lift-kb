# Kettlebell Gorilla Row

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Upper Back, Biceps

## Overview

An intermediate kettlebell pull-pattern exercise for the lats. Kettlebell Gorilla Row strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Instructions

1. Hinge over two kettlebells on the floor, gripping both.
2. Row one kettlebell to your hip while the other stays grounded.
3. Alternate sides without rotating the torso.
4. Keep the back flat and core braced. An anti-rotation row variation.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
