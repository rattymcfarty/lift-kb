# Wide Grip Pull-Up

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

A pull-up with a wide grip that emphasises the lats.

## Instructions

1. Hang from a bar with an overhand grip wider than your shoulders.
2. Pull your shoulder blades down.
3. Pull your chest to the bar.
4. Pause.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Chest to the bar
- Elbows down
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Kipping
- Half reps
- Shrugging

## Safety

Use assistance if needed; stop with shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
