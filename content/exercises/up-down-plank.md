# Up Down Plank

A plank transition between forearms and hands that trains shoulder stability and core control.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Start in a forearm plank.
2. Press up onto one hand then the other.
3. Lower back to your forearms one at a time.
4. Keep your hips level.
5. Alternate which arm leads.

## Cues

- Hips level
- Brace the core
- Alternate arms

## Common mistakes

- Rocking the hips
- Rushing
- Sagging

## Safety

Widen your feet for stability.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
