# Sumo Deadlift (Dumbbell)

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Quadriceps, Adductors

## Overview

A beginner dumbbell hinge-pattern exercise for the glutes. Sumo Deadlift (Dumbbell) builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet wide, toes out, holding a dumbbell between your legs.
2. Squat down gripping the dumbbell, back flat and chest up.
3. Drive through the heels, extending the hips to stand.
4. The wide stance shortens the range and emphasizes the glutes and quads.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
