# Walk

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

A simple walk that builds cardiovascular fitness and daily movement.

## Instructions

1. Stand tall and begin walking at a brisk but comfortable pace.
2. Swing your arms naturally.
3. Land with your foot under your body.
4. Maintain steady breathing.
5. Aim for a continuous 20 to 60 minutes.

## Cues

- Tall posture
- Relaxed arms
- Steady breathing

## Common mistakes

- Slouching
- Overstriding
- Walking too slowly

## Safety

Wear supportive shoes.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
