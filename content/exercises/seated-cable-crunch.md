# Seated Cable Crunch

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A seated cable crunch that trains the abs with resistance.

## Instructions

1. Sit on a bench or the floor facing a high cable holding the rope near your head.
2. Brace your core.
3. Curl your ribs toward your hips.
4. Squeeze at the bottom.
5. Return slowly.

## Cues

- Round the spine
- Ribs to hips
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Using the arms to pull
- Moving the hips
- Using too heavy a load

## Safety

Use a moderate load; avoid with acute back problems.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
