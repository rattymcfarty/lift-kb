# Bent-Over Twist

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

Bent-Over Twist is a beginner core exercise targeting the abs, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart and hinge forward at the hips to about 45 degrees, back flat.
2. Clasp your hands at your chest or extend your arms out to the sides.
3. Rotate your torso to one side, following the movement with your eyes.
4. Return to center, then rotate to the other side. Keep the hips square — the motion comes from the trunk.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
