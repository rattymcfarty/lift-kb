# Terminal Knee Extension

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps

## Overview

A beginner minimal-equipment squat-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Terminal Knee Extension develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Instructions

1. Anchor a light band behind your knee at knee height and face the anchor.
2. Step back to create tension, knee slightly bent.
3. Straighten the knee fully against the band, squeezing the quad hard.
4. Hold one second at lockout, then return slowly. Keep it light and controlled.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
