# Hanging Oblique Knee Raise

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

Hanging Oblique Knee Raise is an intermediate core exercise targeting the abs, performed with just your bodyweight. A strong trunk stabilizes every lift and protects the spine in daily life.

## Instructions

1. Hang from a pull-up bar with a firm grip.
2. Raise your knees up and to one side, rotating the pelvis.
3. Lower with control — don't swing.
4. Alternate sides. Targets the obliques with the lower abs.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
