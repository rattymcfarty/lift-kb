# Hip Thrust (Bodyweight)

A beginner bodyweight hinge-pattern exercise for the glutes. Hip Thrust (Bodyweight) builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings

## Instructions

1. Sit with your upper back against a bench, knees bent, feet flat.
2. Drive through your heels to lift your hips until they're in line with knees and shoulders.
3. Squeeze the glutes hard at the top for 1-2 seconds.
4. Lower with control. Add a barbell across the hips when bodyweight is easy.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
