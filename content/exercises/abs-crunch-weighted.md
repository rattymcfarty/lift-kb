# Abs Crunch (Weighted)

A loaded crunch for progressing ab strength once bodyweight crunches feel easy.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat, holding a plate or dumbbell against your upper chest.
2. Exhale and curl your ribs toward your pelvis, lifting your shoulder blades off the floor.
3. Pause briefly at the top with the abs fully shortened.
4. Lower with control until your shoulder blades touch the floor.
5. Keep the weight against your chest; do not hold it behind your head.

## Cues

- Hug the weight to your chest
- Ribs to hips
- Own the lowering

## Common mistakes

- Holding the weight behind the neck
- Rocking with momentum
- Using a load that pulls your chin forward

## Safety

Keep the load modest; the neck should stay relaxed. Stop if you feel neck or low-back pain.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
