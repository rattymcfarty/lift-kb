# Cable Crossover

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Overview

A cable chest fly that brings the hands together in front of the body, keeping tension on the chest throughout.

## Instructions

1. Set the pulleys high, grab a handle in each hand and step forward so the cables are behind you.
2. Stand with one foot slightly forward, torso leaning a little forward and a slight bend in your elbows.
3. Bring your hands together in an arc down and in front of your hips or chest.
4. Squeeze your chest for a second at the end of the movement.
5. Open your arms slowly back to the start, feeling a stretch across the chest.

## Cues

- Hug a big tree
- Elbows slightly bent
- Squeeze at the centre

## Common mistakes

- Using the arms like a press
- Letting the shoulders roll forward
- Dropping the weights quickly

## Safety

Use a load you can control through the stretch; stop if you feel pinching at the front of the shoulder.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
