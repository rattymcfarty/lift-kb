# Battling Ropes

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest, Forearms

## Overview

A beginner minimal-equipment press-pattern exercise targeting the front delts. Battling Ropes builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Instructions

1. For this exercise you will need a heavy rope anchored at its center 15-20 feet away. Standing in front of the rope, take an end in each hand with your arms extended at your side. This will be your starting position.
2. Initiate the movement by rapidly raising one arm to shoulder level as quickly as you can.
3. As you let that arm drop to the starting position, raise the opposite side.
4. Continue alternating your left and right arms, whipping the ropes up and down as fast as you can.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
