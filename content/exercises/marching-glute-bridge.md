# Marching Glute Bridge

A glute bridge with alternating leg lifts that challenges hip stability and the glutes.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Lower Back, Abductors

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat, arms at your sides.
2. Lift your hips into a bridge by squeezing your glutes.
3. Lift one foot off the floor, bringing the knee toward your chest, without letting your hips drop.
4. Place it back down and lift the other foot.
5. Continue alternating.

## Cues

- Hips level
- Squeeze the glutes
- Slow march

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips drop or rotate
- Overarching the back
- Rushing

## Safety

Keep the range comfortable; skip with acute back pain.

## Longevity notes

Hip-hinge strength protects the lower back in every lift, from groceries to grandchildren. Strong glutes and hamstrings are the engine of walking, climbing, and standing up.
