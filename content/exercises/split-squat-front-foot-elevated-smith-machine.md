# Split Squat Front Foot Elevated (Smith Machine)

A split squat with the front foot elevated on a Smith machine that increases range for the quads and glutes.

## Details

- **Category:** Lunge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** lunge
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Abs

## Instructions

1. Place a low plate under your front foot inside a Smith machine and rest the bar on your upper back.
2. Step the back foot behind you.
3. Lower your back knee toward the floor.
4. Keep your torso upright.
5. Drive up through the front foot.

## Cues

- Torso tall
- Front foot flat
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Front knee caving
- Leaning forward
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Set the safety stops.

## Longevity notes

Single-leg strength and balance are what keep you steady on uneven ground and stairs. Lunge work trains the stability that prevents the falls that change lives.
