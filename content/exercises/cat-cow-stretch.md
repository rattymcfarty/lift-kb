# Cat Cow Stretch

A gentle spinal mobility drill that alternates between rounding and arching the back on hands and knees.

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Instructions

1. Start on hands and knees with wrists under shoulders and knees under hips.
2. Inhale and let your belly sink as you lift your chest and tailbone (cow).
3. Exhale and round your spine toward the ceiling, tucking your chin and tailbone (cat).
4. Move slowly between the two positions, matching your breath.
5. Repeat for 8 to 10 slow cycles.

## Cues

- Move with your breath
- Slow and smooth
- Arch and round the whole spine

## Common mistakes

- Rushing the movement
- Only moving the lower back
- Forcing a big arch

## Safety

Move within a comfortable range; stop if you feel pinching or pain.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
