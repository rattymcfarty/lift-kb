# Landmine Push Press

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps, Quadriceps

## Overview

An intermediate barbell press-pattern exercise targeting the front delts. Landmine Push Press builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Instructions

1. Hold the free end of a landmine bar at shoulder height.
2. Dip at the knees, then drive up, using leg power to press the bar overhead.
3. Lock out with control at the top.
4. Lower to the shoulder. The leg drive builds explosive pressing power.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
