# Jump Rope

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

A skill-based cardio exercise using a rope to improve coordination and conditioning.

## Instructions

1. Hold the rope handles at hip height with elbows close to your body.
2. Swing the rope using your wrists, not your arms.
3. Jump just high enough to clear the rope.
4. Land softly on the balls of your feet.
5. Keep a steady rhythm.

## Cues

- Small jumps
- Turn with the wrists
- Soft landings

## Common mistakes

- Jumping too high
- Using the whole arm to turn the rope
- Landing flat-footed

## Safety

Use a firm surface; skip if you have Achilles or shin problems.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
