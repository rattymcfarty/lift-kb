# Squat (Paused)

An intermediate barbell squat-pattern exercise for the quadriceps. Squat (Paused) develops leg strength through a full range of motion, which carries straight into daily life.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Instructions

1. Unrack a barbell and descend into a full squat.
2. Pause for 2-3 seconds at the bottom — no bouncing, stay tight.
3. Drive explosively out of the hole.
4. The pause builds starting strength and positional awareness.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
