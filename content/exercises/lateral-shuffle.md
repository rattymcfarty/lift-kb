# Lateral Shuffle

A beginner just your bodyweight core exercise for the quadriceps. Lateral Shuffle builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Calves

## Instructions

1. Start in an athletic stance, knees bent, chest up.
2. Shuffle quickly to one side without crossing your feet.
3. Stay low and on the balls of your feet.
4. Shuffle back the other way. A classic agility and conditioning drill.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
