# Chin-Up (Assisted)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

A machine-assisted chin-up that lets you build up to bodyweight chin-ups with a counterweight.

## Instructions

1. Set the assist weight and kneel or stand on the platform, gripping the bar with palms facing you at shoulder width.
2. Start with your arms straight and your shoulders pulled slightly down.
3. Pull your chest toward the bar by driving your elbows down.
4. Pause with your chin above the bar.
5. Lower slowly until your arms are straight.

## Cues

- Chest to the bar
- Elbows down
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Using too much assistance and swinging
- Cutting the range short
- Shrugging the shoulders

## Safety

Lower the assistance gradually as you get stronger; avoid if you have elbow or shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
