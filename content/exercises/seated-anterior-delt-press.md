# Seated Anterior Delt Press

Seated Anterior Delt Press is a beginner pushing exercise for the front delts, performed with dumbbells. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Instructions

1. Sit upright holding dumbbells at shoulder height, palms forward.
2. Press the weights overhead to full extension.
3. Avoid shrugging the shoulders toward your ears.
4. Lower with control. A seated overhead press focused on the front delts.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
