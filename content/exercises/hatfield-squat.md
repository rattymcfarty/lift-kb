# Hatfield Squat

Hatfield Squat is an intermediate lower-body exercise that builds the quadriceps, usually performed with a safety squat bar. It trains the deep knee-and-hip bend you use every time you sit, stand, or climb stairs.

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Safety Bar
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings

## Instructions

1. Set a safety squat bar on your back inside a power rack, holding the uprights for balance.
2. Unrack and take a comfortable squat stance.
3. Descend under control, using the hands lightly for stability — the legs do the work.
4. Drive through the whole foot to stand. The hand support lets you overload the legs safely.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
