# Punching Bag

Conditioning work on a heavy bag that trains punching power and cardio.

## Details

- **Category:** Cardio
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** cardio
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Instructions

1. Stand in a stable stance facing the bag with hands wrapped or gloved.
2. Throw straight punches from your guard, rotating your hips and shoulders.
3. Alternate hands and keep your chin tucked.
4. Work in timed rounds of 1 to 3 minutes.
5. Rest and repeat.

## Cues

- Rotate hips and shoulders
- Chin tucked
- Return to guard

## Common mistakes

- Punching with just the arms
- Dropping the guard
- Wrist bent on impact

## Safety

Wear gloves and hand wraps; learn technique to protect your wrists.

## Longevity notes

Cardiovascular fitness is the single strongest predictor of a long, active life. Regular cardio work keeps the heart, lungs, and brain in shape for decades.
