# Knee Raise (Captain's Chair)

A knee raise in a captain's chair apparatus that trains the lower abs and hip flexors.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Instructions

1. Support yourself on the padded arm rests with your back against the pad.
2. Let your legs hang straight down.
3. Raise your knees toward your chest by curling your pelvis up.
4. Pause at the top.
5. Lower slowly without swinging.

## Cues

- Curl the pelvis
- No swinging
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Using momentum
- Letting the torso rock
- Dropping the legs

## Safety

Keep your back against the pad; stop if your shoulders or elbows hurt.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
