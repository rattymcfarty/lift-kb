# Meadow Row

An intermediate barbell pull-pattern exercise for the lats. Meadow Row strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Upper Back, Biceps

## Instructions

1. Wedge one end of a barbell into a landmine base or corner.
2. Stand perpendicular to the bar, hinging forward with a flat back.
3. Row the free end to your hip with one hand, elbow driving back.
4. Lower with control. Named for John Meadows — a thick-back builder.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
