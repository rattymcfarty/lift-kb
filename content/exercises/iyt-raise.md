# I-Y-T Raise

A beginner dumbbell pull-pattern exercise for the rear delts and middle delts. I-Y-T Raise strengthens the back of the upper body, which pays off in posture and shoulder health.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Rear Delts, Middle Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts, Upper Back

## Instructions

1. Hinge forward about 45 degrees holding very light dumbbells, arms hanging.
2. I: raise both arms straight overhead in line with your body, thumbs up.
3. Y: lower, then raise the arms in a Y shape. T: lower, then raise arms straight out to the sides.
4. Keep every rep slow and controlled — this is prehab, not a max lift.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
