# Single-Arm Lateral Raise (Cable)

A beginner cable press-pattern exercise targeting the middle delts. Single-Arm Lateral Raise (Cable) builds the upper-body pushing strength used in everything from getting off the floor to overhead reaching.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Middle Delts

## Instructions

1. Stand sideways to a low pulley, holding the handle with the outside hand.
2. Raise your arm out to the side to shoulder height, slight elbow bend.
3. Don't shrug — initiate with the side delt.
4. Lower slowly. The cable provides constant tension unlike dumbbells.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
