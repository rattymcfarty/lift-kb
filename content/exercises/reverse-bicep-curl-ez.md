# Reverse Bicep Curl (EZ Bar)

Reverse Bicep Curl (EZ Bar) is a beginner pulling exercise for the forearms, performed with a barbell. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Forearms
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Instructions

1. Hold an EZ bar with an overhand grip, arms extended.
2. Curl the bar toward your shoulders, keeping elbows still.
3. Squeeze the forearms and biceps at the top.
4. Lower slowly. The overhand grip emphasizes the brachioradialis.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
