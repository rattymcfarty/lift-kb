# Seated Front Raise

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts

## Overview

Seated Front Raise is a beginner pushing exercise for the front delts, performed with dumbbells. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Instructions

1. Sit on a bench holding dumbbells at your sides.
2. Raise one or both arms straight in front of you to shoulder height.
3. Keep a slight elbow bend and don't swing the torso.
4. Lower slowly. Isolates the front delts.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
