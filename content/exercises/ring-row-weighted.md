# Ring Row (Weighted)

A rowing exercise on gymnastic rings that builds the lats and biceps; the weight adds resistance.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Biceps

## Instructions

1. Hang from rings at about waist height with a weight vest or plate, feet on the floor and body straight.
2. Brace your core.
3. Pull your chest toward the rings, rotating your palms in.
4. Squeeze your shoulder blades.
5. Lower slowly.

## Cues

- Straight body
- Pull to the ribs
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag
- Shrugging
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Make sure the rings are secure; use a lighter weight.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
