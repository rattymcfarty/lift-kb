# Romanian Deadlift to Row

An intermediate dumbbell hinge-pattern exercise for the hamstrings. Romanian Deadlift to Row builds posterior-chain strength that protects your back in everyday lifting.

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Lats, Upper Back

## Instructions

1. Hold dumbbells at your sides and hinge at the hips until you feel the hamstrings load.
2. At the bottom of the hinge, row both dumbbells to your ribs.
3. Lower the weights back to the hang, then drive the hips forward to stand.
4. Each rep is one hinge plus one row — keep the back flat throughout.

## Cues

- Hips back, spine long
- Feel the hamstrings load
- Squeeze glutes to finish

## Longevity notes

A strong hinge is back insurance. Learn it light, groove it often, and add load slowly — the goal is a back that never complains about picking things up.
