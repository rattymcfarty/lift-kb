# Standing Dumbbell Press

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Front Delts
- **Secondary muscles:** Triceps

## Overview

Standing Dumbbell Press is a beginner pushing exercise for the front delts, performed with dumbbells. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Instructions

1. Standing with your feet shoulder width apart, take a dumbbell in each hand. Raise the dumbbells to head height, the elbows out and about 90 degrees. This will be your starting position.
2. Maintaining strict technique with no leg drive or leaning back, extend through the elbow to raise the weights together directly above your head.
3. Pause, and slowly return the weight to the starting position.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
