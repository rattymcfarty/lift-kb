# Seated Dip (Machine)

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest

## Overview

Seated Dip (Machine) is a beginner pushing exercise for the triceps, performed with a machine. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Instructions

1. Adjust the seat so the handles are at shoulder height.
2. Grip the handles and press down until your arms are straight.
3. Keep the elbows tracking back, not flaring.
4. Return slowly. The machine path makes this an accessible triceps builder.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
