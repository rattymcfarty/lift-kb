# 21s (EZ Bar)

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

A seven-seven-seven curl protocol: seven partial reps in the bottom half, seven in the top half, then seven full reps without rest. It builds biceps endurance and teaches control through the whole range.

## Instructions

1. Stand holding an EZ bar with an underhand grip at shoulder width, arms extended, elbows pinned to your sides.
2. Curl the bar from the bottom up to halfway (forearms parallel to the floor) for 7 reps, lowering fully each time.
3. Without resting, curl from halfway to the top and lower only to halfway for 7 reps.
4. Finish with 7 full-range reps, lowering all the way to straight arms each time.
5. Set the bar down once all 21 reps are complete; do not swing to finish.

## Cues

- Elbows stay glued to your ribs
- Control the lowering
- Stand tall, no leaning back

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the torso to finish the last reps
- Letting the elbows drift forward
- Using a load that forces cheating by the third block

## Safety

Use a light-to-moderate load; the volume is high for the elbow tendons. Stop if you feel sharp pain at the front of the elbow.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
