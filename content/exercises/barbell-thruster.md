# Barbell Thruster

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts, Glutes

## Overview

Barbell Thruster is an intermediate lower-body exercise that builds the quadriceps, usually performed with a barbell. It trains the deep knee-and-hip bend you use every time you sit, stand, or climb stairs.

## Instructions

1. Clean a barbell to the front-rack position, elbows high.
2. Squat to full depth.
3. Drive up explosively, using the leg drive to help press the bar overhead.
4. Lock out over your mid-foot, lower to the rack, and flow straight into the next squat.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
