# Cable Crunch

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Abs

## Overview

A kneeling cable crunch that loads the abs through spinal flexion with adjustable resistance.

## Instructions

1. Attach a rope to a high pulley and kneel facing the machine, holding the rope beside your head.
2. Keep your hips still and curl your ribs toward your pelvis, bringing your elbows toward your thighs.
3. Squeeze your abs hard at the bottom.
4. Slowly return to the start without letting your hips move.
5. Keep the movement in the spine, not the hips.

## Cues

- Round the spine, not the hips
- Elbows to thighs
- Slow return

## Common mistakes

- Pulling mostly with the arms
- Sitting back onto the heels
- Using too heavy a load and swinging

## Safety

Keep the load moderate so you control the movement; avoid with acute back injuries.

## Longevity notes

A strong, stable core protects the spine in every other movement and makes daily tasks — bending, twisting, reaching — safer. Core endurance matters more than core power.
