# Neck Flexion

An isometric or light-resisted neck flexion that builds neck endurance.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Neck

## Instructions

1. Sit tall and place your palm on your forehead.
2. Gently push your head forward into your hand while resisting with your hand.
3. Hold for 5 to 10 seconds.
4. Relax slowly.
5. Repeat for several reps.

## Cues

- Gentle pressure
- Relaxed shoulders
- Breathe normally

## Common mistakes

- Pushing too hard
- Holding your breath
- Shrugging

## Safety

Keep the effort light to moderate; stop with dizziness or pain.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
