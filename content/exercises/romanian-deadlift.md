# Romanian Deadlift

## Details

- **Category:** Hinge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** hinge
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell, Barbell, Kettlebell
- **Primary muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Lower Back, Lats, Forearms
- **Aliases:** RDL

## Instructions

1. Stand tall holding the weight in front of your thighs, feet hip-width apart, knees softly bent.
2. Brace your core and push your hips straight back, letting the weight slide down the fronts of your thighs.
3. Keep your back flat and your chest proud as you lower until you feel a deep stretch in your hamstrings, usually just below the knees.
4. Drive your hips forward and squeeze your glutes to return to standing. Do not hyperextend your lower back at the top.

## Cues

- Push your hips straight back, like closing a car door behind you
- Weight slides down your thighs, close to your body
- Feel a deep stretch in the hamstrings, then squeeze the glutes to stand

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the lower back to reach further down
- Bending the knees into a squat instead of hinging at the hips
- Letting the weight drift away from the legs

## Safety

If you have an acute disc issue or radiating leg pain, skip loaded hinging until cleared by your clinician. Stop the set the moment your lower back starts to round — depth is earned, not forced.

## Longevity notes

The hip hinge is the movement you use every time you pick something off the floor — groceries, grandchildren, a dropped pen. Training it keeps the posterior chain (glutes, hamstrings, spinal erectors) strong and teaches a spine-safe lifting pattern that protects your back for decades. Load it progressively but never maximally; a crisp, controlled hinge at RPE 7 beats a sloppy max that costs you a disc.

## Progression

- **Loading guidance:** Add 2-5 kg when you can complete 3 sets of 10 with a flat back and full hamstring stretch at RPE 7 or less.
- **Progress to:** Barbell Romanian deadlift from a deficit
- **Regress to:** Dowel hip-hinge drill (no weight, dowel along the spine)
