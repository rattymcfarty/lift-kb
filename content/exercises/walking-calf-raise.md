# Walking Calf Raise

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Other

## Overview

A calf raise performed as you walk forward.

## Instructions

1. Stand tall.
2. Rise onto your toes.
3. Walk forward on your toes keeping your heels high.
4. Take small steps.
5. Lower your heels at the end.

## Cues

- Stay high on the toes
- Small steps
- Tall posture

## Common mistakes

- Dropping the heels
- Leaning forward
- Rushing

## Safety

Hold a support if needed.

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
