# Sumo Squat

## Details

- **Category:** Squat
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** squat
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Adductors, Glutes

## Overview

Sumo Squat is a beginner lower-body exercise that builds the quadriceps, usually performed with just your bodyweight. It trains the deep knee-and-hip bend you use every time you sit, stand, or climb stairs.

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet wide, toes turned out 45 degrees.
2. Squat down keeping the knees tracking over the toes and chest up.
3. Descend until thighs are parallel.
4. Drive through the heels to stand. The wide stance hits the adductors hard.

## Cues

- Knees track over toes
- Chest tall, brace the core
- Drive through the whole foot

## Longevity notes

Keep the squat pattern for life: depth and control matter more than load. Train it pain-free, leave reps in reserve, and your knees and hips will thank you at 80.
