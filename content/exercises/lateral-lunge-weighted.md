# Lateral Lunge (Weighted)

## Details

- **Category:** Lunge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** lunge
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Adductors, Hamstrings

## Overview

A side lunge with added load that trains the quads, glutes and adductors in the frontal plane.

## Instructions

1. Stand holding a dumbbell at your chest or a kettlebell in goblet position.
2. Take a wide step to one side.
3. Sit back into the hip of the stepping leg while keeping the other leg straight.
4. Keep your chest tall and your foot flat.
5. Push through the stepping foot to return to standing.

## Cues

- Sit back into the hip
- Chest tall
- Whole foot flat

## Common mistakes

- Letting the knee cave in
- Lifting the heel
- Leaning forward

## Safety

Use a range you can control; avoid with groin pain.

## Longevity notes

Single-leg strength and balance are what keep you steady on uneven ground and stairs. Lunge work trains the stability that prevents the falls that change lives.
