# Chest Press (Machine)

A seated machine chest press that trains the chest, shoulders and triceps on a guided path.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts

## Instructions

1. Adjust the seat so the handles are at mid-chest height and sit with your back against the pad.
2. Grip the handles and retract your shoulder blades.
3. Press the handles forward until your arms are almost fully extended.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Return slowly until you feel a stretch in the chest without letting the weight stack touch down.

## Cues

- Shoulder blades pinned
- Press straight out
- Control the return

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging the shoulders forward
- Locking the elbows hard
- Letting the weights slam

## Safety

Set the seat height correctly; avoid pressing through shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
