# Bent Over Row (Barbell, Paused)

A barbell bent-over row with a pause at the top that removes momentum and builds upper-back strength.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Pull
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Upper Back, Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Instructions

1. Stand with feet hip-width apart holding a barbell, hinge at the hips until your torso is roughly 45 degrees and your back is flat.
2. Brace your core and pull the bar toward your lower ribs.
3. Pause for one to two seconds with the bar touching your torso, squeezing your shoulder blades.
4. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are straight.
5. Keep your torso position fixed throughout.

## Cues

- Pull to the lower ribs
- Hold the pause
- Flat back the entire set

## Common mistakes

- Standing up as the weight gets heavy
- Rounding the lower back
- Shrugging the shoulders

## Safety

Use a lighter load than a normal row because of the pause; stop if your back rounds.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
