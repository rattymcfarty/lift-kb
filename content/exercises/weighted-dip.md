# Weighted Dip

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Push
- **Difficulty:** advanced
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Triceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Chest, Front Delts

## Overview

Weighted Dip is an advanced pushing exercise for the triceps, performed with just your bodyweight. Pressing strength keeps daily pushing tasks — and your shoulders — working well for decades.

## Instructions

1. Attach weight via a dip belt or hold a dumbbell between your feet.
2. Grip the parallel bars and lift yourself to straight arms.
3. Lower with control until your shoulders drop below your elbows.
4. Press explosively back up. Lean slightly forward to emphasize the chest.

## Cues

- Ribs down, glutes tight
- Control the lowering
- Press without flaring the elbows

## Longevity notes

Pressing keeps shoulders useful, but joint-friendly volume beats grinding maxes. Full range, controlled tempo, and balanced pulling keep the shoulders healthy for decades.
