# Lying Chest Pass Med Ball Throw

A lying explosive chest pass that trains upper-body power with a medicine ball.

## Details

- **Category:** Horizontal Push
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** push
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Chest, Triceps

## Instructions

1. Lie on your back with knees bent, holding a medicine ball at your chest.
2. Explosively press the ball straight up.
3. Catch the ball with soft elbows.
4. Immediately bring it back to your chest and repeat.
5. Keep your lower back flat.

## Cues

- Explode up
- Catch softly
- Lower back flat

## Common mistakes

- Arching the back
- Catching with locked elbows
- Throwing off-centre

## Safety

Use a soft medicine ball; clear the space above you.

## Longevity notes

Pressing strength keeps daily pushing — doors, shelves, getting up from the floor — easy and safe. Controlled pressing builds the shoulders, chest, and arms that protect you in a fall.
