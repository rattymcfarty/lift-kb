# Lateral Medicine Ball Slam

An intermediate minimal equipment core exercise for the abs. Lateral Medicine Ball Slam builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Other
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Full-Body

## Instructions

1. Stand sideways to a wall holding a medicine ball at chest height.
2. Rotate away from the wall, then explosively rotate and throw the ball into the wall.
3. Catch the rebound and immediately reload.
4. Alternate sides each set. Builds rotational power for sports.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
