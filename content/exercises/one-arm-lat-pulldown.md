# One Arm Lat Pulldown

One Arm Lat Pulldown is a beginner pulling exercise for the lats, performed with a cable machine. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Primary muscles:** Lats
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps, Upper Back

## Instructions

1. Select an appropriate weight and adjust the knee pad to help keep you down. Grasp the handle with a pronated grip. This will be your starting position.
2. Pull the handle down, squeezing your elbow to your side as you flex the elbow.
3. Pause at the bottom of the motion, and then slowly return the handle to the starting position.
4. For multiple repetitions, avoid completely returning the weight to keep tension on the muscles being worked.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
