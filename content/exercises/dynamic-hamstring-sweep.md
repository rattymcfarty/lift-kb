# Dynamic Hamstring Sweep

A beginner just your bodyweight mobility drill targeting the hamstrings. Dynamic Hamstring Sweep restores range of motion that desk life and hard training both tend to steal.

## Details

- **Category:** Mobility
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** mobility
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes

## Instructions

1. Stand tall, step forward and extend one leg with the heel down and toes up.
2. Hinge at the hips, sweeping your hands down the extended leg toward the foot.
3. Keep the back flat — fold from the hips, not the waist.
4. Return upright and alternate legs as you walk forward.

## Cues

- Move slowly, no forcing
- Breathe into the stretch
- Stay relaxed outside the target area

## Longevity notes

Mobility is use-it-or-lose-it. Ten minutes of daily joint work preserves the ranges that keep you independent — reaching, bending, and getting up from the floor.
