# Incline Hammer Curls

## Details

- **Category:** Other
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

Incline Hammer Curls is a beginner pulling exercise for the biceps, performed with dumbbells. Pulling balances all the pressing we do in life and keeps the upper back and posture strong.

## Instructions

1. Seat yourself on an incline bench with a dumbbell in each hand. You should pressed firmly against he back with your feet together. Allow the dumbbells to hang straight down at your side, holding them with a neutral grip. This will be your starting position.
2. Initiate the movement by flexing at the elbow, attempting to keep the upper arm stationary.
3. Continue to the top of the movement and pause, then slowly return to the start position.

## Cues

- Chest up, shoulders down
- Pull the elbows to the ribs
- Squeeze at the top

## Longevity notes

Pull twice as much as you push and your posture will age well. Rowing and pull-up strength are among the best predictors of a capable, pain-free upper body later in life.
