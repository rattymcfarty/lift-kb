# Zercher Front-Foot Elevated Split Squat

## Details

- **Category:** Lunge
- **Difficulty:** intermediate
- **Movement pattern:** lunge
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes
- **Secondary muscles:** Hamstrings, Abs

## Overview

A split squat holding a barbell in the crook of your elbows with the front foot elevated.

## Instructions

1. Hold a barbell in the crooks of your elbows against your chest.
2. Place your front foot on a low plate and step the other foot back.
3. Lower your back knee.
4. Keep your torso upright.
5. Drive up through the front foot.

## Cues

- Torso tall
- Front foot flat
- Slow lowering

## Common mistakes

- Leaning forward
- Front knee caving
- Using too much weight

## Safety

Pad the bar; use light loads and safeties.

## Longevity notes

Single-leg strength and balance are what keep you steady on uneven ground and stairs. Lunge work trains the stability that prevents the falls that change lives.
