# Walkout Plank

A beginner just your bodyweight core exercise for the abs. Walkout Plank builds the trunk stability that underpins safe movement as you age.

## Details

- **Category:** Core
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** core
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Abs
- **Secondary muscles:** Front Delts, Hamstrings

## Instructions

1. From standing, fold forward and walk your hands out to a plank.
2. Hold the plank for 2-3 seconds with a tight, straight body.
3. Walk the hands back in and stand up.
4. Repeat for reps. Combines a hamstring stretch with core work.

## Cues

- Breathe behind the brace
- No sagging or hiking
- Slow and controlled

## Longevity notes

A stable trunk protects the spine through every decade. Train the core for endurance and control rather than chasing burn.
