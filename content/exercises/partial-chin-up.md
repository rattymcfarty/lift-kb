# Partial Chin-Up

## Details

- **Category:** Vertical Pull
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** pull
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **Primary muscles:** Lats, Upper Back
- **Secondary muscles:** Biceps

## Overview

A partial-range chin-up that builds strength in the strongest portion of the movement toward full chin-ups.

## Instructions

1. Hang from a bar with an underhand grip at shoulder width.
2. Pull yourself up about halfway, until your elbows are at 90 degrees.
3. Pause briefly.
4. Lower under control to a hang.
5. Repeat for reps.

## Cues

- Shoulders down
- Slow lowering
- No swinging

## Common mistakes

- Swinging
- Dropping fast
- Shrugging

## Safety

Build to full range over time; stop with elbow or shoulder pain.

## Longevity notes

Pulling strength balances all the forward-reaching of daily life and protects the shoulders and upper back. A strong back is one of the best predictors of staying active and independent.
