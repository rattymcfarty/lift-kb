# Reverse Lunge

A beginner dumbbell lunge-pattern exercise targeting the quadriceps. Reverse Lunge develops single-leg strength and stability that transfers to walking, stairs, and sport.

## Details

- **Category:** Lunge
- **Difficulty:** beginner
- **Movement pattern:** lunge
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Primary muscles:** Quadriceps
- **Secondary muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings

## Instructions

1. Stand tall holding dumbbells at your sides (or bodyweight).
2. Step one foot straight back, lowering until both knees approach 90 degrees.
3. Keep the torso upright and front knee over the ankle.
4. Drive through the front heel to return. Alternate legs. Easier on the knees than forward lunges.

## Cues

- Torso tall over hips
- Front knee over ankle
- Push through the front heel

## Longevity notes

Single-leg strength is balance insurance — the ability to catch yourself on one leg prevents falls. Train lunges for control, not maximal load.
