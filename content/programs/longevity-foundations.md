# Longevity Foundations

## Goal

Build the fundamental movement patterns (squat, carry, hang) with joint-friendly volume over 4 weeks. Designed for beginners or returning lifters; every session caps at RPE 8.

## Details

- **Weeks:** 4
- **Days per week:** 3
- **Deload:** week 4 at 50% volume (×0.5)

## Weekly plan

### Week 1

| Day | Session | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | RPE cap | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Squat + Carry | [Goblet Squat](../exercises/goblet-squat.md) | 3 × 8-12 | 90 s | ≤ 8 | Week 1: start at the low end of the rep range. |
| 1 | Squat + Carry | [Farmer Carry](../exercises/farmer-carry.md) | 3 × 40 seconds | 90 s | ≤ 7 | Choose a weight you can carry with perfect posture. |
| 1 | Squat + Carry | [Dead Hang](../exercises/dead-hang.md) | 2 × max time | 60 s | ≤ 8 | Accumulate hang time; step down before grip fully fails. |
| 3 | Squat + Carry (volume) | [Goblet Squat](../exercises/goblet-squat.md) | 4 × 8-10 | 90 s | ≤ 8 | Add one set vs Day 1. Same weight unless all sets felt RPE 6 or less. |
| 3 | Squat + Carry (volume) | [Farmer Carry](../exercises/farmer-carry.md) | 2 × 60 seconds | 90 s | ≤ 7 | Longer carries, one fewer set. |
| 5 | Hang + Carry (light) | [Dead Hang](../exercises/dead-hang.md) | 3 × max time | 60 s | ≤ 7 | Easy day: stop each set with 1-2 seconds in reserve. |
| 5 | Hang + Carry (light) | [Farmer Carry](../exercises/farmer-carry.md) | 2 × 30 seconds | 60 s | ≤ 6 | Light recovery carries. |
